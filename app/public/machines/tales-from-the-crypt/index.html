Tales from the Crypt

Data East, 1993 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Tales from the Crypt in the Pinball Genie app →

Designed by John Borg, released Nov/Dec 1993 (~4,500 units). Single production release — no Pro/Premium/LE editions exist; that convention postdates this era. The only meaningful variance is ROM revision (factory 2.x, 3.01, 3.03, plus an unofficial fan rebalance "v4.00" many home/route operators run) which changes scoring values, not the physical layout.

Layout basics

Three flippers: standard lower-left and lower-right, plus a 3rd upper-right flipper that feeds and catches returns from the Electric Chair scoop and center spinner — the two most-repeated shots in the game. Two ramps (Left Ramp, Right Ramp) each cross the top of the playfield and drop into the opposite inlane. Two orbit spinners (left, right) loop into each other. A 3-target drop-target bank ("Guillotine") doubles as the skill-shot target. A large Tombstone standup blocks the Crypt eject hole until spelled open. Six eyeball standup targets (K-E-E / P-E-R) spell KEEPER. A captive ball sits lower-left. Three pop bumpers cluster top-center.

Skill shot

An auto-plunge sends the ball up and into the shooter lane where it drops on the Guillotine bank; a light travels across the 3 targets as the ball travels. Landing on the lit target pays 2M on ball 1, 3M on ball 2, +1M each ball after. The ball saver covers only the first two skill-shot attempts of the game.

CRYPT spelling and Crypt Multiball

Hitting the Tombstone spots C-R-Y-P-T letters in sequence. 'C' is auto-spotted at game start, and the game continues auto-spotting letters between balls so that a well-played ball 3 nearly guarantees CRYPT is complete. Once spelled, the Tombstone lowers, opening the Crypt VUK — shoot the Crypt (or, the first time only, the center spinner) to start Crypt Multiball. The designed money loop is: Left Ramp = Jackpot (base 25M, built by left-spinner spins and Crypt hits) → Right Ramp = Double Jackpot (2x the Jackpot, min 50M) → hit the Tombstone 5 times to respell CRYPT and light Monster Jackpot (50M + Double Jackpot value, min 100M, +50M every repeat) → loop back to the Left Ramp. Captive-ball hits add balls before the Double Jackpot is collected.

Creature Features (12 modes)

The center scoop ("Electric Chair") starts whichever Creature Feature (CF) is currently flashing on the playfield's door-mechanism display. Shooting either orbit spinner cycles which CF is flashing; the door-handle plunger button locks the current one in (press again to unlock) before it rotates to something worse. All 12 CFs — Thunder Storm, Lite Skull Cracking, Door Prize Select (the game's one video mode), Frightmare, Psycho Pops, Robbing the Crypt, Lite Extra Ball, Super Guillotine Targets, Lite Werewolf Countdown, Lite Super Crypt Kicker, Electric Chair, and Super Keeper Targets — can run concurrently with each other and even during multiball, and stacking two at once is the closest thing the game has to a genuine scored "combo" system. Two CFs are themselves multiballs: Electric Chair Multiball (instant 6-ball, scoop pays 1M x balls in play) and Skull Cracking (20-sec captive-ball add-a-ball, escalating per-hit value).

Multipliers

The Bonus Multiplier (2x→4x→6x→8x, then flat 1M/hit) is advanced by clearing the Guillotine bank or fully spelling KEEPER, and applies only to the end-of-ball bonus. The Right Orbit Loop value escalates on consecutive, uninterrupted right-spinner shots (roughly 0.5M→1M→1.5M→2M→2.5M, capping and repeating). There is no general playfield-wide "X" multiplier — the game's other escalating values (Fast Goring frenzy tiers, Super Guillotine/Keeper per-target growth, Crypt Multiball's balls-in-play scaling) are self-contained inside their own modes.

Fast Goring frenzies

Collecting 4 "bat" lights via the Left Ramp advances a 15-second Frenzy tier started via the Right Ramp: Living Dead → Chainsaw Mode → Grave Digger → Play the Organ, each raising the flat per-switch value (roughly 250K/500K/750K/1M). Best farmed with multiple balls in play during Crypt Multiball.

Wizard mode — Crypt Jam

Complete all 12 Creature Features at least once each to light the scoop for Crypt Jam, the game's single wizard mode: a 6-ball multiball with a 25-second ball saver where every switch hit scores a flat 1M while 2 or more balls remain in play. There is no deeper second-tier wizard mode.

Strategy notes

Chase multiball over grinding single modes — nearly every big number scales with balls in play. Once in multiball, spam the scoop shot off the upper-right flipper (it returns directly to that flipper, making it the most controllable repeat shot in the game). The left outlane is a live drain unless KEEPER is lit — hit any K-E-E target early to relight the kickback. Route center shots off the upper-right flipper rather than the lower flippers, since scoop mishits from below feed straight down the middle.

How Tales from the Crypt plays

You emcee the Cryptkeeper's ghoulish variety show: pin down twelve Creature Features one grisly bit at a time, and dig into Crypt Multiball for the fattest jackpots. Clear every feature to open Crypt Jam, the closing wizard romp.

The loop

  1. Batter the skull standup to spell CRYPT; once it sinks, the eject hole behind it opens and multiball is one shot away.
  2. Rotate the door clock with a spinner rip until a feature you like blinks, pin it with the cast door handle, then cash it at the center scoop.
  3. In Crypt Multiball, run the left ramp jackpot into the right ramp double, batter the letters back on, and empty the hole for the monster.
  4. Bank each finished feature toward the dozen you need, and keep the left kicker armed so a stray bounce does not cut the ball short.

Reading the lights

  • CRYPT on the Tombstone (letters) — lit: The big skull standup blocking the hole carries the C-R-Y-P-T spelling; a letter you have banged in stays spotted, and the game hands you leftovers between balls, so the word tends to finish itself by your final ball. · then: Bury the last letter and the stone drops, unblocking the eject hole. Dive in there -- or, the very first time only, the center spinner -- to spit out the extra balls and open the jackpot chain.
  • KEEPER Eyeball Targets (letters) — lit: The eyeball standups hugging each side rail spell KEEPER -- K-E-E on the left, P-E-R on the right; every stack you round out counts, and your first eyeball of the ball re-arms the left kicker that is otherwise a straight drain. · then: Finish the whole name to notch your end-of-ball bonus multiplier up a rung and relight that kicker once more. Only the Lite Super Crypt Kicker feature bolts the save on for keeps, so lean on these as your recurring insurance.
  • Creature Feature Clock (status) — lit: One Creature Feature blinks on the Cryptkeeper's clock at any moment, and the electric-chair scoop is where you actually collect the one you have settled on; a feature you finish gets checked off toward the full dozen for good. · flashing: The blinking feature is the one that will collect if you drive the scoop right now, and a spinner hit rolls the blink along to the next one on the clock. · then: Pin the feature you want, drive the scoop, and the ball fires up to the third flipper for your next move. Check off all twelve and this same scoop lights for Crypt Jam. · control: Action button: a press of the cast door handle clamps whichever feature is blinking so a later spinner cannot spin it off, and pressing again turns it loose to hunt for a better award (a beat after you launch, before you rip any spinner)
  • Guillotine Drop Targets (shot arrows) — lit: The skull-faced drop trio lights as your skill goal on the launch, and a roll through the left return arms it again for a door prize for a few seconds; drops you already knocked stay down until the bank kicks back up. · then: Time the handle-fired launch so the ball meets the lit drop for a skill award that grows every ball. Later, feed the left return and sweep the armed bank for a random one-to-three-million door prize, and clearing the whole trio also bumps your bonus multiplier a step.
  • Multiball Jackpot Ramps (shot arrows) — lit: With balls loose in Crypt Multiball the ramps and the hole light for the jackpot run; the left ramp pays the plain jackpot, the right ramp doubles whatever you just collected, and a freshly re-spelled stone lights the monster at the hole. · then: Take left ramp, then right ramp, batter the stone through its letters again, and empty the hole for the monster -- then keep looping it, since each trip stacks more onto the monster with no ceiling. Pad the base first with left-orbit spins and scoop hits before you ever start.
  • Left Outlane Kicker (lanes) — lit: The left outlane holds the only kicker on the whole machine, lit at the top of each ball and after any fresh eyeball hit; the right outlane has no such net, so the left side is the one you can actually defend. · then: Keep it armed with KEEPER work through the ball, and light the Lite Super Crypt Kicker feature to weld it on for the rest of the ball. A live kicker buys you the extra shots that reach multiball and stretch the closing jam.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Bank the dependable stuff first. Time the door handle so the launch drops the ball on a lit guillotine target, re-arm the left kicker off an eyeball, and pin one strong feature on the clock before a spinner rolls it away. — Guillotine Skill Shot, Protect the Outlanes, Bonus Multiplier
  • Building — Drive toward multiball, where almost every large number scales with balls in play. Spell the stone for Crypt Multiball, cash Electric Chair for an instant six-ball, and step the Fast Goring frenzy up a tier off the right ramp so every switch is worth more. — Crypt Multiball, Electric Chair Multiball, Fast Goring Frenzy Tiers
  • The big one — Clear all twelve features to light Crypt Jam, the closing wizard jam, and keep a second ball alive to keep its flat per-switch pay flowing. The uncapped left-ramp-to-monster loop inside Crypt Multiball is the other road to the game's very biggest totals. — Crypt Jam, Milking Crypt Jam, Crypt Multiball Jackpot Loop

The Cryptkeeper Door Handle on Tales from the Crypt

Front right of the cabinet, where a shooter rod would normally be.

There is no plunger to pull on this one. A cast door handle with the Cryptkeeper's face on it launches every ball, and it keeps earning its keep afterward: it is the lock switch for whichever Creature Feature is flashing on the clock, and it is the button that opens doors in the video mode.

  • Ball waiting in the shooter lane: Push the handle like you are opening a door and the ball auto-launches.
  • A mode you score well on is flashing: Press once to lock that Creature Feature in place so spinner hits cannot rotate it away; press again to unlock.
  • A second after launching each ball: Lock the flashing feature before you hit any spinner — Lite Extra Ball is often sitting there on later balls.
  • Inside Door Prize Select video mode: Flippers move between the three doors, then the handle opens the one you chose.
  • A bad feature is locked in: Press again to release it, then hit a spinner to rotate the clock to something better.

Goals in Tales from the Crypt

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Guillotine Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    Time the auto-plunge to land as a specific Guillotine drop target is lit for a bonus that grows every ball.

    Reward: 2M (ball 1), 3M (ball 2), +1M each ball after

  • Crypt Multiball Jackpot Loop combo · Hard

    The top money sequence in the game: Left Ramp Jackpot, Right Ramp Double Jackpot, respell CRYPT for Monster Jackpot, then loop.

    Reward: 25M+ Jackpot, then 50M+ Double Jackpot, then 100M+ Monster Jackpot (+50M every repeat, uncapped)

  • Scoop Return Farming combo · Medium

    The Electric Chair scoop kicks straight back to the upper-right flipper, making it the most repeatable high-value shot in the game during Electric Chair Multiball or Crypt Jam.

    Reward: 1M x balls in play during Electric Chair Multiball; flat 1M per switch during Crypt Jam

  • Right Orbit Loop Escalator combo · Medium

    Uninterrupted right-orbit shots climb an escalating value ladder before capping.

    Reward: 0.5M climbing to 2.5M per uninterrupted orbit loop, then caps and repeats at 2.5M

  • Fast Goring Frenzy Stack combo · Hard

    Start a Frenzy off the Right Ramp during multiball so every switch on the playfield counts at the elevated per-hit rate for every ball in play.

    Reward: 250K-1M per switch on an escalating Frenzy tier, counted for every ball in play

  • Right Ramp Trick Shot Fallback combo · Easy

    A partial Right Ramp shot that doesn't complete the loop still pays out by rolling onto the Left Ramp's return habitrail.

    Reward: Flat 5M Trick Shot bonus, even on a partial/missed loop

  • Crypt Multiball multiball · Hard

    The main multiball. Spell CRYPT on the Tombstone to qualify, then start it at the Crypt or center spinner.

    Reward: Starts the Left Ramp -> Right Ramp -> Tombstone loop: 25M+ Jackpot, 50M+ Double Jackpot, 100M+ Monster Jackpot (uncapped)

  • Electric Chair Multiball multiball · Medium

    Creature Feature #11 — an instant 6-ball multiball started at the center scoop.

    Reward: Instant 6-ball multiball; every scoop shot after pays 1M x balls in play

  • Skull Cracking Add-a-Ball multiball · Medium

    Creature Feature #2 — a 20-second captive-ball add-a-ball mode that starts automatically once lit.

    Reward: Adds a ball per captive-ball hit (up to 5-6 total); escalating 5M/10M/15M/20M+ per hit

  • Robbing the Crypt mode · Medium

    A loopable Creature Feature scored at the lowered Crypt/tombstone that also spots CRYPT letters.

    Reward: 10M + a spotted CRYPT letter per loopable Crypt hit; completing T scores 25M and ends the mode

  • Frightmare mode · Medium

    25 spinner hits inside 25 seconds pays 25M. One hard shot up the right orbit can register as many as 10 spins as it loops back through the left spinner, so this is one of the quickest Creature Features to cash.

    Reward: 25M total for landing 25 spinner hits within 25 seconds

  • Psycho Pops mode · Medium

    25 pop-bumper hits in 25 seconds for 25M, and easy to sustain because early hits reset the timer.

    Reward: 25M for 25 pop-bumper hits in 25 seconds; hits under 9 sec left reset the timer

  • Super Keeper Targets mode · Medium

    Hit the K-E-E-P-E-R eyeball target banks for escalating value with a large bonus for clearing all six.

    Reward: Each eyeball target starts at 2M and climbs 1M a hit to a 5M cap; clearing all six pays a 99M bonus

  • Bonus Multiplier multiplier · Easy

    Raise the end-of-ball Bonus Multiplier from 2x up to 8x by completing the Guillotine bank or spelling KEEPER.

    Reward: +1 step toward the 2x/4x/6x/8x end-of-ball Bonus Multiplier (flat 1M/hit once capped)

  • Fast Goring Frenzy Tiers multiplier · Medium

    Step up the Frenzy tier via repeated Right Ramp shots to raise the per-switch value from 250K toward 1M+.

    Reward: Raises the per-switch Frenzy value: Living Dead 250K -> Chainsaw 500K -> Grave Digger 750K -> Play the Organ 1M

  • Crypt Jam wizard · Hard

    The single wizard mode — complete all 12 Creature Features at least once each to light Crypt Jam at the scoop.

    Reward: 6-ball wizard multiball, 25-sec ball saver; flat 1M per switch while 2+ balls remain in play

  • Beginner First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    The recommended opening sequence on a fresh ball, from plunge to mode stacking.

    Reward: 2M-3M+ skill shot plus a collected Creature Feature, often stacked into a second concurrent CF within 30 seconds

  • Best Opener: Chase Multiball strategy · Medium

    Prioritize reaching Crypt Multiball over grinding single-ball modes — nearly every big number in the game scales with balls in play.

    Reward: Access to the Crypt Multiball Jackpot loop (25M+ -> 50M+ -> 100M+, uncapped) instead of grinding single-mode points

  • Protect the Outlanes strategy · Easy

    The left outlane is a live drain unless KEEPER is lit — manage it early instead of losing balls while chasing modes.

    Reward: A relit left-outlane kickback (saved balls) instead of a live drain, plus Bonus Multiplier progress from KEEPER

  • Door Prize Award mode · Easy

    Any ball through the left inlane lights the Guillotine bank for about five seconds; hit the targets inside that window for a random award of 1M to 3M. The door handle also picks doors when the game runs its door-select sequence.

    Reward: 1M-3M door prize off the Guillotine bank

  • Extra Ball Collect strategy · Medium

    Light the extra ball off a Creature Feature award, then cash it in at the center spinner before losing the ball.

    Reward: One extra ball

  • Milking Crypt Jam wizard · Hard

    Wizard-mode scoring only pays while two or more balls are alive, so protect the second ball and hammer the most repeatable switches.

    Reward: Flat 1M per switch hit for as long as 2+ balls stay in play

  • Door Prize Select Video Mode secret · Medium

    One of the Creature Features is a genuine mini-game where you pick the doors yourself instead of watching an award roll by. It is the one mode where the door handle you launch balls with becomes your action button — and pushing your luck one door too far ends the round on the spot.

    Reward: 5 million or 10 million per money door on older code, and 5, 10, 15 or 20 million on newer code, with a fresh set of three doors each time you clear one
