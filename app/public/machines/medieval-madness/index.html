Medieval Madness

Williams, 1997 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Medieval Madness in the Pinball Genie app →

Designer: Brian Eddy (rules), Lyman Sheats (software). The CGC 2015 remake and all its trims (Standard, Royal, Special/Limited, Merlin editions) are rules- and layout-identical to the 1997 original — cosmetic/hardware only. There is no Pro/Premium/LE split, so every shot is trim = all.

Objective

Destroy 6 castles — five random King's Men castles, then the King of Payne's castle — while completing five single-ball "Madness" modes to fuel multiball, ultimately qualifying the wizard mode Battle for the Kingdom.

The Five Madness Modes (single-ball qualifiers)

  • Joust Madness — 3 orbit shots (joust the orbits into each other).
  • Catapult Madness (Catapult Slam) — 3 catapult slams at the castle.
  • Peasant Madness (Revolting Peasants) — 4 left ramps (Rabble Rouser → Angry Mob → Ugly Riot → Peasant Revolt).
  • Damsel Madness (Save the Damsels) — 4 right (dragon) ramps.
  • Trolls! (Troll Madness) — ~8 troll standup hits light Trolls!, then kill each raised troll 3×.

Completing a Madness flashes its light at Merlin's saucer (arming Multiball Madness). Completing one three times (10 trolls for Trolls) lights its blue "title" dot toward the wizard mode.

Multiballs

  • Multiball Madness — light as many Madnesses as possible, then start at Merlin's saucer. Ball count scales: 1 lit = 2-ball, 2-4 = 3-ball, all 5 = 4-ball. Typical values: Jackpots ≈ 100K, Super Jackpots ≈ 200K scaling to ~1M; the Lock is a Double Super Jackpot (up to 2M). Additive alternate model: Jackpots 75K +25K × lit, Super Jackpots 250K +150K × lit. Fold in castle shots — they out-value jackpots.
  • Castle Multiball — lock 3 balls in the castle (6 for later ones); 3-stage ramp→Super Jackpot loop (~750K ramp jackpots → 5 light a ~1.5M Super → all shots Super → loop with +250K/+500K base).
  • Barnyard Multiball — fling all five distinct catapult items (bowling ball, cow, chicken, cat, skull) into the castle; 3-ball with every switch worth 75K.

Castle Destruction (core progression)

Each castle is a 3-step kill: moat hits lower the drawbridge (hits = castle number) → gate ~3 hits to open (≈200K × castle number each) → one shot into the castle interior destroys it (≈2M × castle number). The 3rd castle lights an Extra Ball; the King of Payne's final castle is worth the most (~1M/gate + ~20M destroy).

Castle Hurry-Up

Completing a Madness after the first Multiball Madness (or via Super Skill Shot / Merlin's Magic) lights the gate for a hurry-up starting ~1M, decaying to ~250K. Stack further completions to build it "millions at a time" (1M → 3M → 5.5M → ~8M+; players hold to >50M), then cash at the castle. 10 hurry-ups light an Extra Ball (40 for a second).

Merlin's Magic

Random award at Merlin's saucer (lights when the three Merlin standups are completed): Light/Award Lock, Start Castle Multiball, Light/Start Trolls!, 3 Troll Bombs, Smack-A-Troll, +5 / Hold Bonus X, Light Extra Ball, 250K/500K/1M, Start Hurry-Up, Start any Madness, Lower Drawbridge, Destroy Gate/Castle, Video Mode.

Combos & Multipliers

  • Troll Combo — ricochet off a raised troll into a scoring feature/saucer for a Troll Bomb (3 bombs off both trolls). Bombs (launch button) clear immortal trolls in the wizard mode.
  • Ultra Combo — post-wizard Victory Laps; a second quick major shot doubles the lap value (up to ~4M), the highest repeatable ceiling.
  • Bonus X — no lane ladder; built via Normal Skill Shots (+5 each) and Merlin's Magic. Multiplies the switch-accumulating end-of-ball bonus (players reach ~100×). Jets add bonus switches that this Bonus X multiplier then scales; there is no separate playfield/shot multiplier. No 2X playfield.

Wizard Modes

  • Royal Madness (mini-wizard) — complete all five Madnesses and play Multiball Madness, then shoot the right eject; hit all lit shots in a resetting 20s timer (250K/750K), all = Extra Ball.
  • Battle for the Kingdom (grand wizard) — light all six blue dots (Joust Champion, Catapult Ace, Patron of the Peasants, Defender of Damsels, Castle Crusher, Master of Trolls), then shoot the castle. 4-ball with ~15s save: Phase 1 six ~2.5M Battle Jackpots, Phase 2 ~6-7 gate shots (~5M) clearing immortal trolls with Troll Bombs, ~50M finish, then Victory Laps/Ultra Combos.

Strategy

Castles are the scoring engine — the 60-second castle loop (moat → gate → castle, repeat) carries scores; weave in ramps/orbits to light Madnesses for a big Multiball Madness, then dump castles during it. Right drains are more common than left; the catapult is the hardest, most drain-prone shot. Battle for the Kingdom only lights in single-ball play, so guard the ball when one goal away.

How Medieval Madness plays

Medieval Madness is a castle siege: drop each drawbridge, crack its gate, and storm the keep, over and over, while knocking out five separate kinds of Madness around it. Light all six title dots to reach Battle for the Kingdom, the grand wizard war that ends on the King of Payne's keep.

The loop

  1. Work the moat to drop the drawbridge, the gate to crack it open, then the keep itself to level a castle -- the richest loop on the machine.
  2. Farm the ramps: four left ramps light Peasant Madness, four right ramps complete Save the Damsels, and both stack a Castle Hurry-Up you cash at the keep.
  3. Joust the orbits into each other, slam the catapult, and battle the trolls to light the rest of the five Madness types.
  4. Light Madness modes in single-ball play, then start Multiball Madness at Merlin's saucer for more balls and bigger jackpots.
  5. Earn all six blue title dots -- the five Madness types plus razing every castle -- to open Battle for the Kingdom.

Reading the lights

  • Castle Siege (shot arrows) — lit: The castle runs in three lit stages -- the moat to lower its drawbridge, the gate to blow it open, then the mouth of the keep to destroy it -- and each next castle wants more hits than the one before. · then: Run moat to gate to keep to raze a castle, then move on to the next; the tally of fallen castles sticks all game, your second one lights an extra ball, and toppling all six is one of the wizard's title dots. The keep out-scores nearly every other shot, so keep this loop turning even inside a multiball.
  • Peasant Ramp (ladder) — lit: The left ramp is the safest repeatable shot, and four of them climb the peasant awards up to Peasant Revolt, lighting Peasant Madness and a Castle Hurry-Up; a clean ramp returns down its wireform to set up the next shot. · then: Keep ramping while a hurry-up runs to stack its value millions higher, then cash the whole thing at the keep. Ten hurry-ups collected light an extra ball, and folding several into one stack still counts every one of them toward that ten.
  • Dragon Ramp & Damsel Tower (ladder) — lit: The right dragon ramp mirrors the left -- four shots complete Save the Damsels and light Damsel Madness -- and after enough hits its diverter opens to the Damsel tower tucked up the back right. · then: Farm it the same way for hurry-up stacking, since the ramp keeps handing the ball back for quick repeats. Damsels is one of the five Madness types feeding the wizard, so this ramp is doing two jobs at once.
  • Joust Orbits (lanes) — lit: The two orbits joust by feeding into one another, and three jousts win a Joust Victory to light Joust Madness; the third victory closes the orbit gate so the ball spills into the pop bumpers. · then: Win three victories across the game to light the Joust Champion dot for the wizard. With the gate shut the orbits routing into the jets also rack bonus switches, which your end-of-ball Bonus X then multiplies.
  • Trolls (status) — lit: The two motorized trolls pop up to guard the lock and Merlin's eject; the standups flanking the castle light Trolls! at Merlin's saucer, and a ball glanced off a raised troll into a feature banks a Troll Bomb. · then: With Trolls! lit, hit each raised troll three times to kill it; kills ladder toward Master of Trolls and its title dot. Bank Troll Bombs now off those ricochets, because those banked bombs are the only thing that drops the deathless trolls in the wizard fight.
  • Upper Rollover Lanes (lanes) — lit: The two upper rollover lanes light for Bonus X, but the lane the ball is drifting toward may not be the lit one. · then: Complete both lanes to add to Bonus X, the multiplier on an end-of-ball bonus that builds all ball long; the same lit lane is the Normal Skill Shot for a bigger Bonus X jump off the plunge. Bonus X only holds for the ball unless Merlin gives you Hold Bonus X to carry it over. · control: Either flipper button: a flipper tap walks the lit rollover across to the other top lane, so you can slide it beneath whichever lane the ball is heading for and finish the pair (as the ball rolls through the upper lanes)
  • Merlin's Saucer & Lock (status) — lit: Completing the three Merlin standups lights Merlin's Magic at the right eject, and the eject is also where Multiball Madness and the mini-wizard begin; the Moat Jump left of the castle is your ball lock. · then: Take Merlin for a mystery award -- Light Lock, extra Bonus X, Hold Bonus X -- then lock three balls at the Moat Jump for Castle Multiball. Start Multiball Madness here with as many Madness types lit as you can, since ball count and jackpots both scale with how many are showing.
  • Catapult (status) — lit: The far-left catapult flings loaded objects at the castle, and three slams into it light Catapult Madness; the flipper buttons cycle which object the catapult will hurl next. · then: Land all five different objects to trip the hidden Barnyard Multiball, and three completed slams also light the Catapult Ace dot. It is the single toughest shot on the game and the likeliest to drain, so take it only from a dead cradle when nothing safer is a hit from lighting.

Lane change: the flipper buttons move the lit lane. A flipper tap walks the lit upper rollover across to the other top lane. Set it beneath whichever lane the ball is heading for to finish both, which steps up Bonus X and multiplies your end-of-ball bonus; Bonus X stays for the ball only, unless Merlin's Hold Bonus X carries it forward.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Get safe points and the multiplier going: take the Normal Skill Shot into a flashing top lane for Bonus X, hit both ramps to light Peasant and Damsels, grab an orbit toward Joust, and bank your first castle. Landing a castle or two early sets your score up for the rest of the game. — Normal Skill Shot, Peasant Madness (Revolting Peasants), Damsel Save (Right Ramp Farm), Castle Destruction Loop
  • Building — Light Madness types in single-ball play, then start Multiball Madness at Merlin with as many lit as you can for a four-ball start. Lock three balls for Castle Multiball, and keep razing castles the whole time, since the keep out-scores almost everything even during a multiball. — Multiball Madness, Castle Multiball, Castle Destruction Loop
  • The big one — Battle for the Kingdom is the summit. Light all six blue dots -- the five Madness types plus Castle Crusher -- then storm the King of Payne's keep, clearing its immortal trolls with banked bombs between gate shots. Winning lights Victory Laps whose Ultra Combos double each Victory Lap as you chain them, climbing with no cap anyone has found; Royal Madness is the mini-wizard you pass on the way. — Battle for the Kingdom, Ultra Combo (Victory Laps), Royal Madness

The Launch Button (Troll Bombs) on Medieval Madness

The red ball-launch button on the cabinet front — this game auto-plunges, so there is no shooter rod.

The red button that fires your plunge is also a weapon. Bank Troll Bombs from the troll standups or from Merlin, and one press puts a troll down without you having to hit it — which is exactly what you want when both trolls are up and you have three balls to watch.

  • Start of every ball: Launches the ball. Hold the left flipper as you press for the Super Skill Shot feed to the left flipper.
  • Trolls! with bombs banked: Detonates one bomb, worth a single hit on whichever troll is nearest to dying.
  • Multiball Madness with trolls up: Spend bombs rather than aiming — each one still counts toward Master of Trolls while you juggle balls.
  • Battle for the Kingdom, second phase: Bombs are how you clear the immortal trolls guarding the gate, so save a few for the wizard mode.
  • Save the Children video mode: Keep a thumb on it here too — the button is documented as having a job in this mode, but no source spells out what it actually does.

Goals in Medieval Madness

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Normal Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    The safe default plunge: roll the flashing top rollover lane for points plus a compounding +5 Bonus X. The consistency/beginner choice.

    Reward: 50K +10K per successive skill shot, plus +5 Bonus X

  • Super Skill Shot skillshot · Medium

    Hold the LEFT flipper during the plunge to feed the ball around the left orbit to the left flipper with all major shots lit; collect one for points and an instant Castle Hurry-Up.

    Reward: 100K and starts a Castle Hurry-Up

  • Castle Destruction Loop combo · Medium

    The primary tournament money farm: moat to drop the drawbridge, gate to open it, castle to destroy it, repeat. The castle out-values almost every other shot, even in multiball.

    Reward: Destroy 6 castles (~71M+ total); 2nd castle lights an Extra Ball on default 3-ball settings

  • Peasant Hurry-Up Stacking combo · Medium

    The go-to filler farm: keep hammering the left ramp. Four left ramps light Peasant Revolt and a Castle Hurry-Up; keep stacking Madness completions to build the hurry-up millions at a time, then cash it at the castle.

    Reward: Castle Hurry-Up cash-in, which can be built well past 50M; 10 hurry-ups light an Extra Ball

  • Troll Combo combo · Hard

    The only standard-playfield combo: ricochet the ball off a raised Troll into a scoring feature to bank a Troll Bomb — the key tool for clearing immortal trolls in Battle for the Kingdom.

    Reward: 1 Troll Bomb (3 Troll Bombs off both trolls into the saucer)

  • Ultra Combo (Victory Laps) combo · Hard

    The game's highest per-shot ceiling. After beating Battle for the Kingdom, all major shots light for Victory Laps; a second quick major shot doubles the current lap value as an Ultra Combo.

    Reward: Up to ~4M per Ultra Combo; uncapped chain until it times out

  • Damsel Save (Right Ramp Farm) combo · Medium

    Mirror of the Peasant farm. Four right (dragon) ramps complete Save the Damsels and feed hurry-up stacking; the ramp geometry returns the ball to the flipper for easy repeats.

    Reward: Lights Damsel Madness; feeds Multiball Madness and Battle for the Kingdom

  • Multiball Madness multiball · Medium

    The signature multiball. Light as many of the five Madness modes as possible in single-ball play, then start at Merlin's saucer — ball count and jackpots scale with Madnesses lit.

    Reward: 2-ball (1 lit), 3-ball (2-4 lit), or 4-ball (all 5 lit) with escalating jackpots

  • Castle Multiball multiball · Medium

    The lock-based multiball. Lock 3 balls in the castle, then run the 3-stage ramp-jackpot loop that compounds each cycle.

    Reward: ~14.25M per full 3-stage cycle; +250K jackpot / +500K super base each loop

  • Barnyard Multiball multiball · Hard

    The hidden novelty multiball. Use the flipper buttons to cycle the catapult item and fling all five distinct items into the castle.

    Reward: 3-ball multiball; every switch worth 75,000

  • Joust Madness (Joust Victory) mode · Easy

    Joust the two orbits into each other three times to win a Joust Victory and light Joust Madness.

    Reward: ~100K; lights Joust Madness. 3 completions light the Joust Champion blue dot.

  • Catapult Madness (Catapult Slam) mode · Hard

    Load and slam items off the far-left catapult at the castle three times. The single hardest, most drain-prone shot on the game.

    Reward: ~100K; lights Catapult Madness. 3 completions light the Catapult Ace blue dot.

  • Trolls! (Troll Madness) mode · Medium

    Hit the two standup targets either side of the castle entrance to light Trolls! at Merlin's Saucer, then hit each raised troll three times to destroy it. Kills count toward Master of Trolls, which needs ten.

    Reward: ~1M completion; kills feed Master of Trolls (10 trolls) for the Trolls blue dot.

  • Peasant Madness (Revolting Peasants) mode · Easy

    Four left ramps cycle the Peasant awards to Peasant Revolt, lighting Peasant Madness. The safest high-percentage mode.

    Reward: 100K on the 4th; lights Peasant Madness. 3 completions light the Patron of the Peasants blue dot.

  • Build Bonus X multiplier · Easy

    Bonus X multiplies a switch bonus that builds all ball long, so it is worth real money on a long ball. Completing F-I-R-E across the inlanes and outlanes adds +2, completing both top rollovers adds +1, a Normal Skill Shot adds +5, and Merlin hands out both +5 Bonus X and Hold Bonus X.

    Reward: End-of-ball Bonus × multiplier (players reach ~100×; 50-60M bonus on a long ball)

  • Jets Bonus Builder multiplier · Medium

    MM has no separate playfield or shot multiplier — but during Multiball Madness you can keep the ball up top so the jets rack up bonus switches, which the end-of-ball Bonus X then scales, while you set up clean Lock shots.

    Reward: Extra bonus switches (multiplied by Bonus X at end of ball) while you work clean Lock shots

  • Battle for the Kingdom wizard · Hard

    The sole grand wizard mode. Light all six blue title dots, then shoot the lit castle to start a 4-ball battle: Battle Jackpots, then storm the King of Payne's castle clearing immortal trolls with Troll Bombs.

    Reward: ~97.5M scripted (2.5M jackpots, ~5M gate shots, ~50M finish) plus Victory Laps

  • Royal Madness wizard · Hard

    The mini-wizard mode. Complete all five Madness types and play Multiball Madness, then shoot the right eject to start a frantic 20-second-reset shot round.

    Reward: Extra Ball on completion (some settings pay ~15M)

  • Opener A — Safe & Consistent strategy · Easy

    Beginner opening line: bank Bonus X, hit the two safe ramps to light Peasant and Damsel, grab an orbit toward Joust, and begin banking Castle #1.

    Reward: Peasant + Damsel lit, Joust progressed, first castle banked

  • Opener B — Aggressive Points strategy · Medium

    Points-first opening: use the Super Skill Shot to collect a lit shot and arm a Castle Hurry-Up, then cash it immediately or grow it with left-ramp stacking.

    Reward: Fast 100K + a Castle Hurry-Up to cash or stack

  • Bowen 60-Second Tournament Loop strategy · Medium

    The canonical all-game line: moat to drop the drawbridge, gate to open, castle to destroy, repeat — weaving in ramp/orbit shots to light Madnesses for a big Multiball Madness where you dump more castles.

    Reward: Highest reliable EV; the castle out-values most shots even in multiball

  • Extra Ball Routes strategy · Medium

    Three reliable ways this game hands you another ball: bank your second castle, run your Castle Hurry-Up count to ten, or finish the mini-wizard shot round.

    Reward: Extra Ball (route-dependent; some also pay a Super Jackpot extra ball on operator settings)

  • Save the Children Video Mode secret · Medium

    Behind the FIRE rollovers sits a full video game most players never see. Complete FIRE eight times across a game to light it, then shoot Merlin's Saucer to start it — you steer a defender with the flippers and shoot down a flock of buzzards trying to carry off the children.

    Reward: 100,000 for each buzzard killed, plus a 1,000,000 bonus for completing the round
