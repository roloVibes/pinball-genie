Twilight Zone

Bally, 1993 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Twilight Zone in the Pinball Genie app →

Designed by Pat Lawlor (co-design Larry DeMar & Ted Estes), Twilight Zone is a single-hardware-revision 1993 widebody (~15,235 units) — there is no Pro/Premium/LE split; that concept is a modern Stern-era convention. Variation across units is limited to ROM code revisions, not physical layout.

The core loop: GUMBALL, Locks, and Multiball

The Left Ramp spells GUM and the Right Ramp spells BALL. Completing GUMBALL lights the Lock Lane and the Gumball Machine (a ball-holding prop that also dispenses balls when shot directly via the Right Orbit, and periodically loads the ceramic Powerball). Locking one ball and starting Standard Multiball gives a 15M starting Jackpot; locking a second ball first (respell GUMBALL) jumps that base to 40M — the single biggest EV decision in the ruleset. In Multiball, the Player Piano is the Jackpot shot and the Camera relights it; each collection steps the base up to 5 times, and the seven Greed Targets flashing throughout add 5M apiece directly to the live Jackpot. The Powerball landing in the Jackpot shot doubles its value. Two balls draining ends Multiball; a 15-second Lock Lane restart window follows if no Jackpot was collected yet.

Other multiballs

Fast Lock Multiball is qualified via its Door Panel award: a radio hurry-up (~17-20M counting down to a 5.3M floor) plays, and shooting the Lock Lane while it's live banks the value and starts 3-ball multiball instantly, with Lock Lane repeating that same value on every subsequent shot (no restart grace period). Powerball Mania kicks off when the ceramic Powerball loads into the Gumball Machine: an immediate payout, a three-ball multiball (one ball from the Gumball Machine plus two from the Lock/trough — the Powerball itself stays loaded in the machine), and every switch scoring 250K toward a frenzy total. The Right Ramp sends the ball onto the raised Powerfield mini-playfield, where player-controlled Magna-Flips work alongside the real flippers; walking the ball out the upper exit ("Defeating the Power") pays a flat, repeatable 50M Mania Jackpot. Most Mania scoring banks into end-of-ball bonus rather than paying live.

Modes: 15 rotating Door Panels

One of 15 Door Panel awards is always "flashing." Jet Bumper hits rotate which panel is flashing; panels are collected by shooting the lit Player Piano, by Defeating the Power on the Powerfield (same effect as a Piano hit), or by the Slot Machine scoop (awards a random panel instead of the flashing one). Notable panels: Town Square Madness (every switch adds to a running total, Jet Bumpers step their own value), Clock Millions (the Clock Target pays the current hour-hand value, up to 12M/hit), The Spiral (escalating orbit-shot values, no grace timer), Greed (clear all seven Greed Targets for 5M each), Fast Lock, Lite Gumball, Lite Extra Ball, Camera (stacks the Camera's 8-award cycle), Hitchhiker, Clock Chaos, Super Skill Shot, 10 Million, Super Slot, and Battle the Power. Each panel is collected once per cycle; once all 14 non-wizard panels are cleared, the 15th slot — "The Handle" — becomes flashing.

Multipliers

TZ has no continuous playfield multiplier. The only true multiplier is Bonus X, built by shooting the Left Ramp while it flashes (lit via a Right Inlane rollover): steps 2X→3X→4X→5X, capped, and lost entirely at ball end — it never carries over. A handful of shot-level escalators function similarly: the Powerball doubles the Multiball Jackpot shot, Camera's 3X Town Square award triples the Town Square subtotal, and Defeating the Power doubles the current Powerfield subtotal.

Wizard mode: Lost in the Zone (LITZ)

Collecting all 14 rotating Door Panels lights The Handle; collecting it launches Lost in the Zone, a fixed 45-second, 6-ball frenzy (all balls auto-plunged from the emptied Gumball Machine, with continuous ball-saving). Nearly everything runs simultaneously — Clock Millions, Town Square Madness (Jets only), the Powerfield (50M Mania Jackpots still available), all seven Greed Targets, a permanently-lit Piano paying random "Odd Change," a permanently-lit Slot Machine, flat-rate Hitchhikers, and 10M-per-hit Left Ramp shots. When the timer expires, virtually everything accrued banks into end-of-ball bonus — a strong LITZ is worth roughly 300M.

End-of-ball bonus & strategy

Base bonus is (500K × Hitchhikers + 500K × Door Panels) × Bonus X, plus banked subtotals from Town Square, Clock Millions/Chaos, Greed, and Powerfield play. Because so much scoring is deferred, protecting a large bonus (don't tilt) matters more than rushing to cash out. The standard opener is the safe Left Ramp → Right Ramp → Player Piano loop, which simultaneously spells GUMBALL, loads the Gumball Machine, and progresses Door Panels while avoiding the table's worst drains: the Jet Bumpers/Town Square area and a mishandled Slot Machine kickout.

How Twilight Zone plays

Twilight Zone is a sprawling Bally crammed with toys - a clock, a Gumball Machine, a ceramic Powerball, and a mini-playfield you steer by hand. Collect all the rotating Door Panels to reach Lost in the Zone, its six-ball finale.

The loop

  1. Run the Left Ramp into the Right Ramp into the Player Piano, the safe loop that finishes GUM-BALL to light the Lock and load the Gumball Machine.
  2. Collect the flashing Door Panel at the Piano - the Jet Bumpers rotate which one is up - to start modes like Clock Millions, Greed, and Battle the Power.
  3. Lock balls for regular Multiball, holding off until a second ball is locked so the jackpot opens at 40M instead of 15M.
  4. Feed the Powerball into the Gumball Machine for Powerball Mania, then ride the Right Ramp onto the Powerfield and drive its magnets with the flipper buttons for 50M jackpots.
  5. Clear every Door Panel and then The Handle to drop into Lost in the Zone.

Reading the lights

  • Door Panels (status) — lit: One Door Panel flashes at a time, the Player Piano collects whichever is flashing, the Slot Machine hands you a random one, and defeating the Power also awards the flashing panel. · flashing: Jet Bumper hits keep rotating which panel is currently flashing, so you can aim the selection by pounding the bumpers first. · then: Work through all fourteen rotating panels and the fifteenth slot, The Handle, lights up. Collecting The Handle launches Lost in the Zone, so every panel you bank is a step toward the wizard mode.
  • GUM-BALL and the Lock (letters) — lit: The Left Ramp spells GUM and the Right Ramp spells BALL, and finishing the word lights both the Lock Lane and the Gumball Machine. · then: Lock a ball at the Lock Lane, then respell the word and lock a second before you start - that swings the opening jackpot from 15M up to 40M. Shoot the Lock or Left Ramp again to launch the three-ball Multiball.
  • The Powerfield (mode) — lit: Collecting the Battle the Power panel opens the Right Ramp as the way up onto the raised mini-playfield. · then: Drive the ball out the top exit to defeat the Power for points and the flashing Door Panel, or drop out the bottom for nothing. During Powerball Mania each exit pays a flat 50M, again and again. · control: Both flipper buttons: the flipper buttons stop working the main flippers and instead fire the two corner magnets on the mini-playfield, and gentle taps nudge the ball upward to the exit at the top (while the ball is on the Powerfield)
  • Camera and Dead End (ladder) — lit: The Camera cycles a fixed set of awards you have to exhaust before they repeat, and the one to wait for is 3X Town Square. · then: Build the Town Square total in the Jet Bumpers, take 3X Town Square at the Camera to triple it on the spot, then bank double the running value at the Dead End for the biggest single grab this system offers.
  • The Clock (mode) — lit: During Clock Millions the Clock Target is worth the value the hour hand is showing, as much as 12M a strike, so a high hand is worth chasing. · then: Hammer the target while the timer runs, then squeeze a couple more fixed 12M hits out of the grace period. Six or more hits tacks on a 10M Clock Damage Bonus.
  • Town Square Jet Bumpers (status) — lit: The Jet Bumpers pull double duty, piling up the Town Square value while every hit also rotates which Door Panel is flashing at the Piano. · then: During Town Square Madness every switch adds to a running total that banks into your end-of-ball bonus. Between modes, pound the Jets to line up the exact Door Panel you want before shooting the Piano.
  • Bonus X (multiplier) — lit: Rolling the Right Inlane sets the Left Ramp flashing for Bonus X, and shooting it there steps your end-of-ball multiplier toward a hard cap of 5X. · flashing: The Left Ramp flashes for Bonus X only right after you roll the Right Inlane. · then: Cash the flashing ramp to climb 2X, 3X, 4X, 5X and to pick up Robots along the way. It resets every ball and never carries over, so bank it before you drain.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Plunge firmly but a hair short of full so the ball clears the red and orange rollovers into yellow for the 10M Skill Shot, then ride out the Rocket that fires you into the Jet Bumpers. Settle into the Left Ramp, Right Ramp, Player Piano loop that keeps the ball away from the outlanes. — Yellow Skill Shot, Beginner First 30 Seconds
  • Building — Spell GUM-BALL and lock a second ball before starting Multiball for the 40M opening jackpot, farming Greed Targets to fatten each jackpot before you cash it. Collect Door Panels for modes, and stop the Camera on 3X Town Square for a huge Dead End payoff. — Best Opener — Lock the 2nd Ball Before Multiball, Standard (Regular) Multiball, Multiball Jackpot Farming, 3X Town Square (Camera award)
  • The big one — Collect all fourteen Door Panels and then The Handle to drop into Lost in the Zone, a 45-second six-ball frenzy with endless ball saves where a strong run banks around 300M. Powerball Mania, with its repeatable flat 50M Powerfield jackpots, is the other giant on the table. — Lost in the Zone (6-Ball Wizard Mode), Complete the Door Panel Cycle, Powerball Mania

Does Twilight Zone have an action button?

Twilight Zone has no dedicated button, but do not assume the flipper buttons only flip. Once the Right Ramp puts the ball onto the raised Powerfield, those same two buttons also fire the Magna-Flip magnets in the mini-playfield's lower corners while the real flippers keep working below. Short taps walk the ball up toward the top exit, which is how you collect the 50M Mania jackpots.

Goals in Twilight Zone

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Yellow Skill Shot skillshot · Medium

    Strong controlled plunge to clear all three rollovers for the biggest Skill Shot award and the deepest Jet Bumper advance.

    Reward: 10M + advances all three Jet Bumpers one step

  • Red Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    The safe, low-risk skill shot option — smallest reward, but the easiest rollover to control before the Rocket fires the ball into the Jet Bumpers.

    Reward: 2M + advances Jet Bumpers 1 level

  • Gum-Ball Combo (2-way) combo · Easy

    The simplest scoring combo on the table: Left Ramp into Right Ramp for a quick 5M.

    Reward: 5M for the 2-way combo

  • LR-RR-Piano 3-Way Combo combo · Medium

    The game's core repeatable loop — safe, builds three progress tracks at once, and pays 10M each lap.

    Reward: 10M 3-way combo + spells GUM-BALL, which lights both the Lock and the Gumball Machine

  • LR-Lock-Camera 3-Way Combo combo · Medium

    Alternate 3-way combo routing through an unlit Lock Lane into the Camera.

    Reward: 10M 3-way combo

  • LR-Lock-Hitchhiker 3-Way Combo combo · Medium

    Same opening as the Camera combo but finishing through the Hitchhiker Lane instead.

    Reward: 10M 3-way combo (alternate ending to the Lock combo)

  • 4-Way Combo via Hidden Lane combo · Hard

    The advanced money combo — Left Ramp, Right Ramp, Hidden Lane, then Camera for 20M.

    Reward: 20M 4-way combo

  • Standard (Regular) Multiball multiball · Medium

    Spell GUMBALL to light Lock, lock balls, then loop Piano and Camera to build and cash Jackpots.

    Reward: 3-ball Multiball, 15M starting Jackpot (40M with 2nd lock) + up to 5 relights at +10M and +5M per Greed Target

  • Fast Lock Multiball multiball · Medium

    Collect the Fast Lock Door Panel, then catch the hurry-up value at the Lock Lane to start an instant 3-ball multiball.

    Reward: Instant 3-ball Multiball + banks the hurry-up value (~17-20M down to a 5.3M floor); Lock repeats that value each shot

  • Powerball Mania multiball · Hard

    Load the ceramic Powerball into the Gumball Machine, then work the Powerfield's Magna-Flips for repeated 50M jackpots.

    Reward: Powerball Payoff + 3-ball Multiball, then a repeatable flat 50M Mania Jackpot per Powerfield exit

  • Town Square Madness mode · Easy

    Timed round where every switch — especially the Jet Bumpers — adds to a running point total.

    Reward: Running total starts at 3M, +350K per switch plus stepped Jet Bumper value; banks into end-of-ball bonus

  • Clock Millions mode · Easy

    Repeatedly hit the Clock Target while the hour hand is high for stacking millions.

    Reward: Up to 12M per Clock Target hit; grace-period hits are a fixed 12M; 6+ hits adds a 10M Clock Damage Bonus

  • The Spiral mode · Medium

    Alternate orbit shots for escalating Spiral Breakthru values with no time-out grace period.

    Reward: 10M for the first two orbit shots, then 15M per Spiral Breakthru shot with no grace period

  • Greed mode · Medium

    Clear all seven Greed Targets for a fast-stacking bonus total.

    Reward: 5M per Greed Target hit; the bank relights once all seven are cleared

  • Bonus Multiplier (Bonus X) multiplier · Easy

    Twilight Zone's only true general multiplier — applies to end-of-ball bonus and resets every ball.

    Reward: Steps Bonus X 2X to 5X (hard cap) on end-of-ball bonus + awards Robots; resets every ball

  • 3X Town Square (Camera award) multiplier · Medium

    Build the Town Square value in the Jet Bumpers first, then take the 3X Town Square Camera award — it pays three times whatever the value is at the moment you collect it.

    Reward: Three times the current Town Square value, paid the moment the award is collected

  • Complete the Door Panel Cycle wizard · Hard

    The qualification path to Lost in the Zone — collect all 14 rotating Door Panels, then The Handle.

    Reward: Completes the Door Panel cycle and launches Lost in the Zone wizard mode

  • Lost in the Zone (6-Ball Wizard Mode) wizard · Hard

    The 45-second, 6-ball finale — nearly every mode runs at once and everything banks into end-of-ball bonus.

    Reward: 45-second 6-ball frenzy: 10M/hit Left Ramp, 50M Powerfield Jackpots, Clock/Greed scoring — all banks into bonus (~300M for a strong run)

  • Beginner First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    Plunge firmly but short of full strength for the biggest Skill Shot, ride the Rocket into the Jet Bumpers, then settle into the safe LR-RR-Piano loop.

    Reward: Biggest Skill Shot + Jet Bumper advances, then a safe GUM-BALL/Lock loop that avoids the worst drains

  • Best Opener — Lock the 2nd Ball Before Multiball strategy · Medium

    Loop the core combo but hold off starting Multiball until a second ball is locked for the 40M starting Jackpot.

    Reward: 40M starting Multiball Jackpot instead of 15M, by locking a 2nd ball before starting

  • Multiball Jackpot Farming strategy · Medium

    Farm Greed Targets before cashing each Jackpot, and keep a ball parked since there's no ball-saver in Multiball.

    Reward: Inflated Jackpots (+5M per Greed Target) before each cash-out, protected by a parked ball

  • Light and Collect Extra Ball strategy · Medium

    Take the Extra Ball Door Panel and collect it at the Lock Lane; the outlane Extra Ball is a separate award, lit by the Camera's Lite Outlanes award or the Orange Super Skill Shot, and steered between the outlanes by the slingshots.

    Reward: Extra Ball, collected by rolling through the left outlane while it is the lit side

  • Battle the Power mode · Hard

    The Door Panel that lights the Right Ramp into the Powerfield; win the mini-playfield fight for points and a free Door Panel.

    Reward: Points for defeating the Power plus the currently flashing Door Panel
