World Cup Soccer

Bally, 1994 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open World Cup Soccer in the Pinball Genie app →

Designed by John Popadiuk and Larry DeMar (software: DeMar/Matt Coriale; art: Kevin O'Connor; callouts: Tim Kitzrow), released February 1994 on Midway's WPC-S hardware, roughly 8,743 units built. There is no Pro/Premium/LE tiering — this predates that convention entirely, and it's a single production run. Notably, WCS94 has only two standard flippers — no upper, lower, or mini-flipper — unusual for a WPC-era title. The Magna-Save is a player-activated magnet (not a flipper) that catches a ball headed for the center drain.

Core shots

Two orbits/ramps mirror each other: the Left Spiral (orbit, hosts the Ultra Spinner and city travel) and Left/Right Ramps (feed BUILD, multiball lock, and Ultra Ramps). Center stage is the GOAL — a wide net hole guarded by a moving plastic goalie, fed either directly or safely via the Assist saucer (upper-left, flipper-fired straight past the goalie). A spinning rubber soccer ball toy sits upper-mid-playfield and deflects shots unpredictably when Goal is lit or during multiball. Other shots: twin Free Kick saucers/target, Striker standups (spell S-T-R-I-K-E-R), Star Rollovers (relight Goal), Striker Scoop (mystery awards + Extra Ball), TV/Final Draw scoop (dual-purpose: TV Awards and Multiball start), 3 jet bumpers, and a real mechanical plunger feeding a 3-way Skill Shot divider.

Skill Shot

Manual plunge into one of three flashing top-lane dividers: 1st = 5M, 2nd = 10M, 3rd = 30M + spots a city. Clearing all three across the game nets a bonus city.

Multiball

Four flashing BUILD shots (left spiral, left ramp, right ramp, Striker scoop) each light a word on the soccer-ball graphic — Strength, Stamina, Skill, Speed, Spirit. All five lit → Lock (physically held on the left ramp's upper shelf). Shoot the lit Final Draw scoop to start. A random Striker Scoop award can also start multiball (guaranteed on ball 3 if not yet achieved, but low starting rank). Multiball is ranked against 15 national teams, USA (20M) up through Germany (250M) — Goal scores the jackpot and beats the current team; either ramp relights Goal and advances rank. The Assist saucer is scripted not to miss during multiball, making it the most reliable jackpot loop. Draining before a jackpot lets you resume via a ramp or the scoop. Beating Germany lights Special at the right outlane and unlocks flat 50M Victory Laps (Goal/ramps, alternating after 5 laps).

Modes

Ultra Modes (Ramps, Spinner, Jets, Goalie) are awarded one per Goal scored, stack up to all four concurrently, and cap at 30M + 10M bonus each — but only pay out through end-of-ball bonus, not live score. TV Awards run in a fixed sequence at the Final Draw scoop, re-lit every 4 Goals: Big Goal Round (3 Goal shots, 15/15/30M), Extra Ball Round, Hit The Goalie!, and Where's Striker (3 Striker standup hits). Boston Tea Party is a hurry-up (40M counting to a 10M floor, collected at the left spiral) unlocked by traveling to Boston.

Cities and travel

Nine cities (Chicago, Dallas, Boston, New York, Orlando, Washington DC, San Francisco, Detroit, Los Angeles) are advanced by lighting TRAVEL at the left spiral (both ramps or both top lanes, then shoot the spiral), by a Striker "Advance Three Cities" award, or by clearing all three Skill Shot lanes. Washington DC lights Extra Ball; reaching Los Angeles (9th city) qualifies the wizard mode.

Multipliers

There is no true 2X/3X playfield or bonus multiplier anywhere on this table. The closest analogues are escalating values: jet bumpers jump from 100K to a flat 1M/pop for the rest of the ball after 25,000 cumulative hits, and the Super Free Kick award climbs +5M per consecutive Free Kick target hit from a 10M base.

Wizard mode — Final Match

Reach Los Angeles (9th city) and shoot the lit Final Draw scoop to start a 45-second, 5-ball timed multiball against Germany, every ball manually plunged. Each Goal scored is worth 75M; Germany accrues its own virtual counter. Beat their total at the buzzer for a 500M bonus; a tie forces sudden-death overtime.

Bonus

Unusually, end-of-ball bonus draws on career (whole-game) totals: 5M per Goal scored so far this entire game + 5M per city traveled to this entire game + that ball's own Ultra Mode earnings — no bonus multiplier applies, and a tilt forfeits it.

Strategy

Center drain is the dominant killer (roughly 7 of 10 balls lost down the middle), so trapping discipline matters more than on most contemporaries lacking an upper flipper for playfield control. Two competing high-score approaches: safe "Goal-only" grinding (Goal → relight → repeat, banking Ultra bonus, no lock/drain risk) versus rushing the 4 BUILD shots to lock and grind the ranked multiball jackpot ladder up to Germany's Victory Laps, the single best sustained per-shot value in the game.

How World Cup Soccer plays

Play out the 1994 World Cup: beat one ranked nation after another, travel the host cities, and reach the Final against Germany. Shoot the moving goalie's net for Goals while you build toward the multiball that carries your whole score.

The loop

  1. Shoot a ramp, the left spiral, or the Striker scoop to fill a BUILD panel; fill all five to light Lock.
  2. Trap that fifth ball on the left ramp's shelf, and the Final Draw scoop opens to launch multiball.
  3. In multiball, beat the goalie for the lit Goal jackpot, then relight it with a ramp to climb from USA up to Germany.
  4. Feed the Assist saucer whenever the keeper is in your way -- it fires the ball into the net for a jackpot you almost never miss.
  5. Between multiballs, grind Goal-and-relight for Ultra modes that bank into your bonus, and travel the cities toward the Final Match.

Reading the lights

  • Goal / Goalie (shot arrows) — lit: The Goal arrow lights when the net is open, but a keeper slides across the mouth so an open arrow still has to beat him, and the arrow goes dark once you score until you relight it. · then: Relight it with either ramp or by sweeping the four star rollovers and shoot again -- outside multiball each Goal also hands you an Ultra mode, and in multiball it is the jackpot climbing from USA up to Germany. When the goalie is parked in the lane, feed the Assist saucer instead and let it fire the ball in for you.
  • BUILD Panels (letters) — lit: Every BUILD source you hit fills one panel -- Strength, Stamina, Skill, Speed, Spirit -- on the soccer-ball graphic, and a filled panel stays filled so you only chase the ones still open. · then: Fill all five to light Lock on the left ramp, trap the ball on its upper shelf, and the Draw scoop lights to launch multiball. Locking also jumps your multiball starting rank up four teams, so it pays far more than the panels alone.
  • Assist Saucer (status) — lit: The Assist saucer stays live through multiball and catches a ball dropping in from the upper left, lining it up so you can flipper-fire it straight at an open net with the keeper taken out of the shot. · then: Tap a flipper to auto-fire the caught ball into the Goal -- the feed is scripted not to miss, so as long as the ball keeps returning here you have the most reliable repeating jackpot on the game. Relight the next team's jackpot on a ramp before the Goal will pay again.
  • Ultra Modes (mode) — lit: Every Goal you score switches on one of four Ultra modes -- Ramps, Spinner, Jets, Goalie -- and up to all four run at once, each paying a bonus every time you hit its shot while it stays lit. · then: Feed the matching shot while its Ultra is lit -- ramps, the left-orbit spinner, the jets, or the goalie -- to pile up points that only cash at your end-of-ball bonus. Each Ultra caps out and ends, and a tilt wipes the whole bonus, so nurse the ball rather than risk it.
  • STRIKER Standups (letters) — lit: The Striker standups spell out the word STRIKER, a lit target marks a letter you have not knocked down yet, and the overhead Header target spots both a letter and a star in one hit. · then: Complete the word to light the Striker scoop, then shoot it for a random award -- a Super Free Kick, an Advance Three Cities jump, or even a multiball dropped in at the bottom rank. The scoop also starts lit at the beginning of the game, so you have one award waiting for free.
  • Final Draw / TV Scoop (mode) — lit: The Final Draw scoop lights for multiball once you have a ball locked and otherwise cycles the TV Awards -- a Big Goal round, an Extra Ball round -- relighting every four Goals you score. · then: Shoot it to launch multiball or to start whichever TV Award is up, and if both are lit the multiball wins. Travel all nine host cities to Los Angeles and this same scoop starts the Final Match against Germany.
  • City Travel (ladder) — lit: TRAVEL lights at the left spiral once you complete both ramps or both top lanes, and the host cities you bank stay banked toward the Final, so a spiral shot always moves you one city closer. · then: Keep lighting and collecting TRAVEL to climb the nine cities up to Los Angeles, which arms the Final Match at the Draw scoop. A stop in Boston lights a countdown hurry-up at the spinner that bleeds from forty million down to a floor, so collect it fast.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Take the free skill shot into a flashing divider on every plunge, then play the ramps and left spiral -- they fill BUILD panels and feed the flippers back cleanly. When the keeper is in your way use the Assist saucer to put the ball in the net instead of a cold shot, and keep a thumb on the Magna-Save for anything dropping down the middle. — Three-Tier Skill Shot, Beginner First 30 Seconds
  • Building — Fill all five BUILD panels, lock on the left ramp, and start multiball at the Draw scoop, because multiball is the score engine here. Between multiballs grind Goal-and-relight for Ultra modes that bank into your bonus, and spell STRIKER for a mystery award. — Multiball: Build & Lock, Best Opener: Build Toward Multiball Fast, Goal Ultra-Farming Loop
  • The big one — Travel all nine cities to Los Angeles and start the Final Match against Germany -- a forty-five-second timed multiball where every Goal is worth seventy-five million and out-scoring Germany pays a five-hundred-million bonus. Short of that, climbing a multiball all the way to the Germany jackpot and into flat fifty-million Victory Laps is the biggest scoring you will bank in a normal game. — Final Match: Beat Germany, Final Match Qualification, Post-Germany Victory Laps

The Magna-Goal Save on World Cup Soccer

Left cabinet wall, just behind the left flipper button.

WCS94 hands you one extra control, and it is pure defense: a small button on the cabinet's left wall, tucked behind the flipper button, that switches on a magnet mounted over the left flipper tip. It exists for the ball coming straight down the middle — hit it and the ball sticks there instead of splitting the flippers. You hold one charge at a time and have to earn the next one.

  • Ball dropping straight down the middle: Fires the magnet above the left flipper tip so the ball parks there and you can trap it instead of draining.
  • First ball of a fresh game: The save starts lit, so you already have one charge banked before you have hit anything.
  • Right after you spend it: The button goes dead — mashing it again does nothing until you relight the magnet.
  • Rolling through the left inlane: That starts the Light Magna-Goal Save target blinking on the lower right; hit it while it blinks to recharge the button.
  • Ball drifting toward the left outlane: Wrong save — the kickback covers that lane. The magnet only guards the center drain.
  • Goalie rejects your Goal shot: Rejects often come back center-ish, so keep a thumb near the button whenever the goalie is in the way.

Goals in World Cup Soccer

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Three-Tier Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    Real mechanical plunger feeds a 3-way divided loop; first hit scores 5M, but the real prize is stacking toward a city.

    Reward: 5M / 10M / 30M + spots a city, depending on which flashing divider you land

  • Full Skill Shot Sweep skillshot · Medium

    Clearing all three flashing skill-shot lanes over the course of a game is one of three ways to advance cities toward Final Match.

    Reward: Advances a city toward Final Match; clearing all 3 lanes across the game can net an extra city

  • BUILD Combo → Lock combo · Hard

    Shoot BUILD-lit shots — left ramp, left spiral, right ramp — to fill panels on the soccer-ball graphic, then close on the right ramp once Lock is lit to lock the ball.

    Reward: Fills a BUILD panel per shot; locks the ball for multiball once all 5 panels are filled

  • Assist-Saucer Jackpot Loop combo · Medium

    During multiball the Assist saucer is scripted not to miss — the single most reliable repeatable jackpot loop in the game.

    Reward: Current jackpot + auto-relights the next one -- the most reliable repeatable jackpot loop in the game

  • Goal → Ramp Jackpot-Relight Cycle combo · Medium

    The standard multiball climb — score the Goal jackpot, relight it with a ramp, repeat up all 15 ranked opponents.

    Reward: Jackpot for the current rank (USA 20M up to Germany 250M); beats that team and advances rank each cycle

  • Post-Germany Victory Laps combo · Medium

    Beat Germany (rank 1, 250M) and Goal/ramps become flat 50M Victory Lap shots — the best sustained per-shot value in the game.

    Reward: Flat 50M per shot (Goal/Left Ramp/Right Ramp) -- the best sustained per-shot value in the game

  • Goal Ultra-Farming Loop combo · Easy

    Outside multiball, repeatedly scoring and relighting Goal banks Ultra-mode points that pay out at end-of-ball bonus.

    Reward: 10M + starts/advances an Ultra mode each cycle; Ultra points bank into end-of-ball bonus (lost on tilt)

  • Multiball: Build & Lock multiball · Medium

    Fill all five BUILD panels, lock a ball on the left ramp, then start Multiball at the TV/Final Draw scoop.

    Reward: Locks the ball (jumps multiball starting rank by 4) and launches Multiball at the Final Draw scoop

  • Striker Scoop Multiball Backup multiball · Easy

    A random Striker Scoop award can start Multiball on the spot, but at the bottom of the rankings; separately, reaching your last ball with no multiball yet lights the Final Draw scoop on its own.

    Reward: Random Striker award — one possibility is Multiball starting at the lowest rank

  • Drain Recovery Resume multiball · Medium

    Draining mid-multiball before collecting a jackpot doesn't end it — a ramp or the Final Draw scoop resumes right where you left off.

    Reward: Resumes multiball at the rank where you left off; a later, separate multiball starts two ranks higher

  • Ultra Modes mode · Medium

    Four stacking sub-modes — Ramps, Spinner, Jets, Goalie — are awarded one per Goal scored and bank points into end-of-ball bonus.

    Reward: 5M/ramp, 1M/spin, 2M/pop, or 5M/goalie hit while active; each Ultra caps at 30M + a 10M bonus

  • TV Award: Big Goal Round mode · Medium

    Start a timed round at the TV/Final Draw scoop and hammer 3 escalating Goal shots before the clock runs out.

    Reward: 25M just for starting, then 15M + 15M + 30M for the three Goal shots landed within the timer

  • TV Award: Extra Ball Round mode · Medium

    A 20-second window to collect Extra Ball at the Striker Scoop, then keep milking it at 50M per additional shot.

    Reward: An Extra Ball, then 50M per additional Striker Scoop shot for the rest of the 20-sec timer

  • Boston Tea Party Hurry-Up mode · Medium

    A countdown hurry-up unlocked by traveling to Boston — starts at 40M and drains to a 10M floor if you wait too long.

    Reward: Hurry-up starting at 40M, counting down to a 10M floor if you wait

  • Packed Stands: 1M Jet Bumpers multiplier · Medium

    WCS94 has no true playfield multiplier — the closest thing is the jet bumpers jumping from 100K to a flat 1M per pop for the rest of the ball.

    Reward: Jet bumpers jump from 100K to a flat 1M per pop for the rest of the ball

  • Super Free Kick Escalator multiplier · Medium

    Light Super Free Kick from a Striker award, then chain hits on the Free Kick target — value climbs +5M per consecutive hit.

    Reward: 10M base, +5M per consecutive Free Kick target hit while Super Free Kick is active

  • Final Match Qualification wizard · Hard

    The full chain to unlock the World Cup Final — travel all 9 cities culminating in Los Angeles, then start it at the Final Draw scoop.

    Reward: Starts the Final Match wizard mode against Germany at the Final Draw scoop

  • Final Match: Beat Germany wizard · Hard

    A 45-second timed multiball against Germany — the trough keeps serving balls for you to plunge all round, each Goal worth 75M, and outscoring Germany pays a 500M bonus.

    Reward: 75M per Goal scored in 45 sec; beating Germany's total nets a 500M bonus (tie forces sudden-death overtime)

  • Beginner First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    A safe, concrete opening sequence off a fresh ball — free points, build progress, and a low-risk Goal shot.

    Reward: Free skill-shot points, a BUILD panel, and a safe goalie-bypass Goal shot for 10M + an Ultra mode

  • Best Opener: Build Toward Multiball Fast strategy · Medium

    Prioritize the 4 BUILD sources over risky direct Goal shots early — multiball is the game's primary score engine.

    Reward: Fills all 5 BUILD panels and locks the ball, jumping multiball starting rank by 4

  • Goal-Only Grinding (Safe Farm) strategy · Easy

    The lowest-risk high-score approach — skip multiball entirely and just grind Goal-relight-Goal, banking Ultra bonus every ball.

    Reward: 10M + Ultra-mode progress per cycle with zero lock/drain risk -- can rack tens to hundreds of millions purely from bonus

  • Light and Collect SPECIAL strategy · Hard

    Climbing to the #1 ranked team, Germany, lights SPECIAL in the right outlane -- the one time a right-outlane drain is what you want.

    Reward: SPECIAL award (typically a credit or replay, per operator settings) collected in the right outlane
