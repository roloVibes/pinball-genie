Transformers: More Than Meets the Eye

Stern Pinball, 2026 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Transformers: More Than Meets the Eye in the Pinball Genie app →

Designer Elliot Eismin (layout), code/rules Elizabeth Gieske. Released June 2026. Three editions — Pro / Premium / LE — with identical scoring; only physical toys differ (animatronic firing Megatron cannon, Soundwave physical ball lock, Grimlock sculpt, right-ramp diverter are premium-le). Playfield is split Autobots-left / Decepticons-right, three flippers including an upper-right flipper fed by the left orbit and plunge.

Core loops

  • Missions (5): Knock down the 3 drop targets in front of Megatron, then shoot the scoop/VUK to start. Each is ~60s with a final blow at Megatron. Missions: Autobot Run, Microbots, Solar Needle, Prime Target, Golden Lagoon. Each has Normal / Energized (easier, spend Energon) / Rusted (harder, higher scoring) variants. A final blow grants a 2x Shot-X on a random shot (pays when a Tech Spec is lit there).
  • Transformers Multiball: Shoot the center VUK to light/lock (6 VUK shots, 3 locks first MB). Shots cycle purple → red → flashing jackpot (3rd hit scores); lit jackpots multiply the next jackpot. Super jackpot at the side ramp after ~2 jackpots. Physical lock is premium-le; Pro uses virtual locks.
  • Combiner Multiball: Build 3 of 5 Combiners (5 parts each, from side ramp or matching Tech Specs), then shoot the space bridge. Cycle the spinner grid onto a completed Combiner for 8M→10M jackpots; super at side ramp + space bridge.
  • Tech Spec Mania: Collect 3 of each of 5 colors (15) to start a utility 3-ball MB; farms Combiner parts.
  • Dinobot Missions: Hit the 2 Grimlock standups, then left orbit on the orange arrow. War / Island (2-ball MB) / S.O.S.; 3 Dino Jackpots light Dinobot Desertion (all three → Super Dinobot Desertion).

Combos & multipliers

  • Cassette Combos: Roll the left inlane to light the Cassette target; chain right ramp → left ramp → Cassette target (3-way, base 3M) to qualify the action button for all Tech Specs lit 15s.
  • Autobot Run combo: center spinner → left ramp → right orbit → left orbit → side ramp (3x, 4x Rusted).
  • Bumblebee Playfield X: Hit the lit captive ball to spell BUMBLEBEE → 30s of 2x. Stack with multiball add-a-ball 2x (space bridge → captive ball) for 3x.
  • Bonus X: Advanced only via Mystery award (bumper → space bridge).

Skill shots

Soft-plunge to the upper flipper, then hit Optimus (500K/+5s), side ramp (5M/+8s), or space bridge (8M/+10s). Secret: catch on right flipper → left orbit → upper-flipper shot = 15M/+10s.

Wizard modes (code ~0.87)

Three mini-wizards top the ladder: One Shall Fall (play 2 missions → right ramp; 4-ball Megatron-vs-Optimus duel), Rescue Optimus (complete all 5 missions → VUK; 3 phases), Dinobot Desertion / Super Dinobot Desertion. No grand final wizard documented yet.

How Transformers: More Than Meets the Eye plays

Transformers puts the Autobot and Decepticon war under glass: run Megatron's five missions, assemble Combiners, and bring Optimus and Megatron to a head-to-head duel. Set each mission's difficulty with Energon, stack playfield multipliers into Transformers Multiball, and reach the mini-wizards One Shall Fall and Rescue Optimus.

The loop

  1. Knock down the three Megatron drop targets to open the scoop, scroll to a mission with the flippers, and start it -- spend or bank an Energon cube first to set that mission's difficulty and payout.
  2. Rip the center spinner to bank Energon cubes, and hit the color-coded standups to light nearby shots for the Tech Specs that feed the extra ball, Combiners, and your mission shot multipliers.
  3. Feed the center VUK to lock balls for Transformers Multiball, and spell BUMBLEBEE at the captive ball to arm 2x playfield before you start it.
  4. Play two missions to light One Shall Fall at the Right Ramp; clear all five instead and the VUK opens for Rescue Optimus, handing you an extra ball too.

Reading the lights

  • Megatron Drops & Mission Scoop (mode) — lit: The three drop targets sit lit in front of the Megatron scoop and block it; knocking them down opens the scoop, and they reset as the recurring toll on every new mission. · flashing: The scoop flashes when a mission is ready to start or a mission's final blow is lit. · then: Start your pick at the scoop, make its shots, then cash the final blow back at the scoop for a 2x shot multiplier; clearing all five missions lights the VUK for Rescue Optimus and an extra ball at the Space Bridge. · control: Either flipper button: with the scoop open, the flippers scroll the five missions so you start the one whose shots you are hitting cleanly, and spending or banking an Energon cube beforehand sets whether it runs Energized (easier, smaller) or Rusted (harder, far bigger) (drops cleared, scoop lit, no mission running)
  • BUMBLEBEE Playfield X (multiplier) — lit: The Bumblebee captive ball adds three BUMBLEBEE letters per lit hit and changes color as it climbs; if it has gone dark, two pop-bumper hits relight it. · then: Three lit hits complete the word and arm thirty seconds of 2x playfield -- start it just before a multiball, then shoot the Space Bridge for its once-a-game add-a-ball and bash the captive ball again within ten seconds to push the whole thing to 3x.
  • Tech Spec Standups (status) — lit: Hitting a color-coded standup lights the major shot beside it for a Tech Spec in that target's color, and the specs you bank hold as a running inventory across five circulating colors. · then: Collect fifteen specs in any mix to light the extra ball at the Space Bridge, three of every color to start Tech Spec Mania, and keep in mind a mission's final-blow 2x only pays while a spec is lit on that shot -- so neither system scores much without the other.
  • Transformers Multiball Locks (mode) — lit: The center VUK lights for a lock after its shots stack up, and each locked ball holds -- physically on Premium and LE, virtually on Pro -- until three are banked. · flashing: Major shots flash as they cycle purple to red to a flashing jackpot during the multiball. · then: Lock three to start Transformers Multiball, hit a major shot until it turns over for each jackpot, and cash the Super Jackpot at the Side Ramp for the combined base value of every jackpot you banked.
  • Combiner Parts & Grid (status) — lit: Each Combiner needs five parts, most coming off the Side Ramp, and a completed Combiner stays built for the rest of the game. · flashing: The Space Bridge flashes for Combiner Multiball once three of the five Combiners are built. · then: Build three Combiners to start at the Space Bridge, spin the grid onto a finished one for eight-to-ten-million jackpots, then finish the Side Ramp into the Space Bridge for a super that tacks on an extra ball, sized to the number of Combiners you had built going in. · control: Action button: inside Combiner Multiball, holding the button spins the Combiner grid -- land it on one you actually finished and jackpots light at the upper-flipper shots, the spinner, Megatron and the Right Ramp, while a half-built Combiner is just a slot the grid can waste itself on (during Combiner Multiball)
  • Cassette Combo & Button (shot arrows) — lit: Rolling the Left Inlane briefly lights the Cassette target, and chaining the Right Ramp into the Left Ramp into that lit target completes the three-way Cassette combo before the light dies. · then: Complete the combo, press the lit button, then cash the fifteen-second window at shots carrying a mission final-blow 2x -- the game's go-to repeatable scoring loop. · control: Action button: completing a Cassette combo lights the button -- press it to switch on every Tech Spec for fifteen seconds and double scoring at your completed mission shots (right after a Cassette three-way combo)
  • Inlane Tug-of-War (lanes) — lit: Once the Megatron drops are cleared the return lanes light a tug-of-war, and you pick a side by which lane you feed the ball through rather than by tapping a flipper. · then: Roll the Left Inlane twice without touching the right and the Left Ramp lights for a chain of pink hurry-up shots opening at fifteen million; feed the Right Inlane twice while sparing the left instead, and Starscream Frenzy lights over at the Right Orbit.
  • Grimlock & Dinobot Missions (mode) — lit: The two Grimlock standups flanking the Left Orbit gate the Dinobot missions, and hitting both lights the orbit's orange arrow to start one. · then: Shoot the lit Left Orbit on its orange arrow to start the selected Dino mission -- the hidden upper-left slingshot quietly cycles which one -- then score three Dino Jackpots back at the Left Orbit to complete it and qualify Dinobot Desertion.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Short-plunge to the upper flipper and take a skill shot -- Space Bridge pays the most of the standard three at eight million with a ten-second ball save, the back-door secret route fifteen. Clear the Megatron drops, start an easy mission like Autobot Run off the scoop, and take incidental VUK locks as they present. — Space Bridge Skill Shot, Beginner Safe Opener, Solar Needle
  • Building — Arm Bumblebee 2x at the captive ball, lock three balls at the VUK, and start Transformers Multiball; trigger the Space Bridge add-a-ball to reach 3x, light four jackpots, and cash the Side Ramp super inside that window. Build Combiners off the Side Ramp for their own multiball on the side. — Transformers Multiball, Bumblebee Playfield X, Add-a-Ball 3x Stack, Combiner Multiball, Super Jackpot Stack
  • The big one — Play two missions to light One Shall Fall at the Right Ramp, a four-ball Optimus-versus-Megatron duel whose payout you pre-load with banked Energon and rusted missions. Clear all five missions instead to light the VUK for Rescue Optimus -- a three-phase wizard that hands you an extra ball and ends in victory laps. — One Shall Fall, Rescue Optimus, Bank Energon and Stack Perks Before One Shall Fall

The Action Button on Transformers: More Than Meets the Eye

Center of the lockdown bar.

Two very different jobs. It cashes the reward you earn from cassette combos, and inside Combiner Multiball it is the steering wheel that decides which Combiner your jackpots come from.

  • After any cassette combo (Cassette target off a lit left inlane): The button lights; press it to turn on every Tech Spec for 15 seconds and double scoring at completed mission shots.
  • Combiner Multiball about to start: Press to shelve it — the multiball requalifies when your next Combiner finishes, so you can stack it better.
  • During Combiner Multiball: Hold it to cycle the Combiner grid; land on one you completed and jackpots light at the upper flipper shots, spinner, Megatron and right ramp.
  • While holding the button in that multiball: White build shots keep raising the jackpot value as the grid cycles.
  • After a Combiner super jackpot: Use it again to pick the next Combiner and restart the jackpot wave.

Goals in Transformers: More Than Meets the Eye

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Optimus Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    The safest of the three standard skill shots: short-plunge to the upper flipper and hit Optimus Prime for a quick, reliable ball-save extension.

    Reward: 500K + 5s ball save

  • Side Ramp Skill Shot skillshot · Medium

    The middle standard skill shot: short-plunge to the upper flipper and thread the Side Ramp — the steepest shot on the machine — for a bigger points-and-ball-save package than Optimus.

    Reward: 5M + 8s ball save

  • Space Bridge Skill Shot skillshot · Medium

    The best-paying standard skill shot: short-plunge to the upper flipper and hit Space Bridge for the top standard value and the longest ball-save extension.

    Reward: 8M + 10s ball save

  • Secret Skill Shot skillshot · Hard

    Highest-value opener: short-plunge but outrun the feed to catch on the lower-right flipper, shoot the Left Orbit past Grimlock to reach the upper flipper, then complete any of the three upper-flipper skill shots for a premium payout.

    Reward: 15M + 10s ball save at Space Bridge (Optimus/Side Ramp pay more than standard here too, but their secret-route values aren't published)

  • Cassette 3-Way Combo combo · Medium

    The flagship combo: light the Cassette target off the left inlane, then chain right ramp to left ramp to Cassette target to max the combo multiplier.

    Reward: Base 3M, +1x per consecutive shot (max 3-way)

  • Cassette Combo Tech Spec Money Loop combo · Hard

    The signature repeatable money loop: complete a Cassette 3-way to qualify the action button, light all Tech Specs for 15s, and cash them at your multiplied mission shots.

    Reward: Compounds combo multiplier with Tech Spec shot multipliers

  • Autobot Run 5-Shot Combo combo · Medium

    The most-farmed fixed combo string: a memorizable five-shot sequence worth 3x per shot (4x in the Rusted variant).

    Reward: 3x per shot (4x Rusted)

  • Prime Target Ramp Combo combo · Medium

    Combo the left and right ramps for 2x while bashing Optimus to raise base ramp value; 6 total ramp shots light the final blow.

    Reward: 2x ramp combos (climbing +1x in Rusted)

  • Autobot Hurry-Up combo · Medium

    Inlane tug-of-war qualifier: complete the Megatron drops, roll the left inlane twice without the right, then collect a chain of pink hurry-up shots at the left ramp.

    Reward: 3 pink shots at 15M (then 25M round 2)

  • Transformers Multiball multiball · Medium

    The scoring engine: light and lock at the center VUK (6 shots, 3 locks), then cycle shots purple to red to jackpot and cash a side-ramp super jackpot.

    Reward: Super jackpot = combined base value of prior jackpots

  • Combiner Multiball multiball · Hard

    Build 3 of 5 Combiners from side-ramp shots and Tech Specs, start at the space bridge, then cycle the spinner grid onto a completed Combiner to light 8-10M jackpots.

    Reward: 8M/9M/10M jackpots; 2x super + add-a-ball

  • Tech Spec Mania multiball · Hard

    Utility 3-ball multiball started by collecting 3 Tech Specs of all 5 colors; collect one of every color via captive-ball color changes to light a 5M super jackpot and farm Combiner parts.

    Reward: 5M side-ramp super jackpot; Combiner parts

  • Solar Needle mode · Medium

    Mission: hit two lit cyan standups to light the captive ball, then bank 3 lit captive-ball hits to light the final blow at Megatron.

    Reward: Mission completion + 2x Shot-X

  • Golden Lagoon mode · Medium

    Mission: build the center-spinner per-spin value by shooting the solidly-lit yellow ramps, then rip the spinner to collect 25M (60M Rusted) and light the final blow.

    Reward: Big spinner payout + 2x Shot-X

  • Microbots mode · Medium

    Mission: hit a ramp to raise a drop target, then hit the lit drop (avoid unlit); land 3 lit drop targets while ramps raise the multiplier to light the final blow.

    Reward: Mission completion + 2x Shot-X

  • Dinobot Missions mode · Medium

    Parallel track via the Grimlock standups gating the left orbit: start War / Island / S.O.S., score 3 Dino Jackpots at the left orbit to qualify Dinobot Desertion.

    Reward: 3 Dino Jackpots light Dinobot Desertion (500K each in bonus)

  • Bumblebee Playfield X multiplier · Medium

    Spell BUMBLEBEE at the captive ball (3 letters per lit hit) to start 30 seconds of 2x playfield scoring; arm it right before a multiball or big mission.

    Reward: 30s of 2x playfield scoring

  • Add-a-Ball 3x Stack multiplier · Hard

    The marquee multiplier stack: carry an active Bumblebee 2x into multiball, shoot the space bridge for add-a-ball, then hit the captive ball within 10s to reach 3x for 30s.

    Reward: 3x scoring for 30s during multiball

  • One Shall Fall wizard · Hard

    Mini-wizard qualified after playing 2 missions (right ramp lights): a 4-ball Megatron-vs-Optimus duel where purple shots damage Megatron and a final Megatron scoop scores the finishing hurry-up.

    Reward: ~600K x stored Energon Cubes + Rusted/final-blow bonuses

  • Rescue Optimus wizard · Hard

    Mini-wizard qualified by completing all five missions (lights the VUK and awards an extra ball): three ~70s phases centered on Optimus and the VUK.

    Reward: Extra ball + 3-phase wizard scoring

  • Tournament High-Score Opener strategy · Hard

    The high-score line: bank the skill shot, arm Bumblebee 2x, lock Transformers Multiball, then trigger add-a-ball 3x and cash a side-ramp super jackpot.

    Reward: Stacked 3x super jackpots

  • Beginner Safe Opener strategy · Easy

    A low-risk opening line: take the Space Bridge skill shot, cradle and survey, then start Autobot Run while taking incidental VUK locks.

    Reward: Steady points + mission progress

  • Super Jackpot Stack strategy · Hard

    The biggest-scoring play: layer Bumblebee 2x and add-a-ball 3x onto Transformers Multiball, light 4 jackpots, then collect the side-ramp super inside the multiplier window.

    Reward: Multiplied side-ramp super jackpot

  • Five-Mission Sweep strategy · Hard

    Route for clearing all five scoop missions in one game: reset the drops, pick the easiest remaining mission, use Optimus to buy back mission time, and always close with the final blow.

    Reward: Lights the VUK for Rescue Optimus plus an extra ball

  • Last-Ball Countdown mode · Medium

    Your one built-in reprieve: draining the right outlane on the last ball starts a short timer, and one right-ramp shot inside it puts a ball back in play.

    Reward: Ball returned to play

  • Energon and Cosmic Rust: Set Your Own Difficulty progression · Medium

    There are five missions and you decide the terms of each one before it starts. Spinner rips bank energon cubes; spending a cube makes the next mission easier and cheaper, while saving it lets you rust the mission after that into something much harder and much better paid. What changes is the actual objective, not just the payout.

    Reward: Control over both the difficulty and the point value of every mission you start

  • Tech Specs: The Five Colours Everything Else Runs On progression · Medium

    Almost every other system on this machine is paid for in tech specs. Standup targets sitting between the major shots light those shots for a spec in the target's colour, and what you bank buys an extra ball, a multiball, combiner parts, and the only conditions under which your mission shot multipliers are worth anything.

    Reward: Extra ball, Tech Spec Mania three-ball multiball, combiner parts, and live mission shot multipliers

  • Build Combiners, Then Spin for Your Jackpot progression · Hard

    Five combiners at five parts each, collected one at a time mostly off the side ramp. Three completed combiners open the multiball, and once inside it a spinning grid picks which combiner actually pays — so every one you left half-built is a slot that grid can land on and waste.

    Reward: Combiner Multiball with jackpots based at 8M for three combiners, rising to 10M with all five

  • Spell BUMBLEBEE, Then Take It to 3x progression · Medium

    The playfield multiplier comes off the captive ball three letters at a time and pays thirty seconds of doubled scoring. There is a route to triple inside a multiball, but the 2x has to already be running when that multiball begins.

    Reward: Thirty seconds of 2x scoring, upgradeable to 3x inside a multiball

  • Bank Energon and Stack Perks Before One Shall Fall progression · Medium

    The Optimus Prime and Megatron duel lights early and then simply waits for you. What it pays, and how survivable it is, depends on the energon you are still holding, how many missions you rusted, how many final blows you landed, and how many missions you played at all before cashing it in.

    Reward: A mini-wizard duel whose value and difficulty you set for yourself in advance
