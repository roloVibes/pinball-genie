The Machine: Bride of Pin·Bot

Williams, 1991 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open The Machine: Bride of Pin·Bot in the Pinball Genie app →

Designed by John Trudeau and Python Anghelo, code by Brian Eddy — Williams' sequel to 1986's Pin·Bot, ~8,100 units produced. The last Williams title with a segmented (non-DMD) score display. There is only one hardware edition; the sole variant is a 2014 third-party Dutch Pinball "2.0" ROM conversion on identical hardware, not covered here.

The core engine: the Shuttle Ramp and the rotating Face

The whole game is built around one progressive objective rather than modern named "modes." The Shuttle Ramp feeds a robot head unit whose face physically rotates through four states. Shooting the ramp when it's unlit just diverts the ball into the Pin·Bot mini-playfield (a repeating 100K-600K scoring ladder that lights an Extra Ball every 7th entry) and returns it to the plunger. Shooting it when lit advances the Face:

  • Activate Voice (Face 1→2) — 250K
  • 1st Eye (Face 2) — 250K, launches a second ball into play
  • 2nd Eye (Face 2) — 1M, starts 2-ball Multiball, Face becomes 3

Multiball and Metamorphosis

All scoring doubles during multiball (except Big Wheel awards, Jackpot, and Power Charge). The Face keeps progressing through multiball via more Shuttle Ramp shots, which now lock balls:

  • Face 3, 1st shot — Human Influx (500K), locks a ball
  • Face 3, 2nd shot — Metamorphosis (1.5M), releases both balls, Face becomes 4 (the Bride's human face)
  • Face 4, 1st shot — Increased Pulse (500K), locks a ball
  • Face 4, 2nd shot — 2M, releases both balls, awards a Big Wheel spin

The Big Wheel has six outcomes (10M / 50M / Special / 5M / 1M / Lights 1 Billion) — Billion must be collected via the Heartbeat Ramp within 12 seconds or it's lost. During multiball, shooting the Left Lane builds Power Charge (100K per shot, capped at 1M, not doubled).

Loops, ramps, and combos

The Left Loop, Right Loop, and Heartbeat Ramp each carry their own escalating shot values (25K→500K for the loops, 100K→1M for the ramp) and all three feed one shared combo counter: any 4 loop/ramp shots within roughly 3-4 seconds of each other score a 4-combo (500,000 pts), and every shot beyond that (5 through 9) is worth a flat 1,000,000. A 10-combo scores the secret "Backdoor Billion" — an alternate route to a billion points entirely outside the Big Wheel, though it's exceptionally hard (on the order of 49+ consecutive shots without a drain) and still counts against the game's Billionaire's Club award cap. The Left Loop additionally lights an Extra Ball at its 500K tier; the Right Loop contributes toward lighting Jackpot (4 shots) and auto-advances the bonus multiplier on every exit pass.

Jackpot, Small Wheel, Jets, and the standups

Jackpot (1M-8M, built mainly from Jets hits) is lit by a Small Wheel "Light Jackpot" award or four Right Loop shots, then must be collected via the Left Lane within 20 seconds. The Small Wheel (spun via the Left Lane, outside multiball, when Jackpot isn't lit) awards one of six prizes including Extra Ball. Jets pay a flat 10K/hit but roughly 50 hits advance a separate tier-award ladder. Three standup targets (one left, two right) advance the bonus multiplier when all three are completed.

Multipliers

The skill-shot multiplier increases +1x every time the ball returns to the plunger lane (Pin·Bot return, a released lock, a new extra ball). It persists within a ball — including any extra balls played on that same ball-in-play — and resets to 1x only at the true start of each new ball; it can run into the high teens or 20s+ within a long ball. The bonus multiplier climbs via the standup targets and free Right Loop advances, and is applied to the end-of-ball bonus (base = loop shots that ball × 10,000).

Extra balls and wizard-level scoring

Extra balls come from the Left Loop (4x quick shots), the Small Wheel, every 7th Pin·Bot entry, and a mercy "WIMP" award for a struggling game; a practical stacking cap sits around 4 EBs. There's no formal named wizard mode in the original ROM — instead there are two independent routes to a billion points: the Big Wheel Billion (full Face 1→4 double-lock progression, then a lucky wheel spin collected on the Heartbeat Ramp) and the Backdoor Billion (a 10-loop combo chain). Both post to a separate ~19-20 slot Billionaire's Club high-score table.

Strategy in one line

Take the skill shot every ball, rush the Shuttle Ramp to reach multiball fast, farm the Left Loop for Extra Ball insurance, then park on the Heartbeat Ramp during multiball for safe, doubled, repeatable points — and avoid the Small Wheel lane and cold Right Loop shots — both are low-value drain risks.

How The Machine: Bride of Pin·Bot plays

The Machine has taken a bride and you are driving her transformation. Advance the rotating face through its stages to reach the head's multiball, then chase a billion points either off the Big Wheel or by chaining loops the back way.

The loop

  1. Plunge into the top lanes for a skill shot, and know that every trip back to the shooter ratchets its multiplier up for the rest of the ball.
  2. Feed the Shuttle Ramp off the right flipper to walk the face from Voice to First Eye to Second Eye, which opens a two-ball multiball and doubles everything.
  3. During multiball, lock both balls at the mouth to trigger Metamorphosis, turn the face human, and set up a spin of the Big Wheel.
  4. Chain combos across the two loops and the center ramp, backhanding and rebound-catching to keep long strings alive.
  5. Cash the Big Wheel's billion on the center ramp, or take the back door by chaining ten loops in a row without a break.

Reading the lights

  • Top Skill-Shot Lanes (status) — lit: Each of the five top lanes carries a pocket value for a plunged ball scored before a flipper ever touches it, and the skill-shot multiplier riding on them steps up one every time the ball comes back to the shooter. · then: Plunge a lane for its value times the current multiplier, then get the ball back to the shooter by any means -- a mini-playfield return, a released lock, a fresh extra ball -- to ratchet that multiplier before your next pull. It holds the whole ball and drops back only when a new ball begins.
  • The Machine's Face (ladder) — lit: The Shuttle Ramp is the single shot that turns the head, and each lit ramp steps the face forward one stage -- Activate Voice, First Eye, Second Eye -- with a stage you reach staying reached. · then: Take Second Eye to open two-ball multiball with everything doubled, then double-lock at the mouth for Metamorphosis to turn her human, and double-lock once more to earn a wheel spin. Ramp shots alternate between the head and the bagatelle, so count on a couple of spare tries.
  • Big Wheel (status) — lit: Two double-locks at the final face state free the balls and hand you a spin of the motorized prize wheel; your ball never strikes it, the wheel simply resolves to one of its six awards. · then: Hope the wheel stops on Lights 1 Billion, then fire the center ramp inside its short collect window to bank the billion. The other five faces still pay handsomely, from a million on up.
  • Loops & Center Ramp (shot arrows) — lit: The two side loops and the center ramp all feed one shared combo counter and their own value ladders, and four quick left-loop shots also light an extra ball. · then: Chain any mix of the three inside the timing window for the four-combo, then keep it alive -- each shot past four pays a flat million. Rebound-catch the right loop on the left flipper and backhand the center ramp to stack strings, all the way to the ten-in-a-row Backdoor Billion.
  • Jackpot & Jets (status) — lit: The pop bumpers quietly swell the Jackpot's value as you play, four Right Loop shots (or a Small Wheel award) light the Jackpot, and once it is lit the Left Lane becomes the collect. · then: Light the Jackpot up the Right Loop, then cash it up the Left Lane before its window shuts for a payout fattened by every jet hit. The bumpers climb their own tier ladder while they feed the pot.
  • Small Wheel Award (status) — lit: Away from multiball and with the Jackpot dark, a Left Lane shot ducks the ball through a tunnel to the Small Wheel, which traps it and spins an under-playfield wheel for a random prize. · then: Take the spin for whatever it lands on -- a lit Jackpot, points, or an extra ball outright -- and it kicks the ball back to you. It is the cheapest source of an extra ball on the machine when the wheel is generous.
  • Pin-Bot Mini-Playfield (ladder) — lit: When the Pin-Bot shot is lit in place of the face, a Shuttle Ramp shot drops the ball onto the raised bagatelle for a repeating points ladder. · then: Let the pins score you up the rungs; every seventh trip up here lights another extra ball, and the ball comes back to the shooter, which bumps your skill-shot multiplier on the way.
  • Bonus Multiplier Standups (multiplier) — lit: The lone left standup and the stacked right pair light as the set that steps your end-of-ball bonus multiplier up a notch once all three have fallen. · then: Complete all three standups to advance the multiplier, and steal a free advance any time a ball rolls far enough back up the Right Loop to trip the switch at its end. That multiplier rides your loop-heavy bonus when the ball ends.

Lane change: the flipper buttons move the lit lane. The bottom return lanes double as your extra-ball collector: with an extra ball stacked, tap either flipper button to slide the lit return lane under the incoming ball and take it. A Heartbeat Ramp return drops in below that switch, so it cannot grab one -- feed the collect off a different shot.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Plunge the top lanes for the skill shot, then pound the Shuttle Ramp off the right flipper to rush the face toward Second Eye and two-ball multiball. Farm four quick Left Loop shots for a cheap extra ball whenever it sits dark. — Skill Shot, Best Opener — First 30 Seconds, Left Loop Extra Ball Combo
  • Building — Open two-ball multiball at Second Eye with scoring doubled, double-lock through Metamorphosis, and grind the center ramp cradle-and-fire for its doubled value. Stack extra balls off the loop, the mini-playfield and the Small Wheel, then collect the lit ones by tapping a flipper to slide the lit bottom return lane under the ball. — 2-Ball Multiball — Second Eye, Metamorphosis Double Lock, Heartbeat Ramp Backhand Grind, Extra Ball Stacking Insurance, Power Charge Ladder
  • The big one — A billion points is the summit, reachable two ways. Walk the face through both of its multiball double-locks to earn a wheel spin and land that spin's Lights 1 Billion on the center ramp, or ignore the wheel and string ten loops in a row for the Backdoor Billion. — Big Wheel Billion, Backdoor Billion, Big Wheel Jackpot Spin

Does The Machine: Bride of Pin·Bot have an action button?

This one is pure early-90s Williams: two flipper buttons and a manual shooter rod, nothing else. The one place your buttons do something off-playfield is the bottom return lanes — tap a flipper to slide the lit lane under an incoming ball and collect a stacked Extra Ball.

Goals in The Machine: Bride of Pin·Bot

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    Plunge into one of the five top lanes before the ball touches a flipper for near-free points, scored at the current skill-shot multiplier.

    Reward: 25K-200K lane value x current skill-shot multiplier

  • 4-Loop Combo combo · Medium

    Chain four shots into any mix of the Left Loop, Right Loop, or Heartbeat Ramp within about 3-4 seconds for a 500,000-point combo.

    Reward: 500K 4-combo score + callout

  • Loop Combo Escalation combo · Hard

    After the four-loop combo the bar goes up: the next combo needs five loops in a row, then six, then seven, and every combo above the four pays a flat 1,000,000. Working all the way up to a ten-loop combo is what sets up the Backdoor Billion attempt.

    Reward: 1M per combo above the four-loop — each one needs one more loop in a row than the last

  • Left Loop Extra Ball Combo combo · Medium

    Four consecutive quick shots up the Left Loop light an Extra Ball, the cheapest repeatable EB in the game.

    Reward: 500K on the 4th quick shot + lights an Extra Ball (once per ball)

  • Heartbeat Ramp Backhand Grind combo · Medium

    Repeat center-ramp shots, ideally backhanded off the right flipper, for a fast-ramping value and an easy way to keep a loop combo alive.

    Reward: 100K-1M ramp value ladder; ~2M/shot when multiball is active

  • Right Loop -> Left Flipper Rebound combo · Hard

    The core physical technique for stacking loop combos: shoot the right loop, backhand-catch the return on the left flipper, and re-fire it repeatedly without needing to alternate shots.

    Reward: Keeps the loop combo alive for 5-6 shots in a row, each worth 1M past the 4-combo tier

  • 2-Ball Multiball — Second Eye multiball · Medium

    Three Face hits progress The Machine through Activate Voice, First Eye and Second Eye, which starts 2-ball multiball — but ramp shots alternate between the Pin·Bot area and the Face, so expect roughly five to six Shuttle Ramp shots to get there.

    Reward: 250K + 250K then 1M starts 2-ball Multiball with all scoring doubled

  • Metamorphosis Double Lock multiball · Medium

    During multiball, lock both balls at the Shuttle Ramp back-to-back to complete Metamorphosis and advance the Face to its final human state.

    Reward: 500K then 1.5M (both doubled), advances Face to #4 and releases both locked balls

  • Big Wheel Jackpot Spin multiball · Medium

    Two more Shuttle Ramp locks at Face #4 release the balls and award a spin of the Big Wheel for a shot at Lights-1-Billion.

    Reward: 500K then 2M (doubled), releases both balls + awards a Big Wheel spin

  • Power Charge Ladder multiball · Medium

    While multiball is running, repeated Left Lane shots build Power Charge from 100K up to a 1M cap, undoubled by the multiball 2x.

    Reward: 100K per shot, capping at 1M; not doubled by the multiball 2x

  • Pin·Bot Mini-Playfield Ladder mode · Easy

    A Shuttle Ramp shot taken while the Pin·Bot shot is lit routes the ball into the mini-playfield for a repeating scoring ladder that lights an Extra Ball every seventh entry.

    Reward: 100K-600K repeating ladder per entry; every 7th entry lights an Extra Ball

  • Small Wheel Spin mode · Easy

    Shoot the Left Lane, outside multiball and when Jackpot isn't lit, to spin the Small Wheel for one of six random awards.

    Reward: Random award: Light Jackpot, 250K, Extra Ball, LIGHT Extra Ball, 50K, or 100K

  • Jets Level Tiers mode · Easy

    Repeated pop-bumper hits build toward escalating tier awards even though each individual hit only pays a flat 10K.

    Reward: 10K per hit; ~50 hits advances the 25K-500K Jets tier ladder

  • Jackpot: Light and Collect mode · Medium

    Light Jackpot via the Small Wheel or four Right Loop shots, then collect it up the Left Lane within a 20-second window.

    Reward: 1M-8M Jackpot, value built by Jets hits

  • Bonus Multiplier multiplier · Easy

    Complete all three standup targets to bump the bonus multiplier, and pick up a free advance any time the ball travels far enough backwards into the Right Loop's exit to trip the switch at the end.

    Reward: +1 step on the bonus multiplier, applied to end-of-ball bonus (loop shots x10,000 base)

  • Skill-Shot Multiplier multiplier · Easy

    Every time the ball returns to the plunger lane, from Pin·Bot, a released lock, or a new extra ball, the skill-shot multiplier ticks up 1x; it persists within a ball (including extra balls) and resets to 1x only at the true start of each new ball.

    Reward: +1x skill-shot multiplier; persists the whole ball (can reach 20x+) until the next ball starts

  • Big Wheel Billion wizard · Hard

    The primary route to a billion points: complete the full 4-stage Face/Metamorphosis progression through two double-locks, spin the Big Wheel, and cash a 'Lights 1 Billion' result on the Heartbeat Ramp.

    Reward: Big Wheel spin: 10M / 50M / Special / 5M / 1M, or Lights 1 Billion if collected on the Heartbeat Ramp within 12s

  • Backdoor Billion wizard · Hard

    A secret alternate route to a billion points entirely outside the Big Wheel: chain a 10-loop combo through the Left Loop, Right Loop, and Heartbeat Ramp without breaking the chain.

    Reward: Scores the Backdoor Billion (a billion points) outright, independent of the Big Wheel; counts toward the Billionaire's Club

  • Best Opener — First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    The concrete beginner sequence: take the skill shot, then hammer the Shuttle Ramp to rush toward 2-ball multiball rather than diverting to jets or the right loop early.

    Reward: Banked skill-shot points + fast, low-risk progress to 2-ball Multiball (Second Eye)

  • Extra Ball Stacking Insurance strategy · Medium

    Bank extra balls early from every available source so you can play out drains as free continuations instead of restarting your Shuttle Ramp progression.

    Reward: Extra Balls stacked as insurance, each collected at the lit bottom return lane — use the flipper buttons to move the lit lane under the ball. Note the Heartbeat Ramp returns the ball below the return-lane switch, so it cannot collect one. (Add a 'Bottom Lane Change / Return Lanes' shot to the shots array to anchor this step.)

  • Tournament Line — Shuttle Ramp to Big Wheel to Heartbeat Grind strategy · Medium

    What competitive players actually optimize for: reach multiball/Big Wheel as fast as possible, then park on the Heartbeat Ramp for doubled, low-risk repeated points rather than chasing riskier shots.

    Reward: Fastest path to the Big Wheel + sustained ~2M/shot doubled Heartbeat Ramp grinding for a low-risk high score
