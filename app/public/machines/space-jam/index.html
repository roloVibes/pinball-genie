Space Jam

Sega Pinball, 1996 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Space Jam in the Pinball Genie app →

Space Jam is a single-edition 1996 Sega Pinball machine (Whitestar hardware, ~800 units built) tied to the Michael Jordan / Looney Tunes film. There is no Pro/Premium/LE split — that convention didn't exist until Stern introduced it around 2016 — so this is the complete ruleset for the one production run. Two flippers, no upper/mini flipper, and a fixed center post between the flippers that explicitly blocks death-save recoveries.

Layout

Clockwise from the drain: SPACE standup bank (lower-left) → Left Orbit → Captive Ball → Left Ramp (a narrow, low-percentage jump-shot, not a true loop) → pop bumper cluster (3) with the Super Duper Jackpot standup dead-center at the back → Monstars drop targets (3) below the pops → Wabbit Hole saucer (center) → Jump Shot lock lane (center-right, the single most important shot in the game) → 23 targets and Right Ramp → Right Orbit → JAM standup bank (lower-right) → shooter lane, feeding a mini basketball hoop ("The Basket") used for the skill shot. Four in/outlanes double as the SLAM lane set.

Skill Shot

While the ball sits in the shooter lane, the DMD cycles an award (flipper buttons change selection): 300,000 points, Lite Lock, Wabbit Hole, or Super Pops. Shoot into the basket to bank it; a miss still scores 50,000. Both flippers held = auto-plunge, still credited. Making the basket with 300k/Lite Lock/Super Pops selected, then hitting the left ramp within a few seconds, adds a Super Skill Shot bonus of +10 basket points.

Multiball

Locks are built at the Jump Shot lane. The very first hit of the game lights all three locks simultaneously — a huge head start — so three more Jump Shot hits lock every ball and launch Multiball (four total Jump Shot hits: one to light all locks, three more to lock balls 1, 2, and 3 and start Multiball). Each subsequent multiball raises the requirement: the 2nd needs one dedicated shot per lock, and each one after that adds another shot, capping at five. Once running, Multiball plays out as four escalating jackpot waves that loop: (1) both ramps lit for Double Jackpot, (2) both orbits plus Jump Shot lit for Jackpot, (3) captive ball for Super Jackpot (base 3,000,000), (4) right ramp lights the center Super Duper Jackpot target for a 15-second window (base 5,000,000, re-lightable by re-shooting the ramp). Base jackpot is 500,000, and every switch hit registered along the way adds 1,000 to the eventual payout — the game's core "let it build before you cash" mechanic.

The Seven Planets (wizard qualification)

Seven objectives light lamps toward the wizard mode: SLAM (clear all 4 SLAM lanes once), IN YER FACE (3 captive-ball hits, unlocked by knocking the Monstars drops twice), FIRE (4 right-ramp shots), SPACE JAM (clear both SPACE and JAM banks, which also starts the 45-second Superjam mode), REBOUND (25 pop bumper hits), FAST BREAK (5 shots to each orbit, after which the right ramp becomes an escalating 200k→700k shot), and SUPER JACKPOT (score one during regular Multiball). Lighting all seven and shooting the left ramp starts Final Jam — a 60-second, all-5-balls-in-play wizard mode where every switch hit anywhere adds 10,000 to a running pot and every basket make collects the full accumulated value before it resets and keeps building. This is the sole terminal wizard mode.

Wabbit Hole

Lit by clearing both red "23" standups beside the right ramp. Hitting it shows three of a pool of possible awards and auto-selects one; awards stack and carry across balls until collected. Confirmed pool includes flat points, 1-on-1 and 2-on-2 mini-multiballs, Video Mode, a drop-target Defence hurry-up, Super Pops, and Full Court Frenzy — a rare 5-ball multiball with all six arrow shots lit, an escalating value, and an unusually long ball-save, the single biggest scoring opportunity in the game.

Bonus Multiplier

The only multiplier is Bonus X, advanced by completing all four SLAM lanes (the in/outlanes double-duty as letter lanes). It caps at 6X, and reaching it lights a free Extra Ball at the Jump Shot — good synergy since Jump Shot is already the priority shot all game. End-of-ball bonus is 10,000 per basket/orbit/ramp/Jump Shot switch made that ball, times Bonus X, plus a flat bonus per planet lit.

Basket Points (secondary track)

A separate scoring ladder running alongside normal points: captive ball = 2, left ramp = 3, left ramp during FIRE or as a Super Skill Shot = 10. 23 basket points banks 300,000; 45 points grants a direct Extra Ball.

Strategy in brief

There is no dedicated "combo" system (no combo meter/timer/named combo awards — this predates that design convention), but the game has clear repeatable money sequences: the jackpot wave loop, the Superjam chain, and Fast Break's ramping right-ramp value. The dominant strategy is to spam the Jump Shot almost exclusively pre-multiball (it's the highest-percentage, most important shot and the direct route to locks — four total hits: light all locks, then lock balls 1, 2, and 3 to start Multiball), picking up SLAM/SPACE/JAM/pop-bumper progress only incidentally, then broaden out to the full ramp/orbit/captive-ball jackpot geometry once Multiball is actually running and those shots pay real jackpot value. The left ramp is a low-percentage, hard-to-make shot and should not be chased early. No forgiving ball-save exists beyond the skill shot, and the fixed center post removes the classic death-save recovery, so aggressive nudging and rushed shots (especially under the Super Duper Jackpot's 15-second countdown) are genuine drain risks.

How Space Jam plays

Space Jam (Sega, 1996) turns the table into a game of hoops against the Monstars. Light the seven planets to reach Final Jam, and run the four-wave jackpot multiball.

The loop

  1. Tap a flipper to scroll the pre-plunge skill-shot award you want, then launch into the mini hoop to bank it.
  2. Hammer the Jump Shot lane -- the game's very first Jump Shot lights every lock at once, so four makes in all start Multiball.
  3. Once multiball is running, follow the four jackpot waves in their fixed order and let switch hits inflate each payout.
  4. Satisfy the seven planet objectives spread across the playfield, from SLAM in the lanes to a Super Jackpot in multiball.
  5. With all seven planets lit, shoot the left ramp to start Final Jam.

Reading the lights

  • Skill Shot Basket (status) — lit: The mini hoop beside the shooter lane is the skill-shot target, and its display cycles four awards -- three hundred thousand, Lite Lock, Wabbit Hole, or Super Pops. · then: Cycle to Lite Lock to feed multiball, then plunge the hoop to bank it. With points, Lite Lock, or Super Pops banked, a quick left-ramp shot within the window adds bonus basket points on top. · control: Either flipper button: a flipper tap while the ball waits in the shooter lane scrolls the display to the award you want, and holding both flipper buttons hands the plunge to the machine while still crediting that award (before you launch)
  • Jump Shot Lock Lane (shot arrows) — lit: The raised Jump Shot lane matters more than any other shot here; the game's very first Jump Shot lights every lock at once, and it stays a jackpot shot during multiball. · then: Make it four times to lock all three balls and launch Multiball, and treat it as home base by returning every rebound here. Reaching six-times bonus later opens a free extra ball at this same lane.
  • Jackpot Waves (ladder) — lit: Multiball jackpots light in four fixed waves: both ramps for a Double Jackpot, then both orbits and the Jump Shot for the Jackpot trio, then the captive ball for the three-million Super Jackpot, then the right ramp arms the center target. · flashing: The center Super Duper Jackpot target flashes for a fifteen-second window once the right ramp arms it; re-shoot the right ramp to relight it if that window runs out. · then: Rack up spare switch hits as you go to swell every payout, then take the five-million Super Duper target last -- carefully, since a fixed center post leaves no room for a death save. Collecting it restarts the loop at wave one.
  • SPACE & JAM Banks (letters) — lit: The SPACE bank on the lower left and the JAM bank on the lower right stay lit until spelled; completing both lights Superjam and satisfies the SPACE JAM planet. · then: Superjam is a forty-five-second chain -- hit every jackpot-geography shot once for a climbing award that opens at fifty thousand and adds a hundred thousand per new shot.
  • Monstars Drops (IN YER FACE) (mode) — lit: Knock down the three Monstars drop targets; a second full clear of the bank sets off IN YER FACE and opens the captive ball for a thirty-second window. · then: Rattle the captive ball up to three times at a hundred fifty thousand each to finish the mode and satisfy the IN YER FACE planet.
  • Right Ramp (FIRE & Fast Break) (shot arrows) — lit: Four right-ramp shots spell FIRE and supercharge the main basket to ten points a shot for twenty-three seconds; five shots to each orbit instead qualify Fast Break and turn the right ramp into a rising two-hundred-to-seven-hundred-thousand shot. · then: Spell FIRE and unload on the main basket, or qualify Fast Break and hammer the right ramp -- each clears its own planet toward Final Jam.
  • Wabbit Hole (status) — lit: Down both red 23 targets by the right ramp to light the Wabbit Hole saucer; diving in draws one of a dozen stacked mystery awards, and any you do not collect carry over ball to ball. · then: Hope for Full Court Frenzy, a five-ball multiball with all six arrows lit behind a long ball save, or the smaller 2 on 2 Multiball -- the saucer also hides a ball-catch mini-game and a Jordan trivia quiz.
  • SLAM Lanes & Pops (lanes) — lit: Rolling all four in-and-outlanes spells SLAM to step the bonus multiplier up, and lifetime pop-bumper hits grind toward the REBOUND planet. · then: You can only spell SLAM in single-ball play -- not in a mode, not in a multiball -- so wrap the lanes up early; reaching six-times bonus opens a free extra ball over at the Jump Shot lane, and enough pop hits clears REBOUND.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Cycle the skill shot to Lite Lock and bank it at the hoop, then treat the Jump Shot as home base -- its opening hit lights every lock, so four makes is the quickest multiball here. Pick up SLAM, SPACE and JAM only as they come to you. — Basket Skill Shot, First Lock Multiball, Beginner First 30 Seconds
  • Building — Work the seven planet objectives -- FIRE at the right ramp, IN YER FACE off the Monstars drops, REBOUND in the pops, Fast Break through the orbits -- and light the Wabbit Hole off the 23 targets for a shot at Full Court Frenzy. — FIRE, IN YER FACE, FAST BREAK, Wabbit Hole Double Dip
  • The big one — Light all seven planets and start Final Jam at the left ramp: a sixty-second, five-ball finale where every switch builds a running pot and each basket make collects it. Underneath it, the four-wave jackpot loop ending in the five-million Super Duper Jackpot is the multiball payoff. — Light the Seven Planets, Final Jam, Full Jackpot Wave Loop

Does Space Jam have an action button?

No cabinet button beyond the basics — a spring plunger, two flipper buttons, start. The flippers carry the extra duties: they scroll the skill shot award while the ball waits in the lane, holding both of them hands the plunge over to the machine (and still credits the award), and they pick your answer in the trivia mode.

Goals in Space Jam

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Basket Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    Cycle the DMD-selected award while the ball sits in the shooter lane, then plunge into the mini basketball hoop to bank it — the safest guaranteed points or progress of the ball.

    Reward: Banks the DMD-selected award — 300K, Lite Lock, Wabbit Hole, or Super Pops

  • Super Skill Shot Follow-Up skillshot · Medium

    Immediately after banking a basket skill shot with 300k, Lite Lock, or Super Pops selected, follow up with the left ramp for bonus basket points.

    Reward: +10 basket points added on top of the banked skill-shot award, if the left ramp lands within the window

  • Full Jackpot Wave Loop combo · Hard

    The core money sequence once multiball is running — four escalating jackpot waves that loop, with switch hits along the way inflating each payout.

    Reward: Double Jackpot + Jackpot trio + Super Jackpot (3M) + Super Duper Jackpot (5M), then the loop restarts at wave 1

  • Superjam Chain combo · Hard

    Light every SPACE and JAM target to trigger Superjam, then chain every jackpot shot plus the captive ball before the 45-second timer runs out.

    Reward: 50,000 for the first shot, 150,000 for the second, and an extra 100,000 for each shot after — every lit shot counts once inside the 45-second timer

  • Fast Break Ramp Rush combo · Medium

    Complete both orbits five times each to qualify Fast Break, then hammer the right ramp while its value climbs from 200k to a 700k cap.

    Reward: Right ramp value climbs from 200,000 to a 700,000 cap per hit once Fast Break is qualified

  • SPACE JAM Target Combo combo · Medium

    The lower-playfield bookend banks — clear SPACE on the left, then JAM on the right, to light Superjam and satisfy one of the Seven Planets.

    Reward: Lights Superjam (the 45-second jackpot chain) and satisfies the SPACE JAM planet

  • Wabbit Hole Double Dip combo · Medium

    Light the Wabbit Hole by clearing both 23 targets, then dive in for a stacked mystery award — with a shot at drawing Full Court Frenzy, the biggest scoring opportunity in the game.

    Reward: A stacked mystery award carried ball-to-ball until collected; chance at Full Court Frenzy or 2 on 2 multiball

  • First Lock Multiball multiball · Easy

    The fastest route to multiball — the first Jump Shot hit lights all three locks at once, so three more hits lock every ball and launch it.

    Reward: Locks all 3 balls and starts Multiball in just 4 total Jump Shot hits

  • Repeat Multiball (Escalating Locks) multiball · Hard

    Every multiball after the first raises the bar — each lock now needs its own dedicated Jump Shot hit, with the requirement climbing by one shot per replay up to a cap of five.

    Reward: Starts another Multiball, with the per-lock shot requirement rising each time up to a 5-shot cap

  • Full Court Frenzy multiball · Hard

    A Wabbit Hole mystery-award multiball — all six arrow shots light at once, five balls launch, and value climbs fast behind an unusually long ball-save.

    Reward: 5-ball multiball with all six arrow shots lit at 200,000 + 100 per switch hit, protected by an extended ball-save

  • 2 on 2 Multiball multiball · Medium

    A smaller two-ball Wabbit Hole award — right ramp or Jump Shot pay 100k and briefly light the left ramp for a bigger 300k grab.

    Reward: 2-ball multiball; Jump Shot or right ramp pays 100,000 and lights the left ramp for 300,000 within 10 seconds

  • IN YER FACE mode · Medium

    Knock down the Monstars drop targets twice to light the captive ball for a fast three-hit burst.

    Reward: 100,000 on the first drop-bank clear, then up to 450,000 (150,000 x3 captive-ball hits) and the IN YER FACE planet

  • FIRE mode · Medium

    Four right-ramp shots spell FIRE and temporarily supercharge the basket into a 10-points-per-shot machine.

    Reward: Basket worth 10 basket points per shot for 23 seconds; satisfies the FIRE planet

  • REBOUND mode · Easy

    A passive grind — 25 total pop bumper hits satisfies the REBOUND planet, no active shot-calling required.

    Reward: Satisfies the REBOUND planet after 25 lifetime pop-bumper hits

  • FAST BREAK mode · Medium

    Five shots to each orbit qualifies Fast Break, converting the right ramp into a ramping 200k-to-700k moneymaker.

    Reward: Satisfies the FAST BREAK planet and turns the right ramp into a 200,000-700,000 escalating shot

  • Bonus X via SLAM Lanes multiplier · Easy

    Roll through all four SLAM lanes to advance the end-of-ball Bonus multiplier, capping at 6X and unlocking a free Extra Ball at Jump Shot.

    Reward: +1 Bonus X step (caps at 6X); 6X lights a free Extra Ball at Jump Shot, then 200,000 flat per completion after

  • Super Pops Skill Shot multiplier · Easy

    Cycle the pre-plunge skill shot to Super Pops and bank it to boost pop bumper scoring value for the rest of the ball.

    Reward: Raises pop bumper scoring value for the rest of the ball

  • Light the Seven Planets wizard · Hard

    The full qualification path to Final Jam — light all seven planets, then shoot the left ramp to start the game's sole wizard mode.

    Reward: Lights all 7 planets and starts Final Jam, the game's sole wizard mode

  • Final Jam wizard · Hard

    The 60-second, 5-ball wizard mode — every switch hit builds a running award value, and every basket make collects it.

    Reward: Collects the full running pot (+10,000 per switch hit) on every basket make across the 60-second, 5-ball finale

  • Beginner First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    The lowest-risk opener for a new player — bank the skill shot into Lite Lock, then treat Jump Shot as home base until multiball starts.

    Reward: Banks a low-risk skill-shot award (Lite Lock feeds Multiball) plus steady Jump Shot progress toward locks

  • Best Opener – Jump Shot Rush strategy · Medium

    The documented tournament approach — spam Jump Shot exclusively until multiball starts, ignoring the low-percentage left ramp entirely.

    Reward: Zero-risk skill-shot award, then the fastest route to Multiball (4 Jump Shot hits), skipping the low-percentage left ramp

  • Multiball Priority Shot Order strategy · Medium

    Once multiball is live, follow the fixed jackpot wave order and let switch hits inflate value before collecting the two biggest jackpots.

    Reward: Double Jackpot + Jackpot + Super Jackpot (3M) + Super Duper Jackpot (5M), inflated by extra switch hits banked first

  • League and Wizard Mode — Set Them at Start secret · Easy

    Holding a flipper down while you press Start puts this machine into one of two settings that nothing on the playfield or display ever advertises. It is a one-time choice made in the instant before Start is pressed, and it cannot be set once play has begun.

    Reward: Opens the game straight into League or Wizard mode. League changes the Wabbit Hole: every player draws from one shared set of awards rather than each getting their own

  • Wabbit Hole Video Mode: Catch the Balls secret · Medium

    Hiding in the Wabbit Hole's pool of random prizes is a real mini-game rather than a points award. You move a basket along the bottom of the display and try to catch every ball thrown at you by Michael Jordan and four Looney Tunes characters.

    Reward: 50,000 per ball caught, plus a 200,000 bonus for catching all 16

  • Wabbit Hole Trivia Game secret · Easy

    A second non-pinball prize sits in the same Wabbit Hole pool: a multiple-choice quiz about Michael Jordan's career, answered with the flippers while the ball waits.

    Reward: An award for choosing the correct answer
