White Water

Williams, 1993 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open White Water in the Pinball Genie app →

Designed by Dennis Nordman, programmed by Mike Boon, art by John Youssi. A widebody WPC (Fliptronics II) title, 7,008 units produced. No Pro/Premium/LE editions — that convention didn't exist yet in 1993. Rule variance is by ROM revision only (notably L-3 vs L-4+), affecting a few point values and 5X interactions noted below.

Layout in brief

Two main flippers plus a single upper flipper on a raised upper playfield. Seven named Hazard shots form the backbone of the ruleset: No Way Out (center lock scoop, flanked by two green Lite Lock targets), Spine Chiller (left ramp to the upper deck), Disaster Drop (center ramp/loop that runs under the upper deck and drops out near Boulder Garden), Boulder Garden (left orbit), Boomerang Bend (right orbit, the hardest shot in the game), Insanity Falls (upper-playfield ramp, shot with the upper flipper, doubles as the Multiball Jackpot and Skill Shot), and Bigfoot Bluff (upper-playfield ramp guarded by the animated Bigfoot figure, who normally diverts the ball into the Whirlpool unless distracted). Other key features: Lost Mine (scoop, also the Whirlpool's exit point), the 5-target R-I-V-E-R bank, the 2-target green Lite Lock, the 3-target red Light Bounceback bank, the 2-target white Hotfoot bank, an Extra Ball standup, and the hidden Secret Passage rollover reached only by a soft Boulder Garden shot.

Skill Shot

A gentle-medium plunge sends the ball onto the upper deck and into Insanity Falls, worth an escalating 1M→5M across the game. Too hard a plunge overshoots into Spine Chiller instead (5K consolation on later ROMs).

Hazards & Raft

Each of the 7 Hazards pays 500K × current River Class when lit (50K flat unlit). Completing the currently-lit set advances your Raft number (1M × destination raft) and relights a new set — roughly 1-5 shots required per raft as you climb. Eight completed rafts leads to Wet Willie's.

River Class (multiplier)

Complete the 5 R-I-V-E-R standups (or roll the currently-lit inlane) to advance River Class 1→6. It multiplies both lit-Hazard value and the end-of-ball bonus, but resets to 1 every new ball.

Whirlpool Awards (6-step cycle)

Shoot Insanity Falls to light the current award, then collect it at Bigfoot Bluff: (1) Whirlpool Challenge (2-ball multiball, escalating Bigfoot Bluff hits), (2) Man Overboard (hurry-up, all lit Hazards worth 20M counting down to 3M), (3) Light Extra Ball, (4) Advance Raft, (5) Mystery Canyon (random bonus), (6) 5X Playfield (25-second full-scoring multiplier, hard cutoff, no grace period).

Multiball

Hit both green Lite Lock targets to light lock, then shoot No Way Out — three locks (two-hit pattern each) starts Multiball with all 3 balls fed to the upper-right flipper. The opening volley through Insanity Falls sets the Jackpot tier for the rest of the multiball: 3/3 = Triple, 2/3 = Double, 0-1/3 = Single. Base Jackpot ≈10M, +2M per Raft advanced. No ball-save during multiball.

Bigfoot Hotfoot & Camera Craze

Hitting the two white Hotfoot targets (default ~3-4 hits) distracts Bigfoot for ~20 seconds — Bigfoot Bluff shots pay repeat 10M Jackpots and spot Lost Mine items (flashlight, map, key, camera, cow) in sequence. Collecting the camera (4th item) triggers Camera Craze: every Hazard except No Way Out pays a flat 3M for the rest of the ball.

Gold Rush

Collect the flashlight, map, and key (via Lost Mine or Hotfoot), then shoot Lost Mine again to start a 2-ball multiball where every switch hit scores 250K.

Boulder Bash

Every 3rd Boulder Garden hit triggers a 20-second window of escalating Jet Bumper value.

Wet Willie's

Reached after climbing the Raft ladder. Stage 1: all 7 Hazards lit at once, 10M each, collect all seven. Stage 2: a single travelling hazard light rotates through the same 7 shots every ~3 seconds for a 25-second window, 100M per hit (50M on early ROMs); draining resets you to Raft #2.

Combos

Every combo starts Spine Chiller → Insanity Falls (2M). Extending to No Way Out, Disaster Drop, or Boomerang Bend makes a 3-way (5M). Looping back through Insanity Falls from the No Way Out branch makes a 4-way (8-10M depending on ROM). Extending that with Disaster Drop or Boomerang Bend completes a 5-way (10M, ~25M+ stacked total) — the hardest shot in the game, tuned around a deliberately difficult backhand off the upper flipper.

Wizard Mode — Vacation Bonus

Requires four legs completed in one game: River Class 6, Wet Willie's started, all 6 Whirlpool Awards collected, and all 3 balls locked. Locking the 3rd ball once legs 1-3 are done pays the Vacation Bonus (200M + 10K × prior games played) plus a Special, then starts an elevated Multiball. Competitively deprioritized — most players score more from repeated Multiball/Hotfoot cycles than chasing it.

End-of-ball bonus

(50K × Hazards collected + 75K × Rafts advanced + 100K × Whirlpool Awards collected) × River Class. River Class resets each ball, so bonus does too.

How White Water plays

White Water is a widebody raft trip down a mountain river where you run the rapids, lock balls at No Way Out for Multiball, and climb toward the Vacation Bonus. Every hazard you clear and every River Class you raise feeds a fatter end-of-ball payout.

The loop

  1. Feather the plunge so the ball crests onto the upper deck and rolls Insanity Falls for the skill shot, then keep defaulting to No Way Out any time nothing else is lit.
  2. Tap the two green targets flanking No Way Out to light a lock and shoot the scoop; a third locked ball kicks off Multiball.
  3. Trade Insanity Falls and Bigfoot Bluff back and forth to climb the Whirlpool award ladder up to a full-table multiplier, and sit on it until you can dump it into a multiball or a Hotfoot run.
  4. Spot R-I-V-E-R on the blue standups or the lit return lane to step your River Class, which scales every hazard and your bonus.
  5. Reach the top River Class, open Wet Willie's, sweep all of the Whirlpool awards, and lock a third ball to trigger the Vacation Bonus.

Reading the lights

  • Lock at No Way Out (shot arrows) — lit: The two green targets on either side of No Way Out light a lock; a lit lock means the scoop will hold your next ball rather than kick it back out. · then: Bank three balls at No Way Out to erupt into Multiball, then send all three up Insanity Falls before you lose one - three of three sets the top jackpot tier for the rest of the round, so the opening volley is where the points are.
  • R-I-V-E-R Return Lanes (lanes) — lit: One return lane glows as the next R-I-V-E-R letter; roll the ball through the lit one to spot that letter, while the dark lane does nothing for the word. · then: Spot all of R-I-V-E-R (the row of blue standups on the left spells it just the same) to raise your River Class one rung, which multiplies every hazard award and your end-of-ball bonus. River Class tumbles back to the bottom the instant a ball drains, so bank the rungs while you still hold the ball. · control: Either flipper button: a tap of either flipper button walks the lit return lane one step to the side, so you can park it under whichever inlane the ball is dropping toward and catch the letter you still need (before the ball settles into a return lane)
  • Whirlpool Award Ladder (ladder) — lit: Insanity Falls lights whichever Whirlpool award is next on the ladder, and a made Bigfoot Bluff collects it and steps you up one; each rung stays where it is until you cash it. · then: Work the pair upward through Whirlpool Challenge, an extra ball and the rest to reach the top rung, a full-table multiplier. Its timer is short and merciless, so only light it when you are a shot away from Multiball or a Hotfoot window to spend it inside.
  • Bigfoot Hotfoot (mode) — lit: The pair of white standups above the right flipper light Bigfoot Hotfoot; once it starts, Bigfoot quits swatting the ball away and Bigfoot Bluff stays open for a short farming window. · then: Hammer Bigfoot Bluff again and again in that window - it caroms back to the right return lane for a quick reshot and steps the Lost Mine item chain forward, and reaching the camera item switches on Camera Craze, which then pays a flat award on nearly every hazard through the end of that ball. Collect the mine items straight instead and you light the Gold Rush add-a-ball run.
  • Wet Willie's Hazards (mode) — lit: Advancing the Raft far enough opens Wet Willie's, which lights all seven hazard shots at once; a lit hazard is one you have not collected, and the set carries from ball to ball until you finish it. · then: Gather every lit hazard to close out the first stage, then chase the single travelling light around the same seven shots in the timed second stage, where each on-time hit pays the mode's biggest per-shot award. Draining knocks the Raft back rather than to zero, so it is worth restarting.
  • Boulder Bash Jets (status) — lit: Every boulder-garden hit nudges the jet value up for the rest of the game, and each third hit lights a brief Boulder Bash where the jets pay a flat premium on top. · then: Keep feeding the left orbit to grow the jets and to trip Boulder Bash, then let the ball ride the bumpers while the window is open. A shot that dies near the top of the rail can also slip the hidden Secret Passage lane and push the Raft along for free.

Lane change: the flipper buttons move the lit lane. A tap of either flipper button walks the lit return lane one step to the side; line it up with the inlane the ball is heading down, roll through to spot an R-I-V-E-R letter, and finishing the word lifts your River Class multiplier for hazards and bonus.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Cash the soft-plunge skill shot at Insanity Falls, then lean on No Way Out - your bread-and-butter shot, makeable off either flipper - and work the forgiving Hotfoot targets. Spot R-I-V-E-R off the standups or the lit lane early to get the multiplier climbing. — Skill Shot — Insanity Falls, Strategy — First 30 Seconds, River Class — Build the Multiplier
  • Building — Lock three at No Way Out for Multiball and run its jackpot loop under control. Cycle the Whirlpool ladder toward the full-table multiplier and hold it, then unload it during Multiball or a Bigfoot Bluff Hotfoot run for the game's fattest hits. — Multiball — Lock 3 at No Way Out, Bigfoot Hotfoot, 5X Playfield — Light the Big Multiplier, Strategy — Stack 5X into Multiball or Hotfoot
  • The big one — The Vacation Bonus is the summit: reach the top River Class, start Wet Willie's, collect all of the Whirlpool awards, and lock a third ball to bank a nine-figure bonus plus a Special before an elevated Multiball even begins. Stacking the full-table multiplier into that Multiball is where the hundred-million hits live. — Wizard Mode — Vacation Bonus, Wet Willie's, Strategy — Stack 5X into Multiball or Hotfoot

Does White Water have an action button?

White Water has no cabinet button with an in-game job — two flipper buttons, a real spring plunger, and your hips are the whole interface. The one wrinkle worth knowing is that the right flipper button is a two-stage switch: a normal press works the lower right flipper, and pushing it the rest of the way also fires the upper flipper on Bigfoot's raised deck.

Goals in White Water

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Skill Shot — Insanity Falls skillshot · Easy

    A gentle-medium plunge sends the ball onto the upper playfield and into Insanity Falls for an escalating bonus (1M-5M across the game).

    Reward: 1M-5M escalating skill shot value

  • 2-Way Combo — Spine Chiller Slide combo · Easy

    Every combo in the game starts with this pair: Spine Chiller straight into Insanity Falls.

    Reward: 2,000,000 pts

  • 3-Way Combo — into No Way Out combo · Medium

    Extend the base 2-way combo by finishing at No Way Out for 5M.

    Reward: 5,000,000 pts

  • 3-Way Combo — into Disaster Drop combo · Medium

    Same opener, finished on Disaster Drop instead of No Way Out.

    Reward: 5,000,000 pts

  • 3-Way Combo — into Boomerang Bend combo · Medium

    Same opener, finished on the tough right-orbit shot for 5M.

    Reward: 5,000,000 pts

  • 4-Way Combo — Double Falls combo · Hard

    Loop back through Insanity Falls a second time off the No Way Out kickout for the 4-way tier.

    Reward: 8,000,000 pts (10M on some ROM revisions)

  • 5-Way Combo — Full Run combo · Hard

    The hardest and highest-value combo in the game: extend the 4-way with one more leg for a stacked ~25M total.

    Reward: 10,000,000 for the 5th leg, ~25M+ total stacked

  • Multiball — Lock 3 at No Way Out multiball · Hard

    The core scoring engine: light lock with the green targets, lock all 3 balls at No Way Out, then hammer the opening Jackpot volley.

    Reward: Jackpots at Insanity Falls, base ~10M +2M per Raft, Single/Double/Triple tier

  • Whirlpool Challenge (2-ball) multiball · Medium

    First award in the Whirlpool cycle — light it at Insanity Falls, collect at Bigfoot Bluff, then farm repeat Bigfoot Bluff hits.

    Reward: 5M first hit, +2M per hit, 20M/40M/60M/80M every 6th hit (100M cap)

  • Gold Rush (2-ball) multiball · Medium

    Collect the 3 Lost Mine items — the shot that picks up the third one starts the 250K-per-switch multiball on the spot.

    Reward: 250K per playfield switch hit while running

  • Wet Willie's mode · Hard

    The final Raft milestone — collect all 7 lit Hazards in Stage 1, then chase a single travelling hazard light in the 25-second Stage 2.

    Reward: 10M per Hazard (Stage 1); 100M per hit (Stage 2, 50M on early ROMs)

  • Bigfoot Hotfoot mode · Medium

    Distract Bigfoot with the white standups so he stops diverting the ball, then farm repeat Bigfoot Bluff Jackpots for ~20 seconds.

    Reward: 10M per Bigfoot Bluff shot (50M under 5X); 6-7 shots possible

  • Camera Craze mode · Medium

    Collecting the 4th Hotfoot item (camera) triggers a flat 3M pay on any Hazard except No Way Out for the rest of the ball.

    Reward: Flat 3M per Hazard shot (15M under 5X), regardless of lit state

  • Boulder Bash mode · Easy

    Every 3rd Boulder Garden hit lights a 20-second escalating bonus in the Jet Bumpers.

    Reward: Escalating jet bumper value, starts 50K +50K/hit

  • River Class — Build the Multiplier multiplier · Medium

    Complete the R-I-V-E-R standups (or roll the lit inlane) to raise River Class, which multiplies Hazard value and end-of-ball bonus.

    Reward: River Class 1-6, multiplies 500K×class Hazards and bonus

  • 5X Playfield — Light the Big Multiplier multiplier · Hard

    Cycle through the Whirlpool Award ladder to reach award #6, 5X Playfield, then time its 25-second window into Multiball or Hotfoot.

    Reward: 25 seconds of ~5x scoring on nearly everything

  • Wizard Mode — Vacation Bonus wizard · Hard

    The 4-leg Vacation Planner: River Class 6, start Wet Willie's, collect all 6 Whirlpool Awards, then lock the 3rd ball to trigger the 200M+ Vacation Bonus and an elevated Multiball.

    Reward: 200M + 10K×prior games played, plus a Special, then Multiball starts

  • Strategy — First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    The recommended beginner opener: skill shot, one opportunistic upper shot, then default to No Way Out and Hotfoot.

    Reward: Fast, low-risk points and Multiball progress

  • Strategy — Tournament Priority Order strategy · Medium

    The competitive order of operations: Multiball first, Hotfoot second, Gold Rush and River Class as supplementary value, Vacation Bonus deprioritized.

    Reward: Maximizes points per ball within a time-boxed game

  • Strategy — Stack 5X into Multiball or Hotfoot strategy · Hard

    The single highest-value play in the game: hold 5X Playfield and cash it during Multiball Jackpots or a Bigfoot Hotfoot run.

    Reward: Jackpots in the 100M+ range per hit

  • Secret Passage — The Soft Left Orbit strategy · Medium

    A Boulder Garden shot without enough speed to complete the loop can drop through the hidden lane under the Spine Chiller ramp, which advances the Raft. It is largely luck — a backhanded left-loop shot is the usual way it happens, not a shot you can call.

    Reward: Raft advance plus lane award

  • Extra Ball — Two Ways In strategy · Medium

    Either thread the narrow standup above the right inlane, or take the extra ball rung of the Whirlpool award ladder, which is far safer.

    Reward: Extra ball
