Beetlejuice

Spooky Pinball, 2025 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Beetlejuice in the Pinball Genie app →

Beetlejuice is Spooky Pinball's 2025 standard-width machine designed by Luke "Spooky Luke" Peters and Corwin "Bug" Emery, based on the 1988 Tim Burton film. It ships as a Collector's Edition (999 units) alongside a standard edition; the two differ only in trim (armor, glass, topper, dust cover), not in rules. The headline gimmick is SpookySpeak voice control — the first pinball machine with voice controls — but it lives on the optional topper and is never required to play; every voice action has an Action-Button equivalent. Documentation for this recent machine is still filling in: multiballs and modes are well understood, but playfield-multiplier math, end-of-ball bonus, and the top wizard mode remain unconfirmed.

Layout

Three flippers — two lower plus a full-size upper-right flipper that services the "Waiting Room" upper playfield. Reading roughly clockwise: Left Orbit (figure-8s to the upper flipper), Left Ramp with the physical Dante's Inferno roulette wheel, the Spinner (feeds pops/Juno Scoop), three pop bumpers, the upper-left Juno Scoop, the center Polaroid Camera targets and Center (multiplier) Scoop with 1-2-3-4-5 inserts, the motorized Sandworm guarded by an Attack-from-Mars-style wall-target bank, the dual-lift Right Ramp (up = couch lock, down = loopable), the Handbook target, the convertible Right Orbit / Deadend vari-target with a drop target, the Beetlejuice captive-ball sculpt, and the Mystery (coffin) Scoop. Up top sit the Afterlife Couch ball lock, the Upper Loop (Knock inserts), and the Waiting Room standups/scoop/orbit. A Magna-Save magnet between the lower flippers auto-rescues outlane and center drains, making the game unusually forgiving.

Skill shots

The Standard Skill Shot rewards releasing the plunge on a lit value and feeds the right flipper; a miss is auto-caught by the magnet without spending a save. The Super Skill Shot — the only chain the code scores as a true "combo" — asks you to immediately hit Left Ramp -> Right Ramp -> Spinner while lit.

Modes (the purple stack)

All eight Beetlejuice modes use one flow: shoot purple-lit shots three times to light Mode Start, cycle to your pick with the Mode Change switch and press Launch to lock it, then shoot the Right Orbit to start. Documented modes: As Seen on TV (7 easy shots then the captive ball), Grave Dig (scoops -> headstone -> ramps -> orbit), Day-O Dinner Party (timed; the machine takes the flippers; switch hits build a multiplier), Head Shrink, Charades, Beetle Snake (3 pops, 30 spins, 3 ramps), 'Til Death (blue shots; spinner raises multiplier, right ramp raises shot value), and Otho's Seance (start and finish at the Handbook target). Modes cannot be started during a multiball. The Juno/Waiting Room chain runs Take a Number (Juno Scoop) -> Advance Counter -> Now Serving.

Multiballs

  • Afterlife Couch / Now Serving (3-ball, easiest): loop the Upper Loop to light locks, lock 3 on the Right Ramp; green shots for jackpots, Knock loop builds value, re-lock for the Super at Spinner/Juno.
  • Strange and Unusual (2-ball): inlanes light the Camera targets, hit each to start, shoot ramps for jackpots, complete flashing sets to add a ball.
  • It's Showtime: spell S-H-O-W-T-I-M-E on yellow shots (only available when a mode is qualified but not started), start at the Right Orbit, then ricochet a locked ball off the Right Orbit Drop Target into the vari-target for the Strength Test Super Jackpot (first one adds a ball).
  • Sandworm Battle: bash the wall targets, lower the wall, shoot the mouth to deplete its health bar (battles include Gut Punch, Sharp Shooter, Walk With Rhythm).

Multipliers, toys, and awards

Returning Souls is the playfield multiplier: pops spawn Lost Souls, returned via lit shots to briefly raise the X shown on the Center Scoop's 1-5 inserts (values/duration TBD). A separate bonus multiplier is fed by the upper-right Waiting Room standups (exact mechanics unconfirmed). The Dante's Inferno Wheel (Left Ramp) awards Super Pops/Ramps/Spinner/Worm at 5x, Extra Ball, points, or "You Lose." The Mystery/coffin scoop grants Lite Lock, points, Advance Now Serving, Lite Ball Save, or the Shooting Gallery video mode. Magna-Save re-qualifies at the Handbook target.

Wizard

Singing in the Stairwell is the documented mini-wizard multiball, lit by completing 2 modes or starting 4; it has built-in add-a-balls and a Super Jackpot at the Juno Scoop collected with the Action Button. A top-tier wizard mode and the Scare-o-meter remain undocumented.

Strategy

The strong opener is the Super Skill Shot into 1-2 Wheel spins for guaranteed value, then the Upper Loop to light the easy Couch multiball, tapping upper standups for bonus X along the way. Big scores come from stacking a brief Returning Souls playfield X and a Wheel Super onto Afterlife Couch Super Jackpots via the Knock loop — not from any combo chain, as the game has no formal combo system.

How Beetlejuice plays

Beetlejuice brings the Neitherworld to the playfield, leaning hard on a lockbar magnet that saves your drains and a stack of modes qualified by purple shots. Start the modes and three multiballs, build the Returning Souls playfield multiplier, and reach Singing in the Stairwell for the game's marquee scoring.

The loop

  1. Hit three purple Beetlejuice shots to qualify a mode, cycle to your pick at the Mode Change switch and lock it with the Launch button, then start it at the Right Orbit.
  2. Loop the couch on the Upper Loop to light locks, lock three up the Right Ramp for Afterlife Couch Multiball, and light the camera targets off the inlanes for Strange & Unusual.
  3. Feed the pop bumpers to spawn Lost Souls and return them for a Returning Souls playfield multiplier, then open it right before a multiball Super or a mode's big shot.
  4. Complete two modes or start four to light Singing in the Stairwell, then press the action button at the Juno Scoop to collect its Super Jackpot.

Reading the lights

  • Beetlejuice Mode Start (mode) — lit: Three purple Beetlejuice-face shots that roam the ramps, orbits and spinner light Mode Start, and a qualified mode holds at the Right Orbit until you begin it. · flashing: The Mode Change switch blinks while a mode is qualified, and a Launch-button press during that blink locks your pick so the slingshots cannot bump it. · then: Cycle the Mode Change switch to the mode you want, lock it with the Launch button, and shoot the Right Orbit to start it; modes cannot be qualified or run during a multiball, so fit them between multiballs.
  • Afterlife Couch Multiball (status) — lit: Looping the Upper Loop around the couch lights the Knock inserts for locks, the raised Right Ramp is the lock shot, and the couch holds three balls before it tips forward to release them. · then: Lock three up the Right Ramp to start this three-ball multiball -- the easiest on the machine -- pump jackpot value on the Knock loop, then re-lock a ball and collect the Super at the Spinner or Juno Scoop.
  • Strange & Unusual Camera Targets (status) — lit: Rolling either inlane lights Lydia's center Polaroid targets, and hitting each lit camera target once starts Strange and Unusual Multiball. · then: Bank the camera targets for a cheap two-ball start, then farm ramp jackpots and clear the flashing camera sets for add-a-balls that keep it going.
  • SHOWTIME Letters (letters) — lit: The yellow SHOWTIME shots exist in one narrow window -- after you qualify a mode and before you start it -- and vanish the moment a mode begins. · flashing: The letters glow yellow only in that gap between qualifying and starting, so a mode left waiting is your chance to bank them. · then: Leave a mode qualified and chip the yellow letters on the ramps, orbits and spinner, then begin It's Showtime Multiball back at the Right Orbit. Lock a ball there and bank it off the drop target so it caroms into the vari-target and pays the Strength Test Super Jackpot.
  • Sandworm Battle (mode) — lit: The motorized wall targets guard the Sandworm, and knocking all three down drops the wall and exposes the worm's mouth for a battle. · then: Shoot the exposed mouth to drain the worm's health bar and win the battle; defeating enough Sandworms lights an Extra Ball at its standup. · control: Action button: in the Walk With Rhythm battle, rapid action-button presses lower the worm so you can feed a ball into its open mouth (the Walk With Rhythm sandworm battle is running)
  • The Roulette Wheel (status) — lit: Repeated Left Ramp shots light the roulette wheel and the next Left Ramp shot sets it spinning, and the Left Ramp is the safest repeatable shot on the playfield. · flashing: The wheel spins after a lit ramp shot with a You Lose slice on it, so time your stop. · then: Stop the wheel on a five-times Super and stack it into a matching multiball phase, or bank the Extra Ball; a Super Spinner window turns cheap spinner rips into real multiplier value. · control: Action button: a press of the action button stops the spinning wheel where you want it -- Super Pops, Ramps, Spinner or Worm, an Extra Ball, or points -- while dodging the You Lose slice (the wheel is spinning after a Left Ramp shot)
  • Returning Souls Playfield X (multiplier) — lit: Hitting the pops spawns Lost Souls, and returning each soul on a lit shot banks another level of playfield multiplier up to five times, shown on the Center Scoop's numbered inserts. · then: The multiplier opens at whatever level you banked and bleeds off a step every thirty seconds, so trigger it in the instant before a Super Jackpot lands in multiball or a mode reaches its marquee shot, and it doubles that whole burst.
  • Magna-Save Magnet (status) — lit: The lockbar magnet sits between the flippers and, once charged, snatches balls headed for the outlanes or the middle and hurls them back into play, with a spent charge re-armed by swatting a fly at the Handbook target. · flashing: A green button glow means a charge is ready; once it is spent, hit the Handbook target to put the fly on screen and recharge. · then: Keep a charge banked for the drains you cannot flip out of and re-arm it at the Handbook target, which also starts Otho's Seance, so the save is there when a big ball is on the line. · control: Action button: a press fires the Magna-Save magnet to catch a draining ball, and after it is spent, hitting the Handbook target spawns a fly that a green-lit button press swats to recharge the save (a ball is slipping into an outlane or down the middle)

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Time the plunge to the lit value for the Standard Skill Shot -- a mistimed one gets grabbed by the magnet anyway, so it is nearly risk-free -- then chain the Super Skill Shot up the ramps and spinner. Bank a Wheel award or two off the safe Left Ramp and start looping the couch for locks. — Standard Skill Shot, Super Skill Shot, Left Ramp Wheel Spin, Beginner First 30 Seconds
  • Building — Reach Afterlife Couch Multiball, the easiest on the machine, and the camera multiball off the inlanes, and start Beetlejuice modes in the gaps between them. Spawn Lost Souls at the pops and open the Returning Souls playfield multiplier just before a Super. — Afterlife Couch / Now Serving Multiball, Strange and Unusual Multiball, Start a Beetlejuice Mode, Returning Souls (Playfield Multiplier), Best Opener: Super Skill Shot into the Wheel
  • The big one — Complete two Beetlejuice modes or start four to light Singing in the Stairwell, an add-a-ball mini-wizard multiball, then press the action button at the Juno Scoop to collect its Super Jackpot -- making the shot alone will not pay it. The biggest games stack a Wheel Super and the Returning Souls multiplier onto Afterlife Couch supers. — Singing in the Stairwell (Mini-Wizard), High-Score Stacking Line

The Action Button on Beetlejuice

Center of the lockdown bar.

Few games lean on a button this hard. Its headline job is the Magna-Save magnet tucked under the flippers, which grabs outlane and center drains and flings the ball back at you. Beyond that, modes borrow the button constantly — stopping the wheel, freezing values, and collecting Supers that the shot alone won't pay.

  • Ball slipping into an outlane or the middle: Fires the Magna-Save magnet and throws the ball back up between your flippers.
  • Magna-Save spent, fly buzzing on screen: Hit the Handbook target to spawn the fly, then swat it with a press while the button glows green.
  • Wheel spinning after repeated left ramps: Stops the wheel where you want it — Super Spinner, Super Ramps, Extra Ball, or a fat nothing.
  • As Seen on TV, shot value ticking down: Hold to freeze the current shot value while you line up the next shot.
  • Walk With Rhythm sandworm battle: Rapid presses lower the worm so you can put a ball in its mouth.
  • Super Jackpot lit at the Juno Scoop: During Singing in the Stairwell you must press to collect it; making the shot alone doesn't.

Goals in Beetlejuice

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Standard Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    Time the plunge to the lit value to feed the right flipper. A miss is auto-caught by the magnet, so it's a near-free bonus.

    Reward: Boosted skill shot value; ball fed to the right flipper

  • Super Skill Shot skillshot · Medium

    Land the Standard Skill Shot on the plunge first — that arms the Super: Left Ramp, Right Ramp, then rip the Spinner to finish.

    Reward: Highest-value plunge award (Super Skill Shot)

  • Knock Loop Value Builder combo · Medium

    The signature repeatable loop: cycle the Upper Loop around the couch with the upper flipper to light locks and pump jackpot value.

    Reward: Lights locks and builds Knock jackpot value

  • Right Ramp Loop Farm combo · Medium

    The safe repeat-flow shot: with the ramp down rather than raised for lock, the Right Ramp loops back to the right flipper for stacked jackpots.

    Reward: Repeatable ramp jackpots / rising shot value

  • Super Spinner Rip combo · Easy

    Win Super Spinner off the Wheel, then rip the spinner during the 5x window for cheap multiplier growth.

    Reward: 5x spinner value during the boosted window

  • It's Showtime Super Jackpot Ricochet combo · Hard

    The highest single-hit payoff: lock a ball in the Right Orbit, then bank it off the drop target into the vari-target for a Super Jackpot.

    Reward: Super Jackpot + Add-a-Ball on the first one

  • Left Ramp Wheel Spin combo · Easy

    Repeatedly hit the Left Ramp to light the roulette wheel, then spin for a 5x Super, Extra Ball, or points.

    Reward: Wheel award: 5x Super, Extra Ball, or points

  • Afterlife Couch / Now Serving Multiball multiball · Easy

    The easiest multiball to reach and the best beginner target: loop the couch to light locks, lock 3 on the Right Ramp, cash green-shot jackpots.

    Reward: 3-ball multiball; Super Jackpot at Spinner/Juno

  • Strange and Unusual Multiball multiball · Medium

    Lydia's Camera 2-ball MB: light the Polaroid targets via the inlanes, hit each once, then farm ramp jackpots with add-a-ball sets.

    Reward: 2-ball multiball with add-a-ball loops

  • It's Showtime Multiball multiball · Hard

    Spell S-H-O-W-T-I-M-E on yellow shots between modes, start at the Right Orbit, then chase the Strength Test Super Jackpot.

    Reward: Multiball + Strength Test Super Jackpot

  • Sandworm Battle multiball · Hard

    Bash the three wall targets to start a battle, lower the wall, and shoot the worm's mouth to deplete its health bar.

    Reward: Battle jackpots; progress toward Extra Ball

  • Start a Beetlejuice Mode mode · Medium

    The universal mode flow: three purple shots to qualify, cycle-and-lock your pick, then start at the Right Orbit.

    Reward: Starts your selected Beetlejuice mode

  • As Seen on TV mode · Medium

    An easy opener mode: shoot 7 easy lit shots, then hit the captive ball to stop Lydia and score jackpots.

    Reward: Jackpots for stopping Lydia

  • Day-O (Dinner Party) mode · Hard

    Timed survival mode where the machine takes the flippers: farm switch-dense targets to grow a multiplier, then cash before the timer dies.

    Reward: Big multiplier-driven mode score

  • Otho's Seance mode · Medium

    Start and finish this mode at the Handbook target, clearing the green shots it spawns in between.

    Reward: Completes the seance mode

  • Returning Souls (Playfield Multiplier) multiplier · Medium

    Hit the pops to spawn Lost Souls, return them via lit shots to light a brief playfield X, then dump it into a big scoring window.

    Reward: Brief playfield multiplier (shown on Center Scoop 1-5)

  • Bonus Multiplier (Upper Standups) multiplier · Medium

    Tap the upper-right Waiting Room standups whenever you have upper-flipper control to climb the end-of-ball bonus multiplier.

    Reward: Higher end-of-ball bonus multiplier

  • Singing in the Stairwell (Mini-Wizard) wizard · Hard

    The marquee scoring chase: complete 2 modes or start 4 to light this add-a-ball multiball, then collect the Super at the Juno Scoop with the Action Button.

    Reward: Mini-wizard multiball; Super Jackpot at Juno

  • Best Opener: Super Skill Shot into the Wheel strategy · Medium

    The recommended opener: land the Super Skill Shot, bank a Wheel Super, then light couch locks up top.

    Reward: Front-loaded guaranteed value plus an easy multiball lit

  • Beginner First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    A safe, forgiving opening line: lean on the auto-save magnet, bank Wheel value, reach the easiest multiball, and start an easy mode.

    Reward: Safe, high-value beginner progression

  • High-Score Stacking Line strategy · Hard

    The score ceiling: stack Returning Souls playfield X and a Wheel Super onto Afterlife Couch Super Jackpots via the Knock loop.

    Reward: Score ceiling — the 600M+ lines

  • Light and Collect an Extra Ball strategy · Medium

    Three paths to a free ball: bank one off the wheel, defeat Sandworms until it lights, then cash it at the Extra Ball standup.

    Reward: Extra Ball

  • All Three Multiballs in One Game strategy · Hard

    The order that gets every multiball started in one game: easiest first, camera targets second, then spell out the Showtime letters between modes.

    Reward: All three main multiballs started in a single game

  • Shooting Gallery (Video Mode) secret · Medium

    One of the Mystery awards is not points at all — it drops you into a shooting gallery on the display. It is the rare mode where the action button and the flippers do two different jobs at once: one fires, the other aims.

    Reward: Points for the targets you hit during the video mode
