King Kong: Myth of Terror Island

Stern Pinball, 2025 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open King Kong: Myth of Terror Island in the Pinball Genie app →

Keith Elwin's follow-up to JAWS/Godzilla/Jurassic Park, released spring 2025 in Pro (~$6,999), Premium (~$9,699) and LE (~$12,999, 932 units honoring Kong's 1932 debut). The code is still pre-1.0 as of this writing, so exact point values may shift, but the mechanics below are stable. Roughly 100M is an average game; 300M+ wins tournaments.

Layout at a glance

A four-flipper playfield: two lower flippers with a rare center post between them, a full upper-right flipper fed by the cliffs ramp, and an upper-left mini-flipper serving the drops, river lane and cliffs ramp. There are no pop bumpers. Drain geometry is unusual — the left outlane sits between the two left inlanes. The plunge feeds the top-left, so every skill shot targets left-side features. Key shots: the Gong bash target (summons Kong, starts island scenes; Pro lights it via white island arrows), center ramp (Kong locks/Kombos), Spider Pit (magnet + spider sculpt), cliffs ramp, side ramp, biplane helix ramp, left/right orbits with optical spinners, river lane / Log Bridge (action-button diverter), punchback kicking target, Lost Temple 4-bank drops with a hidden Island Treasure target, and the far-right Kong Cave VUK.

Skill shots

Each unique skill shot instantly spots a KING KONG letter and adds ball-save time, so collecting a different variant every ball is the most efficient early currency. Variants: Skill (pit), Super (river), Anti (left outlane), Secret (inner-left inlane), Super Secret (outer-left inlane), Mega Secret (mini-flipper reflex to cliffs, 1.75M, 2×/3× with a biplane/U-turn follow-up), Double Mega, and Ultra Secret (cliffs → cross-field river, ~7M). A weak plunge that dribbles back grants a Sneak-In re-attempt.

Spelling KING KONG (8 letters)

Letters come from unique skill shots, gong hits, cliffs shots, Pterodactyl cave awards and a flawless Super Sweep. Set 1 perks arm next-shot 2× multipliers (cliffs, river, pit) and light Deadeye (Pro at "G", Prem/LE at "O"); the 8th letter lights T-Rex Battle. A second full spell lights Boss T-Rex (4-ball multiball).

Multiballs

  • King Kong Multiball — the #1 tournament multiball. Complete the KONG drops to lock at the center ramp; the third lock starts it (Prem/LE: Kong flips the NYC train car; Pro uses virtual locks + right-orbit start). Cash the side-ramp super, use cliffs = 2×, and relock at the center ramp for 2× all jackpots (20s).
  • Spider Pit Multiballs — four total; the first three are creature battles (Giant Spider is the best earner, max 25M × 4). The fourth is Spider Queen (super at the pit; an 8th Wonder objective).
  • Lost Temple Multiball — earned via the Heart of the Keepers treasure through the drop-target cipher system.

Modes

Five 60-second Island Scenes (Save Ann, Cross the Chasm, Pterodactyl Attack, Stegosaurus Encounter, Escape the Swamp), all started at the gong; playing/completing them feeds 8th Wonder and yields map segments. Crash the Gate is the 6th-scene mini-wizard. Climbing to 200/500 ft lights Island Locks that upgrade a scene to a 2-ball multiball. NYC Events (Stage Fright, Window Shopping, Manhattan Rampage) light at the right orbit after clearing climb shots and each carry one of three single-use scoring modifiers.

Combos ("Kombos")

The core money system: chain major shots to build a 2→5-way Kombo, which lights the Powerup target — cash it for (sum of Kombos) × Kombo length, up to 100M, plus stacked timed buffs. Disabled in multiball. Named combos: Super Sweep (left orbit through the drops → side ramp = 10M, or 50M + a letter for all four), Ricochet (right return → cliffs = 5M), and Biplane combos Barrel Roll and Chandelle Maneuver. Super Spinners (alternating orbit rips, 25M each) is the safest farm.

Climbing, treasures & multipliers

"Always be climbing" — height lights Island Locks, NYC events, an extra ball, and a large end-of-ball bonus term. Map segments feed Treasure Hunts at the Kong Cave; 2× playfield scoring is lit by the four orange X targets + the roving X. Bonus X builds at the drops. End-of-ball bonus = (100 × switches) + (2,500 × height) + (150,000 × letters), all × Bonus X.

Wizard modes

Mini-wizards: Crash the Gate, T-Rex Battle, Boss T-Rex. The main wizard "8th Wonder of the World" is qualified by filling six gate objectives (Island Scenes, Climbing, NYC Events, Biplane Attack, King Kong MB, Spider Pit/Queen), each with L1 and L2 tiers.

Editions

Rules are identical across editions; only mechs differ. Premium/LE add the physical NYC train-car lock, the animatronic Kong, and the river log-bridge diverter, plus two KING KONG letter-timing tweaks (Deadeye at "O", each Save Ann gong enters the VUK). Pro uses virtual locks and white island arrows. LE adds cosmetic/AV upgrades only.

How King Kong: Myth of Terror Island plays

King Kong sends you across Skull Island and up the Empire State Building, with an action button that steers the ball into a hidden river path and a spider-pit magnet that runs its creature battles. Play the island scenes, spell KING KONG for shot multipliers, capture the multiballs, and fill six gate objectives to reach the 8th Wonder of the World.

The loop

  1. Ring the gong to summon Kong and start an Island Scene at the gong saucer, playing it out single-ball while everything else keeps scoring.
  2. Bank a unique skill shot each ball and ring the gong to spell KING KONG, arming next-shot two-times multipliers on the cliffs, river, pit and biplane.
  3. Clear the KONG drop bank to light Kong locks, lock three at the center ramp for King Kong Multiball, and hit the Pit to run the creature-battle multiballs.
  4. Fill the six objectives above the flippers -- island scenes, climbing height, NYC events, biplanes, a King Kong super, and Spider Queen -- to qualify the 8th Wonder of the World.

Reading the lights

  • Gong Island Scenes (mode) — lit: The white island arrows and orange X standups light the gong on Pro, and ringing it summons Kong and lights the Gong Saucer behind it to start an Island Scene that runs single-ball while everything else keeps scoring. · then: Start scenes like Cross the Chasm and Pterodactyl Attack at the gong and never save them for a multiball, where they are near-impossible; playing all five is the first of the six gates to the 8th Wonder.
  • KING KONG Letters (letters) — lit: A unique skill shot each ball instantly spots one KING KONG letter, while switch hits anywhere fill a green bar you cash at the gong for the next letter, and every letter carries a shot-multiplier perk. · flashing: The green bar under the current letter fills with switch hits; ring the gong to collect the letter once it tops out. · then: Spell the first KING KONG for next-shot two-times perks on the cliffs, river and pit, and the second set for a two-times biplane round; all eight letters light the T-Rex Battle mini-wizard at the gong.
  • King Kong Multiball (status) — lit: Clearing the KONG drop bank lights the Kong locks -- the first clear lights all three at once, each one after needs another clear -- and the center ramp is the lock shot. · flashing: A light-kong-lock prompt flashes at the drops whenever the next lock is ready to work toward. · then: Lock three at the center ramp to start King Kong Multiball, hit the cliffs ramp for two-times jackpots and grow the side-ramp super on green-arrow shots, then relock at the center ramp for a twenty-second window that doubles every jackpot.
  • Spider Pit Battles (status) — lit: The Pit's three standups sit in front of a captive magnet, and hitting the Pit lights and starts its creature multiballs. · flashing: A rotating insert at the Pit cycles the monster choice for about ten seconds, so time it to leave the Giant Spider lit. · then: Start the Giant Spider battle, raise its jackpot multiplier toward four times on ramp shots, then drive the magnet-trapped ball into the standups to collect; clearing the creature battles opens Spider Queen, an 8th Wonder objective.
  • River Log Diverter (lanes) — lit: A hidden log diverter can kick a loose ball off into the secret river path, which feeds the mini flipper so the drop bank and cliffs ramp become gentle, deliberate shots. · then: Use the river to set up the Lost Temple drop ciphers and cliffs-ramp shots calmly, leaning on the river's own four-times multiplier and its ball-save checkpoint; the Echo Horn treasure hands you a pair of extra diverter presses each ball. · control: Action button: a press fires the log diverter to send a loose ball down the river lane -- fire it just after a right orbit shot, or even directly from the plunge -- so the ball arrives at the mini flipper and you can take the drops and cliffs ramp at your own pace (a ball is about to reach the river entrance)
  • Building Climb Arrows (ladder) — lit: Yellow climb arrows rove the orbits and ramps -- four lit at random to start -- and completing a set lights the next as your height climbs the building, with that height holding as the spine of your end-of-ball bonus. · then: Keep hitting climb arrows to reach the milestones -- an Island Lock and extra ball near two hundred feet, NYC events higher up, Climb Complete at twelve hundred -- and grab the Powerup target to flash every arrow lit and bank a set fast.
  • Biplane Attack (shot arrows) — lit: Three side-ramp shots qualify the air battle and light the ramp for a kill, while the biplane's helix ramp and the alternating spinners set how much each downed plane is worth. · then: Rip a spinner to pump the next kill, then shoot the side ramp again to down a plane and stack kills before the timer dies; five planes lights the Biplanes Destroyed gate for the 8th Wonder.
  • Powerup Kombos & 2X Scoring (multiplier) — lit: The Powerup target lights whenever a two-way-or-better Kombo is running, and the orange X standups light two-times playfield scoring once you complete them and take the roving X. · flashing: The Powerup target stays lit only while a Kombo chain is alive, so cash it before the chain lapses. · then: Chain five major shots and cash the Powerup target for up to a hundred million plus a stack of timed buffs, and switch on two-times scoring during a multiball or a mode jackpot rather than wasting it on stray singles; both are single-ball only, since Kombos switch off in multiball.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Soft-plunge a skill shot -- the Pit spots a KING KONG letter and ball save, and the softer Secret and Mega Secret plunges pay more -- then ring the gong to start an Island Scene so a mode is scoring while you get on with everything else. — Basic Skill Shot, Mega Secret Skill Shot, Secret Skill Shot — Inner Left Inlane, Beginner: First 30 Seconds
  • Building — Spell KING KONG off skill shots and gong hits for shot multipliers, clear the KONG drops to lock up King Kong Multiball, and start the Spider Pit creature battles. Keep climbing height on the orbits to feed bonus and light NYC events, and cash five-way Kombos at the Powerup target. — Spell KING KONG for Shot Multipliers, King Kong Multiball, Giant Spider (Spider Pit) Multiball, Always Be Climbing, 5-Way Kombo into Powerup Target, Best Competitive Opener
  • The big one — Fill the six gate objectives above the flippers -- all five island scenes, Climb Complete, the three NYC events, five biplanes, a King Kong Multiball super, and Spider Queen -- to qualify the 8th Wonder of the World. Spelling KING KONG twice over stacks the T-Rex Battle and its boss four-ball multiball on the way there. — 8th Wonder of the World (Qualification), T-Rex Battle, Spider Queen Multiball

The Action Button on King Kong: Myth of Terror Island

Center of the lockdown bar.

Mostly a steering control. The button fires a hidden log diverter that shunts a passing ball into the river lane and down to the mini flipper, where the drop targets and cliffs ramp are easy pickings. In the T-Rex fight it stops steering and becomes a mash button.

  • Ball about to reach the river lane: Fires the log diverter — press right after a right orbit shot, or even straight off the plunge.
  • Lost Temple drops or cliffs ramp needed: Route the ball down to the mini flipper and pick those shots off at your own pace.
  • Ball held in the pit during Pummel: Mash for ten seconds; each press stacks value before the pummel multiplier lands on the total.
  • Echo Horn collected on the third treasure hunt: You get two extra diverter uses every ball, so stop hoarding them.

Goals in King Kong: Myth of Terror Island

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Basic Skill Shot skillshot · Easy

    The safe opener: plunge into the Pit for a free KING KONG letter and ball-save time, while banking a hit toward Spider Pit Multiball.

    Reward: KING KONG letter + ball save

  • Mega Secret Skill Shot skillshot · Hard

    The high-value plunge farm: full-plunge to the mini-flipper, then reflex the cliffs ramp for 1.75M and 8 seconds of ball save.

    Reward: 1.75M + 8s ball save (2×/3× with follow-up)

  • Super Sweep combo · Medium

    The flagship combo: a single left-orbit shot that knocks down drops, then the side ramp. All four drops pays 50M plus a KING KONG letter.

    Reward: 10M (2 drops) / 50M + letter (all 4)

  • 5-Way Kombo into Powerup Target combo · Hard

    The #1 single-ball money play: chain five major shots, then cash the powerup target for up to 100M plus a full stack of timed buffs.

    Reward: Up to 100M + 5-way buff stack

  • Ricochet combo · Medium

    A quick 5M single-ball combo: roll the right return lane, then bounce the rubber near the mini-flipper up the cliffs ramp.

    Reward: 5M

  • Super Spinners combo · Medium

    The safest high-value farm: after the Drillplate of Dunne treasure, alternate ripping the left orbit and right loop spinners for up to 25M per rip.

    Reward: Up to 25M/rip (50M with 2×), 30s

  • Chandelle Maneuver combo · Medium

    A Biplane Attack combo that both boosts the next biplane kill and adds 15 seconds to the mode timer, making it farmable to extend the round.

    Reward: +500K next biplane, +15s

  • King Kong Multiball multiball · Medium

    The #1 tournament multiball: lock balls via the KONG drops, then work cliffs and side-ramp jackpots, relocking at the center ramp for a 2× window.

    Reward: Super jackpots + 2× relock window

  • Giant Spider (Spider Pit) Multiball multiball · Medium

    The strongest of the three creature battles: light the Pit with the Giant Spider insert lit, then use ramp shots to pump the jackpot multiplier to 4×.

    Reward: Max jackpot 25M × 4

  • Spider Queen Multiball multiball · Hard

    The fourth Spider Pit multiball and an 8th Wonder objective: defeat the Queen at random major shots, then bank the escalating super jackpot at the Pit.

    Reward: Super = 5M + 50% of prior pit MBs

  • Lost Temple Multiball multiball · Hard

    Earned via the Heart of the Keepers treasure: solve the drop-target ciphers, claim the treasure, then play a 3-ball multiball with a super at the Island Treasure target.

    Reward: 3-ball MB + Island Treasure super

  • Island Scene: Cross the Chasm mode · Medium

    An accuracy island scene best played single-ball: raise the river diverter, then save six sailors at the cliffs ramp, comboing the U-turn to multiply.

    Reward: 1.5M base +500K per save, growing ×

  • Island Scene: Pterodactyl Attack mode · Medium

    Defeat pterodactyls at the moving drop target to light the Kong Cave VUK; three cave awards complete it and the second spots a KING KONG letter.

    Reward: 5M/pterodactyl + 2×/3× cave awards

  • Island Scene: Escape the Swamp mode · Hard

    Build a punchback jackpot from flashing shots, then multiply it with an upper-flipper shot — river gives the biggest 4× boost.

    Reward: Punchback jackpot × up to 4×

  • NYC Event: Stage Fright mode · Hard

    An NYC event lit at the right orbit after clearing climb shots: hit the lit feature each phase, break the chain at the punchback, and complete with right-orbit shots.

    Reward: 2.5M +250K/phase +500K biplane

  • Light 2× Playfield Scoring multiplier · Medium

    Hit the four orange X targets, then the roving orange X to double all scoring — bank it on top of a multiball or mode jackpot, never on stray singles.

    Reward: 2× scoring, timer extendable up to four times

  • Spell KING KONG for Shot Multipliers multiplier · Medium

    Bank a different skill shot each ball plus gong and cliffs hits to spell KING KONG, arming next-shot 2× multipliers (cliffs, river, pit, biplane).

    Reward: Next-shot 2× on key shots + Kombo boosts

  • T-Rex Battle wizard · Hard

    A mini-wizard: spell all 8 KING KONG letters, start it at the gong, clear colored pit shots to light Pummel, then mash the action button.

    Reward: 7.5M then 15M per T-Rex + add-a-ball

  • 8th Wonder of the World (Qualification) wizard · Hard

    The main wizard mode: fill all six gate objectives shown above the flippers — island scenes, climbing, NYC events, biplanes, King Kong MB, and Spider Queen.

    Reward: Qualifies the 8th Wonder wizard mode

  • Best Competitive Opener strategy · Medium

    The tournament line: gong for an instant timed mode, pit for a letter and multiball progress, then drops to set up King Kong Multiball.

    Reward: Mode + letter + MB setup stacked early

  • Beginner: First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    A safe beginner routine: soft-plunge a skill shot, start a mode, then keep climbing and building Bonus X on the center-field shots.

    Reward: Ball save + mode + height + Bonus X

  • Always Be Climbing strategy · Easy

    Climbing height is the spine of the game — hit climb arrows constantly to light Island Locks, NYC events, an extra ball, and a huge bonus term.

    Reward: Island Locks, NYC events, extra ball, bonus

  • Biplane Attack mode · Medium

    Qualify the air battle with three shots to the side ramp, pump each kill's value on the alternating spinners, then shoot that same ramp again to cash the jackpot and down a plane.

    Reward: Escalating value per biplane destroyed

  • Secret Skill Shot — Inner Left Inlane secret · Medium

    The Pit is not the only thing worth aiming a plunge at. Dropping the ball into the inner left inlane straight off the plunger scores its own separately named skill shot that the machine never advertises.

    Reward: KING KONG letter (once per game) + ball save

  • Super Secret Skill Shot — Outer Left Inlane secret · Hard

    One lane further out sits an even less obvious target. An even softer plunge that settles in the outermost left inlane rather than the inner one scores the rarer of this machine's two hidden plunge awards.

    Reward: KING KONG letter (once per game) + ball save
