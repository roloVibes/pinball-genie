Safe Cracker

Bally, 1996 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Safe Cracker in the Pinball Genie app →

Safe Cracker is not a three-ball game. It is a timed game with an interactive board game bolted onto it, and your score comes from combining the two. You start with a set amount of time, the clock runs continuously during pinball play, and when it expires you enter sudden death — where the next drain ends the game outright.

Feeding the clock

Almost every switch on the playfield adds a small amount of time or briefly pauses the timer. That is deliberate: the machine is paying you to keep the ball moving rather than to trap and aim. Time remaining shows on the centre playfield timer lights and as a life bar on the display during score sweep.

  • The Wheel is the best time source on the machine. Time added is proportional to how far it spins, and a lit wheel adds more than an unlit one.
  • Jet bumpers add time on every hit, and lit jets add more. Complete drop target sets to light them.
  • The A-B-C top lanes add five minutes outright when completed.
  • Extra Time outlanes — once lit, rolling an outlane adds time, and uniquely it still works during sudden death.

Sudden death is survivable but narrow: most normal events stop adding time there, so the usual routes are closed. The outlane Extra Time lights still work, and the wheel's Disable Computer award holds the timer and stops a drain from ending the game. Any successful time add pulls you back out of sudden death and normal rules resume.

Breaking into the bank

Shoot the blinking drop target sets to light an entrance, then take the lock shot on the left ramp. The bottom left 3-bank is worth hitting early because it spots targets in the other sets, and after the first ball is locked it starts lighting additional entrances.

There are three ways in and the choice genuinely matters: the Cellar behind the sliding target, the Main Entrance scoop in the centre, and the Roof at the centre top lane. The entrance you use determines how far you must travel on the board to reach the Vault — your path is drawn from that entrance's light, clockwise around the centre circle, to the first unlit gate.

The sliding target is where the game is won

This is a two-part shot most players get wrong: you must drive the target ALL the way back, then drop the ball through the hole behind it. A partial hit does nothing. The awards it hands out are the ones that rewrite the board game in your favour.

  • ATM Card — multiplies the '$' spots on the board. Collect these BEFORE you break in; afterwards is too late.
  • Note to Teller — gets you past the teller.
  • Safe # — a letter toward V-A-U-L-T.
  • Explosives — a frenzy where the drop sets score big points.
  • Lite Outlanes — arms the Extra Time lights.

The board game

No ball is in play here. You stop a spinning safe knob with the flipper buttons to move, while a guard chases you using a purely random dice roll. He starts after you have moved several spots, from the same entrance you used. If he catches you, the run ends.

Several spots are items you can stockpile, and knowing them changes how you play the pinball half. Keys unlock locked gates and you can hold several. Dog Biscuits defeat the CyberDog, one consumed per landing. The stopwatch adds clock even in sudden death. Money Bags and '$' build your stash, with '$' multiplied by your ATM Cards. Code awards another VAULT letter.

Two spots can backfire. Move Cop Backwards is usually good, but if it moves the guard backwards onto you, you are caught. The '?' box hands you a random item and those items can be bad as well as good. If the continue-or-quit adjustment is enabled, you may stop after any successful spin and bank your stash rather than pushing your luck.

The Vault, and the long game

Completing the board game lands you in the Vault, which awards at least one token plus a large points award. Safe Cracker physically dispenses collectable tokens — twenty of them, including a silver one — which is what makes it the first machine of its kind.

The hidden top end is Token Multiball. Spelling V-A-U-L-T turns your next Vault entry into a multiball instead of an ordinary award, and crucially those letters are held FROM GAME TO GAME. A session on the same machine compounds, which is a rule almost nothing else in pinball has.

The tilt rule

Tilting is punished in two tiers. Tilt with time left and the game continues, but you are dropped straight into sudden death. Tilt while already in sudden death and the game ends with no bonus at all — and since the bonus is only computed at the very end, and every bank entrance has been stepping your multiplier, that is an expensive mistake. Nudge carefully once the clock is gone.

How Safe Cracker plays

Safe Cracker runs on a clock, not three balls: keep time on the timer by staying busy, break into the bank, and win the no-ball board game to reach the Vault for a physical token. Spell V-A-U-L-T across games and your next trip to the Vault becomes Token Multiball.

The loop

  1. Feed the clock -- the wheel is the best time source, the jets add time on every hit, and rolling A-B-C across the top lanes drops five minutes on at once.
  2. Knock down the blinking drop sets to light a way into the bank, then take the lock; after the first lock the bottom-left bank starts opening more entrances.
  3. Rig the board before you go in: stack ATM Cards, spell VAULT letters, and disable the alarm and teller from the sliding target and standups.
  4. Break in through the Cellar, Main Entrance or Roof -- the door you use sets your distance to the Vault -- then play the safe-knob board game to the end.

Reading the lights

  • A-B-C Top Lanes (lanes) — lit: The three top lanes each carry a letter, A, B and C; a lit letter is one you have already rolled this time through, and the unlit ones are what remains to complete the set. · then: Roll all three to bank a flat five minutes on the timer, which on a game where the clock IS your ball is one of the biggest single time awards going. Then start the set over for another five.
  • Drop Target Sets (shot arrows) — lit: The blinking drop sets are your way in -- clear every blinking set and an entrance lights. The bottom-left three-bank spots targets in the other sets, so hitting it first makes the whole job cheaper. · then: Down all the blinking sets, then take the lock to start the board game; completing sets also lights the jets for extra time. After your first lock the bottom-left bank changes jobs and begins lighting additional entrances into the bank.
  • Cellar, Main Entrance & Roof (mode) — lit: Three doors lead into the bank: the Cellar behind the sliding target, the Main Entrance scoop, and the Roof lane above the jets when it is lit. A lit entrance is a live way in. · then: Which door you take genuinely matters -- your starting point on the board is drawn from that entrance around the center to the first open gate, so the right door is a shorter path to the Vault. Every break-in also steps your end-of-game bonus multiplier.
  • The Wheel & Its Awards (status) — lit: Four award lamps in the middle of the playfield show what the wheel is currently offering. A lit wheel steps that award along as it spins, but moving it forward is a separate act from actually taking it. · then: Collect it by shooting the Main Entrance scoop. One offer holds the timer to buy you a sudden-death escape; another throws the locks open so the drop sets can be skipped outright; a third pays a teller gift; and the last can wave you in for a free break-in. The wheel doubles as your best clock, so no spin toward it goes to waste.
  • Sliding Target (Manhole) (status) — lit: Drive the sliding target all the way back and the hole behind it opens; a partial push opens nothing. When it is lit it hands over the awards that quietly rig the board game in your favor. · then: Take an ATM Card to multiply the board's dollar spots, a Note to Teller to slip past the teller, Explosives for a drop-set frenzy, Safe # for a VAULT letter, or Lite Outlanes to arm the Extra Time saves. Stack these BEFORE you break in, because once the board game starts it is too late.
  • V-A-U-L-T (letters) — lit: The V-A-U-L-T letters light one at a time as you earn them, and here is the twist: they are held from one game to the next, so a session on the same machine keeps building the word. · then: Letters come two ways: a Safe # award driven out of the sliding target, or landing on a Code square inside the board game. Once VAULT is complete, the following break-in that gets you to the Vault opens as Token Multiball rather than paying the usual Vault award.
  • Extra Time Outlanes (status) — lit: Both outlanes carry an Extra Time lamp, armed by the Lite Outlanes award from the sliding target. Lit, an outlane you would normally dread instead adds time to your clock as the ball rolls out. · then: Better still, a lit outlane keeps adding time even in sudden death -- one of the few things that does -- so it can pull you back from the brink. When a ball is already heading out, aim it at whichever outlane is lit rather than clawing it up toward the flippers.
  • Bonus Multiplier (multiplier) — lit: Each break-in bumps the bonus multiplier up a notch, and its readout sits low on the playfield. What makes it unusual is that the bonus goes uncounted until the game is completely over. · then: Break in as often as you can to climb it. Because nothing is banked until the end, a tilt in sudden death is doubly expensive -- it ends the game AND wipes the multiplied bonus you spent all game building, so never nudge hard once the clock is gone.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — There is no ball to preserve, only the clock, so plunge to keep the ball busy and feed time early. Rip the wheel, churn the jets, and roll A-B-C for a five-minute block, then clear the blinking drop sets and break in for the first time. — The Timer Is Your Ball, Break Into The Bank
  • Building — Set the board up from the playfield before you commit. Stack ATM Cards to multiply the dollar spots, gather VAULT letters, disable the alarm and the teller, and arm the Extra Time outlanes. Advance and collect wheel awards, and step your bonus multiplier with each entrance. — Sliding Target (Manhole) Awards, ATM Cards, Spell V-A-U-L-T, Bonus Multiplier
  • The big one — Reaching the Vault at the end of the board game is the whole point -- it dispenses a real collectible token and a large score. The top end is Token Multiball: finish spelling VAULT, and because those letters carry across games, your next successful Vault run breaks open into multiball. — Token Multiball, The Vault, Spell V-A-U-L-T

Does Safe Cracker have an action button?

No action button on Safe Cracker — a manual plunger, two flippers and a clock counting down on you. The flipper buttons do get a second job in the board game: with no ball in play, you stop the spinning safe knob with them to decide how far you move.

Goals in Safe Cracker

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • The Timer Is Your Ball progression · Easy

    Safe Cracker is a timed game, not a three-ball game. You start with a set amount of time, it runs continuously during main play, and when it expires you enter sudden death. Understanding what feeds the clock is the whole game.

    Reward: Survival

  • Sudden Death strategy · Medium

    When the clock runs out you are not finished — but the next drain ends the game. The cruel part is that during sudden death most normal events stop adding time, so the usual ways out are closed. Only a few specific things can rescue you.

    Reward: Escape back to normal play

  • Break Into The Bank progression · Medium

    The core pinball objective. Knock down the blinking sets of drop targets and an entrance lights; from there, the lock shot is where you go. Lock the first ball and the bottom left 3-bank takes over the job of opening further entrances.

    Reward: Starts the board game, advances bonus multiplier

  • Choose Your Entrance strategy · Medium

    Three ways into the bank, and the choice genuinely matters: the sliding target hides the Cellar, the scoop is the Main Entrance, and the lane above the jets is the Roof. Whichever you pick sets how far you then travel on the board to reach the Vault.

    Reward: A shorter path to the Vault

  • The Wheel And Its Awards mode · Medium

    A lit wheel advances the current wheel award, shown on four lights in the centre of the playfield — but advancing it is not collecting it. You have to shoot the Main Entrance scoop to actually take what the wheel is offering.

    Reward: Light Locks, Disable Computer, Deposit Window or Call Guard

  • Sliding Target (Manhole) Awards mode · Hard

    A two-part shot most players get wrong. Push the target fully back first; only after that does the hole behind it take the ball. What it pays out are the awards that swing the board game your way.

    Reward: ATM Card, Note to Teller, Explosives, Safe #, Lite Outlanes

  • Spell V-A-U-L-T progression · Hard

    The long game, and the rule that makes Safe Cracker unusual: VAULT letters are held from game to game. Letters come from Safe # awards off the sliding target and from Code spots on the board. Complete it and your next break-in to the Vault becomes Token Multiball.

    Reward: Next Vault entry becomes Token Multiball

  • The Board Game wizard · Hard

    The main feature. No ball is in play — you stop a spinning safe knob with the flipper buttons to move, while a guard chases you with a purely random roll. Get caught and the board game ends. Everything you did in pinball was setting the odds for this.

    Reward: A stash of money, and a route to the Vault

  • Board Game Spots Worth Knowing progression · Medium

    The board is not random noise — several spots are items you can stockpile, and knowing which ones save your run changes how you play the pinball half.

    Reward: A survivable board run

  • The Vault wizard · Hard

    The end of the board game and the point of the machine. Reaching it wins at least one token — Safe Cracker physically dispenses collectable tokens, twenty of them including a silver one — along with a large points award.

    Reward: At least one token, plus big points

  • Token Multiball wizard · Hard

    The hidden top end. With V-A-U-L-T fully spelled — and remember those letters carry across games — the next break-in that reaches the Vault becomes Token Multiball instead of an ordinary Vault award.

    Reward: Token Multiball

  • Collect Donuts combo · Easy

    Donuts come off the left ramp, and the right return lane times the ramp out for two at once. The board game's Donuts spot dumps twelve into your collection in one go, which is the fastest way to build them.

    Reward: Donut collection

  • Bob's Getaway strategy · Easy

    The Big-Kick on the left pays Bob's Getaway, a random points value that ascends as the game goes on. Later in a long game it is worth considerably more than it is at the start.

    Reward: Ascending random points

  • Explosives Frenzy mode · Medium

    A sliding-target award that turns the drop sets into big points for a short frenzy. Best taken when you already have sets standing, since the mode scores off knocking them down.

    Reward: Drop sets score big points

  • ATM Cards multiplier · Medium

    Collected from the sliding target, and they do nothing on the playfield — they multiply the '$' spots in the board game. This is pinball play directly buying a better board result, so stack them before you break in.

    Reward: Multiplies board-game '$' awards

  • Disable The Alarms And The Teller strategy · Medium

    Two obstacles on the board can be removed in advance from the playfield. Spelling A-L-A-R-M disables an alarm spot, and a Note to Teller from the sliding target gets you past the teller. Both are cheap insurance against a run ending early.

    Reward: Fewer board-game hazards

  • Extra Time Outlanes secret · Medium

    A genuine inversion of normal pinball instinct. Once the outlanes are lit for Extra Time, rolling one adds time to the clock — and it works even in sudden death, making a lit outlane a rescue rather than a disaster.

    Reward: Time added, even during sudden death

  • Bonus Multiplier multiplier · Easy

    Every entrance into the bank steps the bonus multiplier, shown at the bottom of the playfield. Unlike most games the bonus is only computed at the very end — which is exactly why tilting during sudden death is so expensive.

    Reward: Multiplied end-of-game bonus

  • Know The Tilt Rule strategy · Easy

    Safe Cracker punishes tilting in an unusual, two-tier way. Tilt while you still have time and the game continues — but you are dropped straight into sudden death. Tilt while already in sudden death and the game ends with no bonus at all.

    Reward: Not losing the whole bonus
