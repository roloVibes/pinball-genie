Breakshot

Capcom, 1996 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Breakshot in the Pinball Genie app →

Designer: Greg Kmiec (frequently misattributed to George Gomez). Art by Stan Fukuoka/Paul Mazur/Hugh van Zanten, software by Bill Pfutzenreuter/Tony DeFeo. One production run, ~1,000 units, May 1996, model BRK3002. No Pro/Premium/LE editions exist — that convention postdates this game.

The table

A single-level, EM-throwback design — no ramps, no spinner. Two main flippers plus an unusually capable upper-right "3rd" flipper that can trap and catch. The playfield reads bottom-to-top: slingshots/in-out lanes → Green (left) and Brown (right) drop-target banks near their respective orbits → a two-ball Captive Ball left-of-center → Purple/Orange standups flanking the Center Pockets (the signature toy: 3 saucers behind a rising post) → 3 jet bumpers → 3 rollover lanes (Yellow/Blue/Red) at the very top, fed by the Left and Right Orbits running up each edge.

Three pool "games"

Only one is active at a time (chalk icons over the slings show which), cycling Nine Ball → Eight Ball → Nine Ball → Rotation, repeating:

  • Eight Ball — sink 7 assigned colors (solids or stripes) via the rollover lanes, Purple/Orange standups, and Green/Brown banks, then shoot the 8-Ball Saucer. 100K.
  • Nine Ball — hit any of 5 shots (Left Orbit, Captive Ball, Right Orbit, Green standup, Brown standup) 9 times total. 200K, plus a Points/Special/Extra-Ball bonus wheel at the Center Pockets entrance.
  • Rotation — sink all 15 balls, cycling the same 7 shot-groups twice. 500K.

Both Super Cues (lit orbit shots) and any Center Pockets saucer hit auto-sink the lowest unsunk ball regardless of which game is active.

Center Pockets — the lock/multiball engine

A ball entering (post down) trips an opto, raises the post, and ejects a fresh ball to the plunger (up to 2 locks). A hard hit on the post with resting balls propels them into the side saucers — a Good Shot (base value), a Trick Shot (+100K, one ball into a lit side saucer), or a Nice Combo/"Breakshot" (+250K, both side saucers made on one hit — the game's namesake).

Ball-O-Rama (2-ball multiball, 2x scoring): lock one ball, then shoot the lit 8-Ball Saucer to start. Jackpot starts at 100K, climbs 10K/sec, never resets. Breakshot Frenzy (3-ball, 3x scoring): lock both side saucers, then capture a 3rd ball in the center saucer to auto-start. Jackpot starts at 200K, resets after each collect (2 "easy" jackpots before relighting is required); a fast second ball into the entry opto right after a collect scores a Double Jackpot. No ball saver runs during either multiball.

Multipliers

  • Bonus multiplier — rollover lanes advance it, seen up to 13x, paid at end-of-ball.
  • Multiball state — Ball-O-Rama 2x / Breakshot Frenzy 3x, global while active.
  • Bank Shot streak — each consecutive Bank Shot make (uninterrupted) doubles/triples the payout, no stated cap.
  • Left Super Cue → Bank Shot Bonus — pre-loading Left Super Cue shots before cashing the Bank Shot doubles a stacking 50K bonus (reported max 3.2M).
  • Super Cue chain — 60K × consecutive makes, reset by any other switch.

Combos and the money loop

Capcombo (250K): Bank Shot → Right Orbit, or Left Super Cue → Right Orbit, immediately. The headline technique is the Bank Shot catch-and-relaunch loop: light-plunge onto the upper-right flipper, catch, shoot the Bank Shot, re-catch, repeat — each lap sinks a ball and the value escalates fast (1M+ per lap is reachable by the eighth to tenth rep, and a single shot can pay far more). Landing both Super Cues back-to-back (both lit simultaneously, then the two orbits shot one right after the other) lights the Captive Ball for an Extra Ball.

Wizard mode — Cutthroat Countdown

Reached after completing 3 regular games (default). A 60-second timer calls balls one at a time (≤10 sec each); hitting the right target sinks it, scores 1M, and adds 10 seconds. A second pass calls missed balls in random order. Ending with at least one ball sunk lights the center saucer for a Super Jackpot worth 2M/ball sunk, scaling to 2x then 3x on repeat completions. Sinking every ball ("perfect") auto-spots the next game complete and chains straight into another round. The Frenzy multiball multiplier still applies to the per-ball 1M Cutthroat scoring during Cutthroat Countdown (up to 9M/ball when 3x is running), but it does not apply to the Super Jackpot itself.

Risk notes

Fast ricochets off the Center Pockets are the game's biggest drain hazard — approach it off a controlled trap, not a bounce. Multiball has no ball saver and the jackpot shot isn't forgiving. The Bank Shot loop's biggest risk is a mistimed catch, which resets the escalating value to base — skilled play means consciously bailing once you've banked a big number.

How Breakshot plays

Breakshot turns a game of pool into pinball: you rack and sink numbered balls to finish Eight Ball, Nine Ball and Rotation. Run enough pool games to reach Cutthroat Countdown, the timed wizard round where the table calls your shots one at a time.

The loop

  1. Sink your assigned balls with the top rollover lanes, the color drop banks and standups, the orbits and the captive ball to complete each pool game.
  2. Soft-plunge onto the third flipper to light the Bank Shot, then catch and re-fire it from that corner for a value that climbs every lap.
  3. Lock a ball in the Center Pockets for the 2x Ball-O-Rama, or trap both side saucers and settle a third in the center for the 3x Breakshot Frenzy.
  4. Light the colored Super Cues off the Purple and Orange standups, then combo them into the orbits for big points and an extra ball.
  5. Complete three pool games to trigger Cutthroat Countdown, then race the called balls before the timer expires.

Reading the lights

  • The Bank Shot (shot arrows) — lit: A soft plunge settles the ball on that third flipper up in the corner and turns the Bank Shot lane green; lit, it is the biggest repeatable earner on the table, and it holds its light only while you keep the ball trapped in that corner. · flashing: The green light times out the moment the ball leaves the corner or a catch goes wrong, dropping the escalating value back to its base. · then: Trap on the upper-right flipper, fire the Bank Shot, catch the rebound and go again -- each straight make scores more, past a million a lap on a long run, and sinks a ball in the active pool game. Bail to the main flippers the instant a catch feels shaky, and follow a Bank Shot into the right orbit for the Capcombo.
  • The Super Cues (shot arrows) — lit: The flashing Purple standup wakes the Right Super Cue on the right orbit, and the flashing Orange standup wakes the Left Super Cue on the left orbit; once a Super Cue is lit it holds through every drain and ball hand-off until you finally shoot it. · flashing: The Purple and Orange standups flash when each is ready to light its Super Cue. · then: Re-shoot the same lit orbit with nothing in between for an escalating chain, or light both cues and shoot them back to back to arm the captive ball for an extra ball. Stacking Left Super Cue hits before you cash the Bank Shot doubles a pending bonus each time, into the millions.
  • Top Rollover Lanes (lanes) — lit: One of the three top lanes -- Yellow, Blue, Red -- is lit; rolling the lit one advances your end-of-ball bonus multiplier a step and sinks the lowest ball you still owe in that lane's group. · then: Slide the lit lane under the ball to bank a free multiplier step on every trip through the top, reported as high as thirteen times. The bonus pays at drain, but the 8-Ball Saucer also lights once a ball for Collect Bonus, so you can cash a big one mid-ball. · control: Either flipper button: shift which of the three top lanes is lit sideways so you can line the lit one up with the lane the ball is dropping into (the ball is coming down through the top, where the operator has enabled flipper lane-change)
  • Color Banks (shot arrows) — lit: Each three-target drop bank hides a color standup behind it; clear the whole bank and its standup lights to sink one assigned ball, and taking a bank down in sequential order also arms the left-outlane kickback. · flashing: A color standup flashes once its three drops are all down. · then: Work the banks to sink your Green and Brown pool balls and to keep the left outlane armed as cheap insurance -- re-arm it after every save. Of the two banks the Brown one is the kinder shot from the upper-right flipper.
  • Center Pockets (status) — lit: A lit side saucer accepts a lock; one locked ball arms Ball-O-Rama over at the 8-Ball Saucer, while trapping both side saucers and then settling a third in the center saucer starts Breakshot Frenzy on the spot. · then: Take Ball-O-Rama for 2x and a climbing jackpot, or hold out for Breakshot Frenzy and its 3x. Drive two balls off the raised center post into both side saucers on one hit for the namesake 250K combo. A fast ricochet out of this toy is the worst drain risk on the playfield, so feed it only off a controlled ball.
  • 8-Ball Saucer (mode) — lit: This one saucer carries the most jobs on the playfield: it sinks the 8-ball to finish Eight Ball, starts and re-jackpots Ball-O-Rama, banks Collect Bonus, and lights Call Your Shot -- briefly off the left inlane, and any time the 8-ball is lit. · then: Clear your assigned colors and shoot here to complete Eight Ball, then use Call Your Shot to name a shot and make it within a second or two to finish the whole game at once. Rolling the left inlane is what briefly lights that pick. · control: Either flipper button: cycle the Call Your Shot display among its three named targets -- the center pocket or one of the Super Cues -- so you pick which one to make (Call Your Shot is lit at the saucer)
  • Captive Ball (The Moose) (status) — lit: The captive ball only ticks toward its extra ball on a hit firm enough to strike the target buried inside it; a soft nudge just trips the little rollover for a few thousand and counts for nothing. · then: Trap on the left flipper and hit it firm and square -- four solid hits by default light it for an extra ball, and one more collects. It is among the safest, highest-percentage shots you have, and it doubles as a Nine Ball shot.

Lane change: the flipper buttons move the lit lane. Tapping either flipper button shifts which of the three top rollover lanes -- Yellow, Blue, Red -- is lit, where the operator has enabled it. Slide the lit lane under the ball as it drops through the top, and rolling it steps your end-of-ball bonus multiplier a notch, reported as high as thirteen times, while sinking that lane's numbered ball.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Light-plunge for the Bank Shot skill shot -- a guaranteed hundred thousand plus a sunk ball at essentially zero drain exposure -- then ride the catch-and-reshoot loop a few laps and bail before your timing gets shaky. Take the left orbit and the forgiving captive ball for free pool progress. — Skill Shot — Light Plunge to the Bank Shot, Beginner's First 30 Seconds, Best Opener: Ride the Bank Shot, Then Bail
  • Building — Light the colored Super Cues and chain them for an extra ball, arm the left-outlane kickback off a drop bank, and lock the Center Pockets for a multiball. Ball-O-Rama runs at 2x and Breakshot Frenzy at 3x, so favor Frenzy whenever you can build to it. — Both Super Cues Back-to-Back, Ball-O-Rama (2-Ball Multiball), Breakshot Frenzy (3-Ball Multiball), Arm the Left Outlane Kickback
  • The big one — Complete three pool games to trigger Cutthroat Countdown, where the table calls balls one at a time for a million each and pays a per-ball Super Jackpot when the clock expires. Time it to overlap Breakshot Frenzy's 3x so each called ball is worth up to nine million, and sink every one for the maxed, repeating Perfect Countdown. — Cutthroat Countdown (Wizard Mode), Macro Strategy: Stack Frenzy Under Cutthroat Countdown, Perfect Cutthroat Countdown

Does Breakshot have an action button?

Breakshot is a 1996 game with a plain manual plunger and two flipper buttons — nothing else on the cabinet does anything in play. Your flipper buttons do double duty off the playfield, though: they pick which shot you are calling on Call Your Shot, and holding one in mid-game brings up the info screens.

Goals in Breakshot

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Skill Shot — Light Plunge to the Bank Shot skillshot · Easy

    A soft plunge parks the ball on the upper-right flipper, lighting the Bank Shot; catch and cash it for a guaranteed 100K and a sunk ball with almost no drain risk.

    Reward: 100K + sinks the next ball in the active pool game

  • Alternate Skill Shot — Hard Plunge to the Lanes skillshot · Easy

    A full-power plunge skips the Bank Shot line entirely and sends the ball to the top rollover lanes (or scores a Right Super Cue if already lit) — lower value but useful for building rollover progress.

    Reward: Advances the bonus multiplier + sinks a numbered ball; scores a lit Right Super Cue instead if already lit

  • Capcombo — Bank Shot into Right Orbit combo · Medium

    The game's signature flow combo: land the Bank Shot, then immediately loop into the Right Orbit before anything else, for a flat 250K and the moose-kicks-a-cow animation.

    Reward: 250K + the moose-kicks-a-cow animation (Capcombo)

  • Capcombo — Left Super Cue into Right Orbit combo · Medium

    Second way to trigger Capcombo: shoot the Left Super Cue (Left Orbit lit via a flashing Orange hit) then immediately follow with the Right Orbit for 250K.

    Reward: 250K (Capcombo)

  • Nice Combo ("Breakshot") — Both Center Saucers on One Post Hit combo · Hard

    With two balls resting against the raised center post, a hard hit that propels both into the left and right side saucers on the same shot scores the game's namesake 250K combo.

    Reward: 250K + both sunk-ball awards (Nice Combo/"Breakshot")

  • Bank Shot Catch-and-Relaunch Loop combo · Hard

    The single highest-value repeatable sequence in the game: keep re-shooting and re-catching the Bank Shot from the upper-right flipper without ever letting the ball leave the top-right corner. Value escalates fast — 1M+ per lap is reachable by the 8th-10th rep.

    Reward: 100K + sunk ball on first make, escalating each consecutive catch (100K, 200K, 300K...; 1M+ by lap 8-10 reported)

  • Both Super Cues Back-to-Back combo · Medium

    Light and hit the Right Super Cue then the Left Super Cue (or vice versa) without a miss in between to light the Captive Ball for an Extra Ball — arguably the highest-leverage combo in the game since it buys more time for the Bank Shot loop.

    Reward: Lights the Captive Ball for an Extra Ball

  • Super Cue Consecutive Chain combo · Easy

    A lit Super Cue stays lit through drains and ball changes — keep re-hitting the same orbit uninterrupted for 60K, 120K, 180K... per consecutive make.

    Reward: 60K on the 1st consecutive make, escalating per make (60K, 120K, 180K...)

  • Ball-O-Rama (2-Ball Multiball) multiball · Medium

    Lock one ball in the Center Pockets, then cash it in at the 8-Ball Saucer to start a 2x-scoring 2-ball multiball with a climbing jackpot.

    Reward: 2-ball multiball, 2x scoring; jackpot starts 100K and climbs 10K/sec, never resets

  • Breakshot Frenzy (3-Ball Multiball) multiball · Hard

    Lock both side saucers, then capture a third ball in the center saucer to auto-start a 3x-scoring 3-ball multiball with an easy, resetting jackpot.

    Reward: 3-ball multiball, 3x scoring; jackpot starts 200K (+~5K erratic climb), 2 easy jackpots before relight needed

  • Breakshot Frenzy — Double Jackpot multiball · Hard

    After collecting a Frenzy jackpot the post rises to block re-entry; sneak a second ball in immediately after for a Double Jackpot bonus.

    Reward: A Double Jackpot on top of the base Frenzy jackpot

  • Eight Ball mode · Medium

    Sink all 7 assigned colors (solids or stripes, randomly assigned) via the rollover lanes and standup/drop targets, then finish at the 8-Ball Saucer for 100K.

    Reward: 100K + lights Call Your Shot for a shot at instantly finishing the next game too

  • Nine Ball mode · Medium

    Hit any of 5 designated shots 9 times total — Left Orbit, Captive Ball, Right Orbit, Green standup, Brown standup — for 200K, plus a bonus wheel at the Center Pockets entrance.

    Reward: 200K + a Center Pockets bonus wheel (Points/Special/Extra Ball)

  • Rotation mode · Hard

    The long game: sink all 15 balls using the same 7 shot-groups as Eight Ball, effectively cycling through them twice, for a 500K completion award.

    Reward: 500K completion award

  • Call Your Shot mode · Medium

    Lit briefly at the Left Inlane (and whenever the 8-ball is lit), Call Your Shot lets you pick one of three shots at the 8-Ball Saucer and instantly finish the current game if you hit it clean.

    Reward: Instantly finishes the current game for its full completion award (flat 1M if during Cutthroat Countdown)

  • Bonus Multiplier (Rollover Lanes) multiplier · Easy

    Hitting the Yellow/Blue/Red rollover-lane diamond inserts advances the end-of-ball bonus multiplier — reported as high as 13x in the wild.

    Reward: Advances the end-of-ball bonus multiplier (reported as high as 13x)

  • Bank Shot Bonus Multiplier (Left Super Cue Stack) multiplier · Hard

    Front-load Left Super Cue hits before cashing the Bank Shot — each one doubles a stacking Bank Shot Bonus, reported as high as 3.2M, then cash it all in on one big Bank Shot.

    Reward: Base Bank Shot value + a stacked bonus starting at 50K and doubling per Super Cue (reported as high as 3.2M)

  • Cutthroat Countdown (Wizard Mode) wizard · Hard

    Complete 3 regular games (default) to reach the game's wizard mode: a 60-second countdown where the machine calls balls one at a time for 1M each, ending in a per-ball Super Jackpot.

    Reward: 1M per called ball sunk (+10 sec each) + a Super Jackpot of 2M/ball at timer's end, scaling 2x then 3x on repeats

  • Perfect Cutthroat Countdown wizard · Hard

    Sink every called ball in a single Cutthroat Countdown round to trigger a special animation, auto-spot the entire next regular game as complete, and drop straight into another Cutthroat Countdown.

    Reward: Maxes the Super Jackpot + perfect-game animation + next game auto-spotted complete + straight into another Cutthroat Countdown

  • Beginner's First 30 Seconds strategy · Easy

    A low-risk opening sequence: safe skill shot, a few free loop reps, then easy standups and the forgiving captive ball before you ever aim at the risky center toy.

    Reward: 100K skill shot + free Bank Shot loop points + a free Nine Ball spot + Extra Ball progress, all low drain risk

  • Best Opener: Ride the Bank Shot, Then Bail strategy · Medium

    The competitive-player opener: treat the Bank Shot catch loop as a meter you deliberately cash out of once the value gets big, rather than a combo you run to failure.

    Reward: Escalating Bank Shot value banked before it resets — community high 8.5M on a single shot

  • Macro Strategy: Stack Frenzy Under Cutthroat Countdown strategy · Hard

    The top-end scoring line: get Breakshot Frenzy's 3x multiplier running, then time it to overlap with Cutthroat Countdown's called-ball scoring and Super Jackpot.

    Reward: Up to 9M per called ball during Cutthroat Countdown (1M x Frenzy's 3x) while 3x is still running

  • Captive Ball Extra Ball strategy · Medium

    The captive ball only counts toward the extra ball when you hit it hard enough to reach its internal target — soft hits just score the little rollover value. Four solid hits (factory default) lights the extra ball.

    Reward: Extra Ball after the required number of hard captive-ball hits (4 by default)

  • Arm the Left Outlane Kickback strategy · Easy

    The left outlane has a kicker coil that saves the ball, and it re-lights whenever you knock down either three-target drop bank in sequential order. Cheap insurance worth taking early on every ball.

    Reward: Lights the left-outlane kickback (a saved ball)
