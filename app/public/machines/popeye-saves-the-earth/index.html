Popeye Saves the Earth

Bally, 1994 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Popeye Saves the Earth in the Pinball Genie app →

Popeye Saves the Earth is a cluttered widebody with short flippers, a wide centre drain, and several shots you cannot see clearly from where you stand. But there is a clean route through it, and knowing that route is the difference between a two-minute ball and a real game.

Two playfields

Downstairs: P-O-P standups left and E-Y-E right, a Round House loop each side, a left ramp, an Animal sinkhole, Bluto's head in the middle with a jab target either side and the Lockjaw hole as his mouth, pop bumpers behind him, the O-Y-L rollovers above those, a Fight Bluto sinkhole, and the Escalator. SEA and HAG targets sit on opposite sides.

Upstairs splits in two: the Animals section, fed by the Animal sinkhole, and Kneedeep, a mini-playfield reachable only via the Escalator, holding the Swea' Pea gates, the Super Animal exit and the 2X Wheel. A rotating skill wheel on the right takes the plunge and empties into the right inlane.

Start at the wheel

The plunge lands on a disc carrying eight awards in fixed order: 100K, 10M, Light Lock, Spinach Can, OYL, Spot Animal, 2M and Spot Item. Light Lock is the one to want. Take it, shoot Lockjaw, and Bluto Multiball can be three shots from the start of a ball — the shortest route to points here. Many of these machines have a weak plunger and a stopper on the launch rail that bounces the ball back, so the shot is not always controllable.

Loops and the ramp

The Round House loops are the biggest repeatable score downstairs. A lit loop pays 2M and each consecutive lit loop doubles it — 2M, 4M, 8M, 16M, 32M. Consecutive is literal: hit any other switch in between and you are back to 2M. Only one inlane is lit for Light Loop at a time, and the lit side swaps every time you press a flipper, so check before you commit. Right inlane lights the left loop and vice versa.

The left ramp is the quiet workhorse. Its counter pays out every fourth shot — four lights a Spinach Can, eight lights the extra ball, twelve another Can, sixteen spots an animal. It also feeds the right inlane, which lights the left loop. When nothing better is lit, shoot the ramp.

About that extra ball: it lights in the left outlane, and the only way to collect it is to drain there. Once the light is on, stop nudging away from the left side.

Spinach Cans

Five shots carry a Spinach Can — the left ramp, both loops, the Animal sinkhole and the Fight Bluto sinkhole. A lit Can doubles the entire value of that shot, jackpots included, and stays lit for the rest of the ball or until you use it. Spelling POPEYE lights one, as do the ramp counter, the Escalator and the wheel. The first is always the left ramp — convenient, because a Can there doubles a jackpot.

Multiballs

  • Bluto Multiball — hit both jab targets to light Lockjaw, lock three. Jackpots are 30M times the jackpot number, collected on the left ramp and relit at Lockjaw, capped at 180M even doubled. Drain to one ball with nothing collected and you get about twenty seconds to restart at Lockjaw.
  • Sea Hag Multiball — collect four Items from the left ramp lit by the SEA and HAG targets, then the Animal sinkhole starts a four-ball instant multiball. Return each item at a different shot: a POPEYE target, an OYL rollover, a Swea' Pea gate and the 2X Wheel.
  • Mutant Spinach Multiball — five balls, after four Fight Bluto wins in one game.

Upstairs, and what to skip

On the Animals section, use the left flipper kicker to push the ball out of the left lane into the animal lane. Each animal is 5M and opens a short window to save it with a loop — and it drops you into the left inlane, which lights the right loop for exactly that save. Save all five and a hurry-up around 50M starts at the left ramp.

On Kneedeep, hit the two standups to light Super Animal, then take either Super Animal (a double-value animal) or the 2X Wheel exit, which collects the extra ball when lit and doubles whatever the wheel lands on next. Hunting Swea' Pea behind the three gates is only worth it if you are chasing the wizard mode — narrow gaps, heavy rubber, and almost no feedback when you find him.

Fights, and the end of the game

Completing O-Y-L adds a bonus multiplier and lights the Fight Bluto sinkhole. The four rounds have no clock — you win or he does. Earth Pavers is a wall race to fifteen blocks and every pop bumper hit smashes one, so feed the bumpers. Never Green Logging is a race to six trees, Blutonium Waste a race over six barrels and the toughest of the four, and Spill Oil Co. a tug of war. Lit loops count towards winning, and the timer runs on while the ball is out of play.

Rescue Olive is the wizard mode, and the checklist is brutal: Sea Hag Multiball, all five animals, Bluto Multiball, Swea' Pea and all four fights, in one game. Only then does the six-ball round start — and you still have to light all five Spinach Cans and shoot Lockjaw for the Super Jackpot.

How Popeye Saves the Earth plays

Popeye Saves the Earth is a cluttered Bally widebody with short flippers, where you battle Bluto's cartel across four fights and a stack of multiballs. Clear the whole checklist to reach Rescue Olive, the six-ball finale.

The loop

  1. Take Light Lock off the spinning skill wheel if the disc lands it, then shoot Lockjaw -- Bluto's mouth -- to put a multiball within three shots of the start.
  2. With nothing else lit, default to the left ramp: an award drops every fourth shot, the first Spinach Can sits here, and its return through the right inlane arms a loop for you.
  3. Only one inlane glows at a time and a flipper tap swaps which, and whichever Round House loop is lit is the one paying the doubling score.
  4. Spell POPEYE and rotate the O-Y-L rollovers to light Spinach Cans, bonus multipliers and the Fight Bluto sinkhole.
  5. Grind through the four fights, the animals, Sea Hag and Swea' Pea to assemble the Rescue Olive checklist.

Reading the lights

  • O-Y-L Rollovers (lanes) — lit: The three O-Y-L rollovers above the jets are a lane you steer; an unlit rollover is a letter you still owe, and the ball leaving the bumper nest upward runs straight through them. · then: Slide the lanes to finish OYL, which bumps the bonus multiplier a step and reopens the Fight Bluto sinkhole. Because you can rotate the lit lanes, most completions cost you nothing. · control: Either flipper button: either flipper press rotates the lit O-Y-L lanes one step to the right, so you can walk an unlit lane under the ball as it falls from the jets (as the ball drops through the rollovers)
  • Lit Inlane & Round House Loops (lanes) — lit: Exactly one inlane is lit for Light Loop at any moment, and the lit inlane arms the loop on the far side -- the right inlane lights the left loop, the left inlane lights the right. · then: Set the lit side, then run that loop: a lit loop pays two million and each consecutive one doubles it, climbing past thirty million until any stray switch drops the value back to the bottom. · control: Either flipper button: each flipper press swaps which inlane is lit, and therefore which Round House loop is armed for its doubling value (before the ball reaches the inlane)
  • P-O-P-E-Y-E Banks (letters) — lit: Two three-target banks spell the name, POP on the left and EYE on the right; a lit target is a letter you have not yet knocked down. · then: Sweep a bank in order for an extra million over a scrambled clear, then finish the whole name to light one of the five Spinach Cans. The Escalator can spot POP or EYE for you as well.
  • The Five Spinach Cans (status) — lit: Five shots can hold a Spinach Can -- both Round House loops, the left ramp, and the Animal and Fight Bluto sinkholes -- and a lit Can doubles everything that shot scores until you cash it or the ball ends. · then: Bank a Can and spend it on a big shot, ideally a Bluto Multiball jackpot on the ramp, though no jackpot tops a hundred and eighty million however you double it. Lighting all five is a hard gate for the game's final multiball.
  • Lockjaw Locks (status) — lit: Hitting both jab targets beside Bluto's head lights Lockjaw, his mouth, as a lock; the skill wheel and one Escalator award light it too. · then: Lock three balls to start Bluto Multiball, then alternate the left ramp for a jackpot and Lockjaw to relight it. Lockjaw spits the balls back down the center drain lane, so a ball saver covers you as the multiball begins.
  • Fight Bluto Cartel (mode) — lit: Completing O-Y-L lights the Fight Bluto sinkhole for one of four cartel rounds; there is no clock, so a round ends only when you beat Bluto or he beats you. · then: Start a fight and win it -- feed the jets to smash blocks in Earth Pavers, and take the lit loops that count toward the win -- and beating all four opens the five-ball Mutant Spinach Multiball.
  • Sea Hag Items (status) — lit: The SEA and HAG targets together light Collect Item at the left ramp; gathering four items arms the Animal sinkhole for an instant four-ball multiball. · then: Ramp in the four items -- a can opener, a bottle, some ketchup, a flower -- then start Sea Hag Multiball at the sinkhole and hand each one back to its rightful owner at a fresh shot for a heavy score.
  • Escalator & Kneedeep (mode) — lit: A lit Escalator hands you a scroll of awards to choose with the flippers, then carries the ball up to the Kneedeep mini-playfield. · then: Shoot it lit and lock your award before the timer expires, then work the upper flippers: strike both standups to open Super Animal, and favor the 2X Wheel exit, which collects the extra ball and doubles the skill wheel. The gates hide Swea' Pea for the wizard checklist.
  • Save the Animals (status) — lit: The Animal sinkhole feeds the upper Animals section, where the left flipper kicks the ball across to collect one of five creatures, each worth five million and each opening a short save window. · then: Take the save loop right off the drop-out -- a saved animal drops you back to the left inlane, and that arms the right loop, so the save is set up before you shoot -- and saves need not be consecutive. Rescue all five to start a fifty-million hurry-up at the ramp.

Lane change: the flipper buttons move the lit lane. Either flipper press rotates the lit O-Y-L rollovers one step to the right, so as the ball falls from the jets you can slide an unlit lane under it to finish OYL. Finishing OYL bumps the bonus multiplier by a step and reopens the Fight Bluto sinkhole; the same taps also swap which inlane is lit, arming the opposite Round House loop for its doubling value.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Grab Light Lock off the skill wheel if the disc lands it, then shoot Lockjaw to put Bluto Multiball three shots from the start. Otherwise lean on the left ramp, which feeds its own next shot and lights Spinach Cans, and spell POPEYE for a Can while the inlanes and O-Y-L rollovers reward your flipper taps. — The Skill Shot Wheel, Bluto Multiball, The Left Ramp Counter, Spell P-O-P-E-Y-E
  • Building — Turn the loops into real points by keeping a Round House chain clean for its doubling climb, bank Spinach Cans to double your big shots, and start Sea Hag Multiball off four collected items. Spot animals toward Stampede and take the 2X Wheel on Kneedeep for the extra ball. — Round House Loops, The Five Spinach Cans, Sea Hag Multiball, Stampede, What To Do On Kneedeep
  • The big one — Rescue Olive is the finale, gated behind a brutal one-game checklist: both the Bluto and Sea Hag multiballs, every animal saved, Swea' Pea found, and all four cartel fights won. Clear it and a six-ball multiball begins, but the Super Jackpot itself waits at Lockjaw, and only with all five Spinach Cans lit. — Rescue Olive

Does Popeye Saves the Earth have an action button?

Nothing extra to press — a 1994 Bally with a plunger, two flippers and a start button. The one place the machine asks you to choose something is the Escalator: when it is lit you pick your award with the flippers before it lifts the ball to the upper level. Worth knowing that the plunger itself is the weak point on many of these, with a stopper on the rail that bounces the ball straight back.

Goals in Popeye Saves the Earth

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • The Skill Shot Wheel skillshot · Medium

    The plunge drops the ball onto a rotating disc carrying eight awards: 100K, 10M, Light Lock, Spinach Can, OYL, Spot Animal, 2M and Spot Item. With a healthy plunger this is a learnable shot, and Light Lock is the one that shortens the whole game.

    Reward: One of eight awards, up to 10M

  • Round House Loops combo · Hard

    The best repeatable score on the lower playfield. A lit loop pays 2M, and each consecutive lit loop doubles that — 2M, 4M, 8M, 16M and at least 32M. Consecutive means exactly that: touch any other switch in between and the value drops back to 2M.

    Reward: 2M doubling per consecutive loop

  • Spell P-O-P-E-Y-E progression · Easy

    Three standups on each side. Completing either half pays 2M, or 3M if you got them in order. Completing the whole name lights one of the five Spinach Cans, which is where the real value lies.

    Reward: 2M or 3M per half, and a lit Spinach Can

  • The Five Spinach Cans multiplier · Medium

    Five shots on the playfield carry a Spinach Can: the left ramp, both Round House loops, the Animal sinkhole and the Fight Bluto sinkhole. A lit Can doubles the entire value of that shot, including a jackpot, and stays lit for the rest of the ball or until you use it.

    Reward: Double value on the lit shot

  • O-Y-L And The Bonus Multiplier multiplier · Easy

    Three rollovers above the bumpers. Spelling OYL adds one to the bonus multiplier and lights Fight Bluto, and the lit lanes rotate on every flipper press, so most completions cost nothing.

    Reward: +1 bonus multiplier, lights Fight Bluto

  • The Left Ramp Counter progression · Medium

    Every left ramp shot is counted all game and an award lands every fourth one. Four ramps lights a Spinach Can, eight lights the extra ball, twelve another Can, sixteen spots an animal, twenty another Can. Roughly every other award is a Can, which makes the ramp the most productive repeat shot here.

    Reward: An award every four ramps

  • The Outlane Extra Ball secret · Medium

    One of the strangest rules in pinball. Eight left ramp shots light the extra ball — in the left outlane. The only way to collect it is to drain there. Once you know it is coming, treat the left outlane as a kickback rather than a disaster.

    Reward: An extra ball, collected by draining

  • Save The Animals mode · Hard

    Get the ball to the Animals section and use the left flipper kicker to push it out of the left lane into the animal lane. Each animal you collect is worth 5M and starts a short window to save it with a loop shot. Save all five and a hurry-up starts around 50M.

    Reward: 5M per animal, roughly 50M for all five

  • What To Do On Kneedeep strategy · Medium

    The mini-playfield has three exits and only two of them are worth your time. Hit the two standups to light Super Animal, then take either Super Animal for a double-value animal or the 2X Wheel for an extra ball and a doubled wheel award.

    Reward: Double animal value, or a doubled wheel award

  • Choose Your Escalator Award mode · Medium

    A lit Escalator lets you scroll through awards with the flippers before it lifts you upstairs. There is a time limit and whatever is showing when it expires is what you get, so decide early rather than cycling the list.

    Reward: One chosen award

  • Bluto Multiball multiball · Medium

    Lock three balls in Lockjaw. Jackpots are worth 30M multiplied by the jackpot number, collected at the left ramp and relit at Lockjaw. A Spinach Can on the ramp doubles the value, though nothing exceeds 180M.

    Reward: 30M x jackpot number, up to 180M

  • Sea Hag Multiball multiball · Hard

    Collect four Items — a can opener, a baby bottle, ketchup and a flower — then the Animal sinkhole lights for an instant four-ball multiball. During it you return each item to its owner at a different shot for a large payoff.

    Reward: Around 50M for returning all four items

  • The Fight Bluto Cartel mode · Medium

    Four head-to-head rounds started at the Fight Bluto sinkhole once OYL lights it. There is no clock — either you beat him or he beats you — and the four differ only in animation and what the tug of war is over.

    Reward: Progress towards the five-ball multiball

  • Find Swea' Pea secret · Hard

    From Kneedeep, shoot the three gates to search behind three doors. The map on the display marks where he is hiding and the doors correspond to the gates. It is genuinely hard, and the game barely acknowledges it when you succeed.

    Reward: A large award, and a wizard mode requirement

  • Stampede mode · Hard

    The Geomap above the flippers holds the same five animals as the upper playfield. Spot all five there — the skill wheel and Escalator both do it — and Stampede starts, a frenzy round where every switch on the machine is worth 100K.

    Reward: 100K per switch

  • Mutant Spinach Multiball multiball · Hard

    Beat all four Fight Bluto rounds and a five-ball multiball follows. It is rarely seen because getting four wins in one game against a mode with no timer and short flippers is a long afternoon's work.

    Reward: A five-ball multiball

  • Rescue Olive wizard · Hard

    The end of the game and a genuinely brutal checklist. Complete Sea Hag Multiball, all five animals, Bluto Multiball, Find Swea' Pea and the four Fight Bluto rounds. Only then does a six-ball multiball start — and you still have to collect all five Spinach Cans and shoot Lockjaw for the Super Jackpot.

    Reward: Super Jackpot

  • Your Route Through The Game progression · Easy

    This is a cluttered widebody with short flippers and a wide centre drain, so the games that score are the ones with a short route. Multiball is three shots away if the wheel cooperates, and the left ramp quietly does everything else.

    Reward: A repeatable route through the game
