Star Trek: The Next Generation

Williams, 1993 — shot-by-shot rules and strategy guide.

Open Star Trek: The Next Generation in the Pinball Genie app →

Star Trek: The Next Generation throws more at you than almost any machine of its era, but nearly all of it funnels through one system: warp factor. Advance it with the spinner and left ramp and you unlock a new toy at every step — bumper values, held multipliers, a countdown hurry-up, double spinner scoring, and a frantic bonus round at warp 9. Chase warp first and the rest — missions, multiball, ranks, artifacts — starts connecting.

The Picard Maneuver

If you learn one shot on this machine, make it this one. Rip the spinner shot on the left orbit, then follow through immediately to the left ramp before the ball settles — that's the Picard Maneuver, worth building real muscle memory around. It advances warp faster than either shot alone, awards a triple jackpot during multiball, and turns Q's Challenge into the highest-scoring mode in the game when you chain it over and over.

Warp Factor And Factor Nine

Every ball starts at Warp 1. Warp 2 is a throwaway 5M, but Warp 3 sets the bumpers to a million a pop, Warp 4 lights the spinner for real scoring, Warp 5 holds your bonus multiplier, Warp 6 unlocks Hurry Up and Super Spinner, Warp 7 doubles the spinner outright, and Warp 8 pays an extra ball the first time you see it. Reach Warp 9 and the game drops you into Factor Nine Mode — the spinner and left ramp push you through 9.1 onward to 9.9 in tenths, each paying more, on a clock that never pauses. A fresh ball resets you to Warp 1, and the left ramp no longer counts toward warp — only the spinner still does.

Seven Missions To The Final Frontier

Start Mission launches whatever's flashing; Command Decision, the small hole by the left ramp, lets you cherry-pick instead. Clear all seven and the eighth, Final Frontier, lights as the actual finish line of the game. Each plays completely differently:

  • Time Rift — every shot pays 10M on a 20-second clock; the two red targets buy more time and, for the first three hits, more money too.
  • Worm Hole — build value on the right ramp and spinner, then cash out on the left ramp before time runs out.
  • Search the Galaxy — hit the three quadrant ramps in the order called out, then finish at the Neutral Zone for the mission's biggest payout.
  • Battle Simulation — load a cannon at any hole and fire when ready; each successful shot climbs a level worth ten times its number in millions.
  • Q's Challenge — no round timer, just a short fuse on each lit shot. This is where the Picard Maneuver turns into a scoring monster.
  • Rescue — thirty seconds to load and save fifty people, using anything that closes a switch to fill the shuttle.
  • Asteroid Threat — freeze a countdown at Start Mission, then clean up all seven shots in a fresh thirty-second sprint.

Nearly every mission can hand you an Artifact too — hit enough of a specific shot and the game tells you. Collect full sets of all four types and Final Frontier pays in the billions: roughly 100M per Artifact just for starting, then every shot scales with how many you're carrying. Show up with none and each shot is a flat 10M instead, so stockpile a few first.

Three Ways The Neutral Zone Turns Ugly

Hit the Neutral Zone's three targets to arm it, then hit it again to pick your poison. Ferengi is the friendliest — it stacks up to four balls in play with jackpots at Start Mission worth 10M times however many balls are live. Cardassian is tighter: a 2-ball fight where the jackpot falls as your shields do, so rebuilding shields on the spinner matters as much as the jackpot shot itself. Romulan isn't a multiball at all — it's a race to cloak three ships, one each on the spinner, left ramp and right ramp, before their timers expire and relight them. None of the three are required for anything else, but Ferengi is worth grabbing just to pad time on the Warp and Time Rift shots you're already chasing.

Multiball, Holodeck, And The Rest

The real multiball is the Enterprise against the Borg — lock three balls through the right orbit and left ramp, work the loaded cannon into Start Mission before the third lock for a fatter base jackpot, then chase single, double and triple jackpots while working the spinner to hold your shields up. Let shields hit zero and the jackpot goes dark until raised again. Separately, Holodeck lights from repeated right ramp shots and offers a choice: a flat 25M, or Shuttle Simulation, a hard video mode steered on the flippers with an Artifact and an extra ball hidden in its later caverns. Watch for Advance Rank too — clearing the lanes above the bumpers lights it at Command Decision, and once you're Captain, a lit Advance Rank pays a flat 100M on the spot instead of waiting for bonus.

One more thing worth knowing: when Holodeck reads "Ready" for the third time, pulling the trigger instead of shooting drops you into a hidden Secret Mission, a short frenzy where every switch pays out. It won't win the game on its own, but it's a good reminder of how much this ruleset has buried in it.

How Star Trek: The Next Generation plays

Play the Enterprise through seven missions -- each one banking Artifacts worth real points -- to light the Final Frontier wizard. Climb warp factors on the spinner and left ramp and fight the Borg, Ferengi, Cardassians, and Romulans along the way.

The loop

  1. Start each ball by cycling the plunge to the choice you want -- a flipper tap speeds it -- then pull the gun trigger to launch.
  2. Rip the spinner and follow immediately with the left ramp, the Picard Maneuver, to climb warp factors and unlock a new award at each step.
  3. Pick a mission at Command Decision or start the flashing one at Start Mission, clear it, and pocket the Artifact it hands you.
  4. Load a swing-out cannon at a hole and fire it with the trigger to feed Start Mission and the Neutral Zone.
  5. Clear all seven missions to light Final Frontier, which pays out by your stockpiled Artifact count.

Reading the lights

  • Plunger Choice (status) — lit: Five choices cycle in the display before launch -- Start Mission, a Flipper Skill Shot, Launch Probe, a Light Lock, or Warp Factor 4 -- and only the one showing is what you get. · then: Take a lit Q's Challenge shot if one shows, since it is the game's richest single mode, and if not, Warp Factor 4 makes a reliable pick that turns on genuine spinner scoring early without burning a mission attempt. · control: Either flipper button: the choices scroll on their own, but tapping a flipper button runs them past faster so you can pull the gun trigger on the one you want (before you fire the launch)
  • Warp Factors (ladder) — lit: The spinner and the left ramp both climb warp from 1 to 9, and each factor turns on something new -- 5M, richer bumpers, live spinner payouts, a locked multiplier, then Hurry Up and Super Spinner at warp 6, a doubled spinner at warp 7, and an extra ball at warp 8. · then: Run the Picard Maneuver -- a hard spinner shot followed straight into the left ramp -- to climb fastest, since the ramp opens up right after a hard spinner rip. Reaching warp 9 launches Factor Nine mode, and afterward the left ramp drops out of warp scoring for the rest of the game, leaving the spinner as the only way up.
  • Mission Select (mode) — lit: Start Mission launches whatever mission is flashing, while the Command Decision sinkhole beside the left ramp instead lets you hand-pick which of the open missions starts next. · then: Finish every one of the seven missions and the eighth, Final Frontier, lights, and nearly every mission also drops an Artifact that scores 50M by itself and a great deal more at the end. You can re-collect the same Artifact, since the wizard pays a bonus for each full set of all four Artifact types in your stockpile.
  • Neutral Zone (mode) — lit: Hitting the three Neutral Zone targets sets them flashing, and hitting it again chooses one of the modes waiting there. · flashing: All three Neutral Zone lights flash once the targets are hit, meaning it is armed to select a mode. · then: Choose Ferengi to add up to four balls with jackpots at Start Mission, or Cardassian for a two-ball fight where the 50M jackpot drops as the Borg lower your shields. Rebuild shields on the spinner before committing to a Cardassian jackpot shot.
  • Swing-Out Cannons (status) — lit: A ball that lands in either slingshot cannon loads it automatically, and the mission clock stops dead the whole time a cannon sits loaded. · then: In Battle Simulation each cannon hit climbs a level worth ten million times its number, up to 100M and often an extra ball at level six. At the start of Borg Multiball, three trigger shots at Start Mission build up the base jackpot and score single, then double, then triple along the way. · control: Action button: the gun-trigger pistol grip fires the loaded cannon at Start Mission or the Neutral Zone, so you can take your time lining up the shot, but sit on a loaded cannon too long and the game launches it on its own and you lose the aim (whenever a cannon is loaded)
  • Enterprise vs. Borg Locks (shot arrows) — lit: Lock three balls at the right orbit and the left ramp, since either shot works toward the same locks, but the third lock has to come from the right orbit. · then: Chase jackpots at Start Mission as they roll from single to double to triple, and take the triple on the left ramp, which then kicks the ball back at you out of the Borg ship. Keep the shields up with a hard spinner shot, because if they hit zero the jackpot switches off until you build them back up.
  • 2x Shuttle Targets (multiplier) — lit: Clipping one red target beside the right ramp entrance doubles the next ramp shot, and clipping both quadruples it, for about a ten-second window. · then: Take the right ramp inside the window for the doubled or quadrupled value, and note the right ramp also feeds extra balls, lights Command Decision and Holodeck, and shares the Explosive Millions ramp ladder with the center ramp.
  • Bonus Multiplier Lanes (lanes) — lit: The three lanes above the bumpers build the end-of-ball bonus multiplier one full pass at a time, and a lit kickback target can spot one lane on its own. · then: Walk the multiplier up through 2x, 4x, 8x and on to 10x, and reaching 10x also lights an extra ball, after which each further pass pays a flat, climbing 10M instead. Completing the lanes also lights Advance Rank at Command Decision.
  • Q Target (status) — lit: The single Q standup below the bumpers advances whichever mode is currently lit, and adds a fresh lit shot during Q's Challenge. · then: During Q's Challenge, hit Q to pile on more lit shots -- each expires on its own short fuse -- and chain the Picard Maneuver through them, where shots climb from 10M to a 100M cap for the biggest single-mode scores on the machine.

Lane change: not verified for this machine yet.

Start scoring

  • Ball one — Cycle the plunge to Warp Factor 4 for early spinner money, or take a lit Q's Challenge shot if one shows -- the most lucrative mode on the machine. Then work the Picard Maneuver to climb warp and keep the kickback lit off the yellow side targets. — Plunger Choice, The Picard Maneuver, Climbing Warp Factors
  • Building — Pick missions at Command Decision and clear them for Artifacts, and load and fire the cannons in Battle Simulation for up to 100M a level. Lock three balls for the Enterprise vs. Borg multiball, building its base jackpot with the loaded cannon first. — Missions & Artifacts, Battle Simulation, Multiball: Enterprise vs. Borg
  • The big one — Clear all seven missions to light Final Frontier, a six-ball multiball that pays roughly 100M per Artifact just to start and about 25M times your Artifact count on every lit shot. Carrying one of every Artifact type at once is worth a billion by itself, and extra full sets stack on top, so bank a few Artifacts first. — Final Frontier, Missions & Artifacts

The Gun Trigger on Star Trek: The Next Generation

A pistol grip on the front right of the cabinet, in the shooter-rod position.

Instead of a rod you get a gun handle with a trigger. Pulling it launches your ball, and once either swing-out cannon on the slingshots has swallowed a ball, that same trigger is what fires it back into the playfield. Learning when to squeeze it is a real part of playing this game well.

  • Starting a ball: Tap the flippers to cycle the five plunge choices, then pull the trigger to launch on the one you want.
  • When a cannon loads in Battle Simulation: Fires at Start Mission or the Neutral Zone; each level cleared pays ten million times its number, up to a hundred million.
  • At the start of Borg Multiball: Fires the loaded left cannon at Start Mission — three straight hits build the base jackpot and pay single, double, then triple.
  • Choosing a Holodeck award: Keep the trigger squeezed and tap right flipper for a hidden poker hand against Riker.
  • The third time Holodeck reads Ready: Pull the trigger three times instead of taking the shot to fall into a hidden Secret Mission where every switch pays.
  • When a cannon sits loaded too long: The machine announces itself and fires for you, so a slow decision costs you the aim.

Goals in Star Trek: The Next Generation

Every goal below is tracked step-by-step, with shot paths drawn on the playfield map, inside the app.

  • Plunger Choice skillshot · Medium

    Five choices cycle in the display before you launch — pull the trigger the instant your pick is lit, or tap the flippers to cycle through them faster.

    Reward: Whatever the lit choice pays

  • The Picard Maneuver combo · Medium

    The signature shot of the game: rip the spinner shot hard, then follow it straight through to the left ramp before the ball settles. It builds warp factors, awards a triple jackpot in multiball, and feeds Q's Challenge better than anything else on the table.

    Reward: Warp progress, triple jackpots, and huge Q's Challenge scores

  • Climbing Warp Factors progression · Easy

    Warp climbs from 1 to 9 off the spinner and the left ramp, and every step unlocks something new — bumper values, the spinner bonus, held multipliers, Hurry Up, double spinner, and an artifact at 8.

    Reward: A new award at every factor

  • Factor Nine Mode mode · Hard

    Reaching warp 9 launches its own round: keep feeding the spinner and left ramp to climb from 9.1 to 9.9 in tenths, each one paying more than the last.

    Reward: 20M climbing to 60M per tenth

  • Missions & Artifacts progression · Easy

    Seven missions lead to an eighth, Final Frontier — and nearly every one of them can hand you an Artifact worth real points on its own and a lot more once you reach the end.

    Reward: Progress toward Final Frontier, plus Artifacts

  • Time Rift mode · Medium

    Every shot on the table pays 10M with a 20-second clock running — and the two red targets in the middle are the only thing standing between you and running out of time.

    Reward: 10M per shot, extended by two red targets

  • Worm Hole mode · Easy

    A straightforward build-and-bank mission: pile value onto the right ramp and spinner, then cash it all in on the left ramp before the 20-second clock runs out.

    Reward: 30M+, built by the ramp and spinner

  • Search The Galaxy mode · Medium

    Plot a course through all three quadrants in order — center ramp, then right ramp, then left ramp — and the Neutral Zone opens up for the biggest single shot in the mission.

    Reward: 10M to 50M per leg, two Artifacts possible

  • Battle Simulation mode · Medium

    Load either cannon at any of the four holes, then fire when ready — each hit climbs a level worth ten times its number in millions, capping at a big payout and often an extra ball on level six.

    Reward: 10M to 100M per level

  • Q's Challenge mode · Medium

    Q keeps adding lit shots faster than you can clear them. There's no round timer, only a short fuse on each individual shot — and the Picard Maneuver run through here produces some of the biggest scores in the whole game.

    Reward: 10M climbing to a 100M cap per shot

  • Rescue mode · Easy

    Thirty seconds to save fifty people: fill the shuttle by hitting anything, then empty it on either ramp.

    Reward: 5M to start, up to 50M for a full rescue

  • Asteroid Threat mode · Medium

    A countdown you can freeze on command, followed by a frantic seven-shot clean-up once it gets low enough.

    Reward: Up to 20M per shot, 50M bonus for clearing all seven

  • Final Frontier wizard · Hard

    The actual finish line. Every mission cleared lights it, and it pays out based on every Artifact you've been stockpiling the whole game.

    Reward: 100M+ per Artifact, plus a shot value that scales with them

  • Multiball: Enterprise vs. Borg multiball · Medium

    Lock three balls to send the Enterprise into battle with the Borg — build the jackpot with the cannon first, then chase it through Start Mission and the left ramp while keeping the shields up.

    Reward: 20M+ jackpots, triple on the left ramp

  • Ferengi multiball · Medium

    Light the Neutral Zone three times, choose Ferengi, and keep it lit to add up to four more balls into play.

    Reward: 10M per ball in play, plus 2M per yellow target

  • Cardassian multiball · Medium

    A tense 2-ball fight where the jackpot itself is under fire — keep the spinner working or watch the payout crumble along with your shields.

    Reward: 5M to 50M depending on shield strength

  • Romulan mode · Hard

    Cloak all three Romulan ships — spinner, center ramp, right ramp — before their timers run out, and use Klingon Assistance to take a stubborn one off the board entirely.

    Reward: 10M per ship, 30M for cloaking all three

  • Hurry Up mode · Easy

    A classic countdown hurry-up, unlocked at warp 6 and started from the left return lane — the sooner you close it out at the right orbit, the more it's worth.

    Reward: 50M counting down to a 10M floor

  • Holodeck mode · Easy

    Right ramp shots light this one, and the choice at the right orbit is either a flat payout or a shot at a genuinely tough video mode with real prizes buried in it.

    Reward: 25M flat, or Shuttle Simulation's rewards

  • Advance Rank progression · Medium

    Complete the multiplier lanes to light Advance Rank at Command Decision — five ranks climbing from Ensign to Captain, each worth more at the bonus count, with Captain paying out immediately instead.

    Reward: 5M to 25M at bonus, 100M instant at Captain

  • Super Spinner multiplier · Medium

    Light it from the right inlane at warp 6 and the left orbit spinner turns into flat 10M-a-spin money for a short window.

    Reward: 10M per spin

  • Bonus Multiplier Lanes multiplier · Easy

    The three lanes above the bumpers build your end-of-ball multiplier from 2x up to a 10x cap — after that, every additional pass just pays straight millions instead.

    Reward: Multiplier up to 10x, then climbing millions

  • Explosive Millions combo · Easy

    Center and right ramp shots share one climbing ladder — land one and both light for the next step, so you can lean on whichever ramp you trust.

    Reward: 5M climbing to an uncapped 50M

  • Secret Mission secret · Hard

    A well-hidden alternate to the normal Holodeck choice — skip both options and get a frenzy instead.

    Reward: 20 seconds of frenzy scoring
